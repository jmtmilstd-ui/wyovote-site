New leaders chosen for the 69th Legislature as Governor-elect Barlow builds his team

News

By WyoVote

Week of Nov 16, 2026

Updated Nov 19, 2026

Caucuses picked Nethercott and Larsen on Nov. 14. The Legislature listed standing-committee chairs and members on Nov. 18. Barlow named senior staff on Nov. 10. Inauguration is Jan. 4. The session opens Jan. 12.

CHEYENNE — Lawmakers have picked the people who will lead the Wyoming Legislature in 2027. The Legislative Service Office listed standing-committee chairs and members on Nov. 18. Governor-elect Eric Barlow is naming the people who will help him run the governor's office. Both groups take over in January.

Senate leaders

Senate Republicans met in Casper on Nov. 14, 2026.1, 2 They chose Sen. Tara Nethercott, R-Cheyenne, for Senate President. She ran unopposed.2 Nethercott has held her Senate seat since 2017. She served as majority floor leader for the past two sessions.1 Sen. Ed Cooper, R-Ten Sleep, was chosen for vice president over Sen. Cale Case, R-Lander.2 Sen. Wendy Schuler, R-Evanston, ran unopposed for majority floor leader.2

House leaders

House Republicans chose Rep. Lloyd Larsen, R-Lander, for Speaker. He defeated Rep. Art Washut, R-Casper.2 Larsen has held his House seat since 2013.1 Rep. Andrew Byron, R-Jackson, was chosen for speaker pro tempore over Rep. Clarence Styvar, R-Cheyenne.2 Rep. JD Williams, R-Lusk, defeated Rep. Jeremy Haroldson, R-Wheatland, for majority floor leader.2 Rep. Cody Wylie, R-Rock Springs, defeated Rep. Martha Lawley, R-Worland, for majority whip.2

Minority leaders

Democrats kept their leaders from the last session.1, 2 In the House, Rep. Mike Yin, D-Jackson, remains minority floor leader. Rep. Karlee Provenza, D-Laramie, remains minority whip. Rep. Trey Sherwood, D-Laramie, remains minority caucus chair.2 In the Senate, Sen. Mike Gierau, D-Jackson, remains minority floor leader. Sen. Chris Rothfuss, D-Laramie, remains minority whip.1, 2

The Legislature has 93 members: 62 in the House and 31 in the Senate.1 In January, Democrats will hold two Senate seats and seven House seats.1, 2

What the leaders do

The Senate President and House Speaker make committee assignments, send bills to committees, and run the Management Council.1 The full Senate and House must still vote on the president, vice president, speaker and speaker pro tem.1 Those votes are expected when the Legislature convenes on Jan. 12, 2027.2, 7

Standing committee assignments

The Legislative Service Office published standing-committee chairs and members for the 2027-2028 biennium on Nov. 18, 2026, after the Nov. 14 caucuses.10 Cowboy State Daily and the Cheyenne Gazette also reported the chair appointments.11, 12 The Senate president and House speaker, who make the assignments, are still formally confirmed when the Legislature convenes on Jan. 12, 2027.7, 10 The tables below follow the Nov. 18 release. Assignments for select committees, task forces and miscellaneous committees will be finalized in March, after the session.10

The governor-elect's team

The Associated Press called the governor's race for Eric Barlow on Nov. 3, 2026.3 He takes office on Jan. 4, 2027.3, 6 On Nov. 4, he named Eydie Trautwein, a former deputy state auditor, and Jennifer Thomson, his campaign manager, as joint leads of his transition team.4 On Nov. 10, he named Erin Taylor as chief of staff, Walter Eggers as his nominee for attorney general, and Trautwein as chief operating officer.5 The attorney general appointment is subject to Wyoming Senate confirmation.5 The transition website, governor-elect.wyo.gov, takes resumes from people who want to serve.4
