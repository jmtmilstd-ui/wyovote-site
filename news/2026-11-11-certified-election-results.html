Certified election results set the calendar for the 2027 Legislature

  1. Home
  3. News
  5. Certified election results set the calendar for the 2027 Legislature

Special

By WyoVote

Nov. 11, 2026

The board certified the Nov. 3 results on Nov. 10. Officers take office Jan. 4, 2027. The Legislature convenes Jan. 12.

CHEYENNE — The State Canvassing Board met on Nov. 10 and voted to approve the official results of the Nov. 3 general election.6 The secretary of state chairs the board. The other members are the governor, the treasurer, and the auditor.6 The board approved the State Abstract Certification.6

The Secretary of State’s official canvass shows 191,713 ballots were cast statewide.2 According to the election division, as reported by Wyoming Public Media, no statewide or legislative candidate had asked for a recount at the time of the Nov. 10 meeting.6

From Canvass to Opening Day

Elected state and county officers assume office on Jan. 4, 2027, under the Wyoming Constitution and state election law.5 The 69th Legislature then convenes for the 2027 General Session at 2 p.m. on Jan. 12 in the House and Senate chambers.7, 9 A joint session is scheduled for noon on Jan. 13 in the House chamber.7 In odd-numbered years, the General Session is limited to 40 legislative days.8

Proposition 1

Voters also decided a statewide initiated measure, Proposition 1, on property taxation.3 Official results were 80,047 votes for and 105,209 votes against, with 6,247 undervotes and 210 overvotes.3 Under Wyoming law, an initiated measure must receive a majority of all ballots cast in the election in order to pass.3 Proposition 1 did not meet that threshold.3

House District 11

In House District 11 in Laramie County, official results were Britney Tennant (D), 1,080 votes; Dan McIntosh (R), 1,027 votes; and Ryan Shollenberger (L), 152 votes.4

Official results for all statewide offices, legislative districts, judicial retentions, and ballot propositions are posted by the Secretary of State.1 Agendas, livestreams, and bill drafts for the remaining interim meetings and the January session are available at wyoleg.gov.7
