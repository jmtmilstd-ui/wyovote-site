Gaps in the data

Copied word for word from the source notes. Nothing here is filled in or estimated.

  • Joseph B. Meyer’s death and Mark Gordon’s treasurer swearing-in. The official State Officers Roster says only that Meyer died in October 2012 and that Gordon was appointed that month. The file uses October 6, 2012 (death) and November 1, 2012 (sworn), with the appointment announced October 26, 2012, from contemporary public reports (Wyoming Tribune Eagle; Schrader funeral home obituary; Wyoming Public Media; Associated Press / K2 Radio). Those days are not on the official SOS roster.
  • Short vacancies with no acting official on the official rosters: Treasurer Oct. 7–31, 2012; Secretary of State Sept. 16–Oct. 2, 2022; Superintendent Jan. 17–21, 2022. No acting holder was added. For Secretary of State after Murray’s resignation, the official Secretaries Roster does list Karen L. Wheeler as Acting, Feb. 10–Mar. 5, 2018.
  • Karl Allred’s start date. The State Officers Roster gives the appointment as Sept. 29, 2022; the Secretaries Roster gives the term of office as Oct. 3, 2022 to Jan. 2023. term_start is Oct. 3, 2022; appointment_date is Sept. 29, 2022.
  • Cindy Hill / 2013 SF0104. Hill remained the elected Superintendent. The governor-appointed Director of the Department of Education (interim Jim Rose, then Rich Crandall) was a different office, later held unconstitutional, and is not listed as Superintendent.
  • Legislative service before 2008. WyoVote rosters start in 2008. Curt Meier’s pre-2008 Senate service is recorded from the official Treasurer site (24 years in the Senate before Jan. 7, 2019, i.e. 1995–2018). Edward Buchanan (Speaker 2011) and Steve Harshman (Speaker 2017 and 2019) may have served in the Legislature before 2008; that earlier service was not confirmed in the official sources collected here and is left in the note rather than as years. No 2008–2026 roster service was found for Robert Short or the other non-legislator officials.
  • 2006 write-ins, overvotes, and undervotes are blank. The official 2006 statewide candidate PDFs do not report those columns the way later workbooks do.
  • 2011, 2012, and 2014 have no veto lastAction and no veto/override roll-call rows in the WyoVote extracts. That may mean there were no vetoes those years; it is only as complete as those extracts. billActions in the bill JSON files is empty, so vetoes rest on lastAction plus roll calls. 2016 SF0001 (line-item) appears in the roll calls but not in lastAction.
  • 2023 SF0131 (Veto Message Received only) is left as type unspecified; no override roll call was found. 2023 SF0071 has a Senate override (30-1-0-0-0) and no House override vote in the roll-call file; the bill-level outcome is marked incomplete.
  • 2026 elects are included because the official 2026 results are in, but they have not taken office as of 2026-11-16. See elect_not_yet_sworn=yes.
