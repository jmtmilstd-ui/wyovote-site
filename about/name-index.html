About Name Index

What's there

The Name Index helps you find a person by name. It has a search box and a set of PDF lists.

The search box lets you choose a year and type at least three letters of a last name. The PDF lists are names only, sorted Last, First, for one year. You can pick all counties, one county, or one committee. The search needs scripts turned on. The PDF lists work without them.

How to use it

  1. Open the Name Index tab at the top of any page.
  2. To search, choose a year and type at least three letters of the last name. Matching names appear below the box.
  3. To download a list, use PDF lists. Pick a year, then All counties, one county, or one committee. Each button opens a PDF of names only.
  4. Once you have a name, you can also open that person from the Officials tab (current office holders and Profiles) or from Candidates (the latest races, by county).

More detail on finding people, including by county, is in the User's Guide.
