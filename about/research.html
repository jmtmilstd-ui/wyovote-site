About Research

What's there

The Research tab is for WyoVote research studies. It is coming soon. Until studies are posted on this tab, the first study is in the Archive.

How to use it

  1. Open the Research tab at the top of any page to see current studies, when they are posted.
  2. Until then, open the Archive tab and choose Research. You can also look under Articles for past pieces, newest first.
  3. Each line in the Archive opens the current file for that item.

The Archive and Research tabs are also described in the User's Guide.
