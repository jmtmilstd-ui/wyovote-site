About Candidates

What's there

The Candidates tab shows the latest races by county. Three buttons at the top choose what you see:

  • Primary — partisan races in the statewide primary. Choose a county.
  • General — federal, statewide, and legislative races in the November general election. Choose a county.
  • Local — county offices (and, later, towns). Choose a county. Earlier Local years are in the Archive.

Races are grouped by office. A short “How to use this page” note sits at the top. Districts that cross county lines appear on every county they cover. Each name opens that candidate's own page, with the office, the party, and the result: Won, Lost, Unopposed, or Withdrawn. When a short biography is available, it appears on the same page. Campaign website or campaign email, when published, is marked as a campaign contact.

On a Local page, each line shows the name, party, vote total, and Won or Lost. A winner's whole line is in bold. Results may be marked unofficial until the canvass is complete.

How to use it

  1. Open the Candidates tab at the top of any page.
  2. Choose Primary, General, or Local.
  3. Click a county to see the races for that county.
  4. Click a name to open that candidate's page.
  5. For earlier election years, open the Archive tab, choose Candidates, and use the same three buttons.

More detail, including how Local and precinct committee lines are written, is in the User's Guide.
