About Counties

What's there

The Counties tab lists each county, plus a combined “All Legislative Counties” report. Each name opens the rating report for the legislators who represent that county. The report shows their ranks and color labels, and the county's overall rank. Some legislators represent more than one county, so a name may appear in more than one county report.

Earlier years are in the Archive, from a link at the top of the Counties page, or from Archive → Counties.

How to use it

  1. Open the Counties tab at the top of any page.
  2. Click your county, or click All Legislative Counties for the combined report. The report opens as a PDF (Legal paper).
  3. Read the ranks and color labels. Ranked lists are sorted from MOST Conservative to Most Liberal. The legend is: Rank: 1 = MOST Conservative.
  4. To print, open the PDF first, then use the PDF reader's Print command. If your printer uses Letter paper, choose “Fit” or “Shrink to printable area” so nothing is cut off.
  5. For earlier sessions, use the Archive link at the top of the page, or open the Archive tab and choose Counties.

You can also look someone up by name on the Name Index. More detail is in the User's Guide.
