About Committees

What's there

The Committees tab lists each legislative committee for the current session, plus a combined “All Committees” report. Each name opens the rating report for that committee. The report shows the committee's members, their ranks and color labels, and the committee's overall rank.

Earlier years are in the Archive, from a link at the top of the Committees page, or from Archive → Committees.

How to use it

  1. Open the Committees tab at the top of any page.
  2. Click All Committees for the combined report, or click a committee name for that committee alone. The report opens as a PDF (Legal paper).
  3. Read the ranks and color labels. Ranked lists are sorted from MOST Conservative to Most Liberal. The legend is: Rank: 1 = MOST Conservative. Color labels group legislators who voted most alike: Conservative, Moderate, or Liberal. A legislator with too few counted votes in that session is shown as Not ranked.
  4. To print, open the PDF first, then use the PDF reader's Print command. If your printer uses Letter paper, choose “Fit” or “Shrink to printable area” so nothing is cut off.
  5. For earlier sessions, use the Archive link at the top of the page, or open the Archive tab and choose Committees.

A short explanation of ranks and labels is also on How the Ratings Work, and in the User's Guide.
