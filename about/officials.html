About Officials

What's there

The Officials tab lists current office holders. Buttons at the top choose what you see:

  • State — statewide elected officers. These buttons open the official state website. They have a gold ring, and a legend above them explains it. A Statewide Profiles link under these five offices opens Profiles for the people who have held them.
  • House and Senate — each name opens that legislator's WyoVote page.
  • General links — official state sites, including the Legislature's own “Find Your Legislator” lookup, which leaves WyoVote.

A legislator's page shows the office, district, and party, and the rank and color label for the session. The legend reads: Rank: 1 = MOST Conservative. The band is also labeled this way: rank 1 = MOST Conservative, last = Most Liberal. A Profile → button under the name opens a closer look: a rank chart, band history, issue areas, missed votes, and committee service. Each legislator in the current window has a Profile. Ranks and bands on the Profile are copied from the published WyoVote rating reports. Nothing there is estimated.

When official contact information is available, it appears in a Contact block under the name, each with an “as of” date. Personal addresses, personal cell numbers, and personal social media are not collected and are not shown. Earlier session rosters are in the Archive.

How to use it

  1. Open the Officials tab at the top of any page.
  2. Choose State, House, Senate, or General links.
  3. For a legislator, click the name. Then click Profile → for the rank chart and the rest of the history. On the chart, the vertical axis is Rank (% of list), with 1 = MOST Conservative at the top. A dashed brick-red arrow is the trend.
  4. For the five statewide offices, use the Statewide Profiles link under State.
  5. To look someone up by name, use the Name Index tab. For earlier rosters, open the Archive tab and choose Officials.

A fuller description of Profiles and of official contacts is in the User's Guide.
