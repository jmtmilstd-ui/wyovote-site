About Archive

What's there

The Archive keeps earlier years and past articles. Choose a section — Articles, Reports, Committees, Counties, Candidates, Officials, or Research — and the list is newest first. Each line opens the current file for that item. If you have an older link, it still works and takes you to the current file.

The Candidates section of the Archive has Primary, General, and Local pages for earlier election years, in the same layout as the current Candidates tab. Officials lists earlier session rosters. Reports holds the Rank and Alpha sheets and, for years that have them, the full reports.

How to use it

  1. Open the Archive tab at the top of any page, or the Archive link on the Home page.
  2. Choose a section. The list is newest first.
  3. For rating reports, open Reports. The top pair in each year is the two-page Rank and Alpha sheet (Letter paper). The lower pair, when it is there, is the full report (Legal paper). Rank lists legislators by rating, sorted from MOST Conservative to Most Liberal. Alpha lists the same people by name. The legend is: Rank: 1 = MOST Conservative.
  4. Open a report as a PDF, then use the PDF reader's Print command. If your printer uses Letter paper for a Legal-size report, choose “Fit” or “Shrink to printable area” so nothing is cut off.
  5. For earlier elections, open Candidates in the Archive, then Primary, General, or Local, and choose a county. For earlier office holders, open Officials and pick a session roster.

A longer walk-through of the Archive and of printing is in the User's Guide.
