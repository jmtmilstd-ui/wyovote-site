County Offices

Park County has five commissioners (three elected in midterm years, two in presidential years). The official March 30, 2026 roster lists Terry Call as assessor; the official 2022 general winner was Pat Meyer. The mid-term date is not in official general results. Barbara A. Poley is the sitting treasurer and the official 2026 commissioner-elect (succeeding Dossie Overfield); Anne Lawler succeeds as treasurer.
