Research

OccupationRetired Air National Guard master sergeant; former information-technology specialist
Background

Born in Cheyenne, she also worked in banking and for the Archdiocese of Denver and has volunteer history including firefighter/EMT work, Habitat for Humanity, and military-family organizations.

She served one House term from 2013 to 2015, won Senate District 5 in 2018, and has served on Labor, Health and Social Services, among other committees.

She is not running for re-election in 2026; her current term ends January 2027.

EducationCheyenne East High School, 1977; attended the University of Wyoming (journalism)
MilitaryWyoming, Colorado, and Alaska Air National Guard, about 1978–2000, retiring as a master sergeant. Her official legislative profile lists 42½ years’ service in the Air National Guard, which appears to combine Guard time with related civilian or contractor IT work.
Prior officesWyoming House District 42, 2013–2015; Wyoming Senate District 5, 2019–Present
Issue positions

2010 and 2018 campaign themes

From her 2010 House questionnaire and 2018 Senate campaign materials, recurring themes were limited government, fiscal discipline and no state income tax, personal property rights, free enterprise, transparency and accountability, pro-life policy, Second Amendment rights, charter and home-school choice, protection of Wyoming natural resources and state sovereignty, border security, and individual responsibility. She presented herself as a “finisher” with military leadership experience who would defend “Wyoming values.”
