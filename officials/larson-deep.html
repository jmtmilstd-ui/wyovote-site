Background

Joshua Thomas “J.T.” Larson is a lifelong Sweetwater County resident. He was the youngest member of the Wyoming House when first elected in 2022.

Committee work has included Minerals, Business and Economic Development and the Select Water Committee.

In April 2026 he announced he would not run again, citing a desire to focus on his professional career while finishing his term, which ends January 2027.

EducationA.S. in Business Administration, Western Wyoming Community College, 2021; B.S. in Accounting, University of Wyoming, 2023; M.B.A., University of Wyoming, 2025; Rock Springs High School, 2019
Prior officesWyoming House District 17, 2023–Present

Issue positions

Campaign themes (2022 and 2024)

His campaigns and website emphasized local economic protection, water resources, parental rights, school choice, opposition to the BLM Rock Springs Resource Management Plan, funding for the new Rock Springs High School, and support for Wyoming peace officers assisting at the southern border.

Energy and land use

In guest columns he argued Wyoming must adapt to save coal rather than rely solely on political defense, while staying “open for business,” supporting economic diversification, and opposing federal overreach.
