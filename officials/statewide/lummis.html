Notes

Public-record biographical notes. Empty claims are omitted.

  • Sworn into the U.S. Senate on Jan. 3, 2021; first woman to serve as a United States Senator from Wyoming (official Senate biography).
  • Born in Cheyenne, Sept. 10, 1954 (Congressional Directory). Official Senate biography: born on a cattle ranch in Laramie County; three-time graduate of the University of Wyoming in animal science, biology, and law.
  • Education: B.S., animal science, University of Wyoming, 1976; B.S., biology, University of Wyoming, 1978; J.D., University of Wyoming, 1985 (Congressional Directory).
  • Congressional Directory: attorney (from 1986) and rancher (from 1976); clerk, Wyoming Supreme Court, 1985; Wyoming House, 1979–1982 and 1985–1993; Wyoming Senate, 1993–1994; general counsel, Office of the Governor, 1995–1997; Interim Director of State Lands, 1997–1998; State Treasurer, 1998–2006.
  • Elected to the U.S. House on Nov. 4, 2008, and reelected to the three succeeding Congresses (Congressional Directory); served in the 111th–114th Congresses (Congress.gov). Official Senate biography: after leaving the House in 2016, operated her family's cattle ranches and the Sweetgrass development in Laramie County with her brother and sister.
  • Elected to the U.S. Senate on Nov. 3, 2020 (Congressional Directory). Official general results: 198,100 votes to 72,766 for Merav Ben-David.
  • Official Senate office: lummis.senate.gov.
