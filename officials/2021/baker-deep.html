Each point is overall rank shown as a percent position in that year’s list, from 0 (MOST Conservative, #1) at the top to 100 (Most Liberal) at the bottom, so a year with 90 ranked legislators can be compared with a year with 93. Labels give the actual rank, such as 77 of 92. The solid line is each year’s rank. The brick-red arrow is the trend: it starts at the first ranked year and ends, at the last ranked year, at this person’s average position across all ranked years.
