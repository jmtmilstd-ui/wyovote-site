Background

John Bear was born in Trenton, Missouri. Bear served in the U.S. Navy. He earned a B.A. in economics from the University of Colorado in 1988. Bear's career experience includes owning a business.

I have been representing the hard working people of Gillette for three terms. I’m a Christian and veteran naval officer. I married my college sweetheart Sage. We raised two children in Gillette and our daughter lives here with her family. My family and friends are the primary reason I wish to serve the people of Campbell County.

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Today the families in House District 31 are facing inflation that is cutting into their budget everyday. I have advanced real and lasting tax relief for the people of Wyoming and I will continue to do that as well as striving to ensure that state and local governments do not add to the cost of achieving the American …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

The One Big Beautiful Bill will provide nearly $1 billion to Wyoming for Rural Health Transformation. I authored the bill determining how this money will be spent. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I have no interest in selling public lands. If Wyoming residents want to have the public lands managed for their best interests, then the government closest to the people of Wyoming should be the government to manage those lands. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I would ensure, as I already have through my work as chairman of the House Appropriations Committee, that local governments have access to steady streams of revenue other than residential property taxes. I led the way in redistributing sales tax, already being collected for the state, to local governments. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The state should first and foremost create a business freindly environment by having the lowest tax burden and the least restrictive regulatory burden. The Wyoming Business Counsel should be the chief advertising and promoting arm of the state when it comes to developing new business relationships. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I believe that the current system honors both transparency for those who wish to see who is funding whom and the first amendment rights of individuals who wish to participate in this representative government.
