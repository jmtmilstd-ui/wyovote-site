Background

Evan Simpson of Afton served in the Wyoming House from 2017 to 2022, representing House District 21. The 2021–22 legislative directory said he was born Feb. 11, 1957, in Afton; listed a B.S. in engineering from the University of Wyoming; and identified him as a retired consulting civil engineer. It also listed service as a trustee of the Lincoln County North Hospital District, and said he was married to his wife, Bev, and had six children and 18 grandchildren.
