Background

Jerry Paxton of Encampment served in the Wyoming House from 2013 to 2022, representing House District 47. The 2021–22 legislative directory said he was born Dec. 19, 1944, in Riverton; listed a B.S. and an M.S. from the University of Wyoming; identified him as a retired school principal; and recorded U.S. Army service. It also listed service as town council member and mayor of Encampment, town council member and mayor of Riverside, and Carbon County commissioner, and said he was single with two daughters and four grandchildren.
