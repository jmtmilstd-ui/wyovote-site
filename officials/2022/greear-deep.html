Background

Michael (Mike) Greear of Worland served in the Wyoming House from 2011 to 2022, representing House District 27, and was listed as House speaker pro tempore in the 2021–22 legislative directory. That directory said he was born March 1, 1967, in Sturgis, South Dakota; listed an A.A. from Sheridan College, a B.S. in economics from the University of Wyoming, and a J.D. from the University of Wyoming; identified him as a CEO; and recorded U.S. Army service. It said he was married to his wife, Tiffany, and had two children and one grandchild.
