Background

Pam Thayer's career experience includes working as executive director of Rawlins Downtown Development Authority/Main Street.

Originally from Lakewood, Colorado. Rawlins DDA director since spring 2006; the program was accepted as a nationally accredited Main Street program after her first year. Previously a consultant to the Carbon Mercantile Board for a community clothing store in Rawlins. Earlier employers listed in her DDA staff biography include ESPIRIT Clothing, Bausch & Lomb/Ray-Ban, and Banana Republic.
