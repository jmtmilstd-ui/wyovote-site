Research

OccupationBanker; former chairman and CEO of Western States Bank, later consulting and managing member of Snowy Range Investments, LLC
Background

Born in Rawlins, he played football at the University of Wyoming, was drafted by the Miami Dolphins in 1982, and later officiated NCAA Division I football and basketball for more than 30 years.

He spent a long career in banking, co-founding Wyoming State Bank in 2006, which became Western States Bank, headquartered in Laramie, and served as chairman and CEO until a 2022 sale. Civic roles have included chairman of the Ivinson Memorial Hospital Board and prior service on the Wyoming State Board of Education and the Wyoming Banking Board.

He assumed office January 6, 2025. His current term runs through January 2029; the seat was not on the 2026 ballot.

EducationB.S. in Finance, University of Wyoming, 1981; M.B.A., University of Utah, 2004; Rawlins High School, 1977
Prior officesWyoming Senate District 10, 2025–Present
Issue positions

2024 campaign priorities

Stated 2024 priorities included restoring “civility” and “common sense” in the Legislature; rural health-care access; education funding for K-12 and the University of Wyoming; addressing rising residential property taxes while protecting funding for schools and local government; property rights and reducing regulatory barriers to housing; “all of the above” energy with emphasis on Wyoming’s mineral industries; and economic development described as a “hand up, not a hand out.”

Property taxes

He campaigned for a “fair” property-tax structure mindful of school and local-government funding rather than a total repeal.

Education choice

He supported education savings accounts during the campaign.
