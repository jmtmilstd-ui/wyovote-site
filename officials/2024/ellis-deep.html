Background

Affie Ellis of Cheyenne served in the Wyoming Senate from 2017 to 2024, representing Senate District 8. The 2021–22 legislative directory listed her as an attorney, with a B.S. in political science and American Indian studies from the University of Wyoming (2000) and a J.D. from the University of Colorado Law School (2007). The same entry said she was married to her husband, Dennis, and had three children.
