Research

ResidenceThermopolis
OccupationRancher; former Bureau of Land Management range conservationist and big-game outfitter
Background

Born in Cody, he worked for the Bureau of Land Management as a range conservationist and wild horse specialist for about 14 years, then operated Two Ocean Pass Outfitting for decades before selling the business around the time of his first legislative campaign.

He has lived near Thermopolis since 1999 and is a founding member of the Wyoming Freedom Caucus. He chaired the House Agriculture, State and Public Lands and Water Resources Committee during the 2025 session.

He did not run in the 2026 election; his current term ends January 2027.

EducationB.S. in range management, University of Wyoming, 1972
Prior officesWyoming House District 28, 2019–Present
Issue positions

Governing goal

He has consistently described his goal as making Wyoming “the most conservative state in the Union” and pushing back against what he calls spending sprees and “liberal minded people in Cheyenne.”

Candidate themes

Recurring themes in his candidate statements include limited government, fiscal restraint, private property rights, opposition to federal overreach on land and wildlife, protection of agriculture and minerals, election integrity, Second Amendment rights, pro-life positions, and opposition to Medicaid expansion and federal 30x30 land-conservation initiatives.
