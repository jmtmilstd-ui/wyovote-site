How to read this report

Each legislator is ranked by how conservatively he or she voted on the roll calls of the session, scored with the WyoVote Bill Rating Guide. Rank 1 is the most conservative. Every row shows two ranks: the overall rank among all 92 ranked legislators of both chambers, and the chamber rank (House 1–61, Senate 1–31). Within each chamber section the legislators are listed in chamber rank order.

The bands group legislators who voted most alike this session, split at the natural breaks in the ranking, so band sizes can differ; legislators with the same result share a band. The same session-wide bands apply on every page. Votes = counted Aye and Nay votes; excused, absent and conflict votes are not counted.

Minimum-vote rule: a legislator needs at least 25% of the counted votes of the legislator with the most votes (545, so at least 137) to be ranked and banded. 1 legislator below it is not ranked or banded and is listed at the end of the chamber section as “Not ranked: too few votes” (NR). Bands and ranks use the ranked legislators only.
