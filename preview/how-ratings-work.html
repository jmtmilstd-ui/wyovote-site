How WyoVote Ratings Work

Method as used in the 2022–2026 rating reports (v2.1 for 2024–2026; v1.2 for 2022; v1.3 for 2023).

Three levels Rating table Logic flow

Three levels: roll call, bill, ranking

1. A roll-call vote is scored

Each recorded floor or committee vote gets one score from −3.000 (liberal) to +3.000 (conservative). Decimals are allowed (for example 1.333). The score follows the WyoVote Bill Rating Guide categories below. Procedural motions with no policy content score 0.000. Floor votes use positive IDs; committee votes use negative IDs.

2. A bill is not one score

One bill can have many roll calls: introduction, readings, committee, concurrence, amendments, conference reports, veto overrides. Non-amendment votes on the same bill share that bill’s one score. Conference committee reports and full-bill veto overrides also take the bill’s score. From 2024 on, every amendment is scored on its own. In 2022 and 2023 only, some amendments were not scored individually and got 0.000 (6 in 2022, 55 in 2023), and line-item veto overrides got 0.000 (18 in 2022, 15 in 2023). The bill itself is not ranked; only the roll-call scores feed the legislator rating.

3. The legislator rating and ranking

  • Aye = +score for that roll call.
  • Nay = −score (sign flip).
  • Rating = sum of those signed values ÷ number of Aye + Nay votes.
  • Zero-score roll calls are included in the count.
  • Excused, Absent and Conflict are not counted; they are tallied separately.
  • Ratings are rounded to 3 decimals before ranking.
  • Legislators are ranked by that average (most conservative = Rank 1).
  • Bands are set each session by splitting all legislators of both chambers into thirds by rank (for 93 legislators: ranks 1–31 Conservative, 32–62 Moderate, 63–93 Liberal; 2022 had 90; in 2023 a tie at ranks 31 and 32 made it 12022 had 90)ndash;32, 332022 had 90)ndash;62, 632022 had 90)ndash;93). House and Senate reports use the same session-wide bands. Legislators tied at a cutoff share the band.

Bill Rating Guide (evaluation table)

Policy scores cite one or more of these guide codes; procedural votes (0.000) and budget amendments scored by dollar size do not. There is no 0800 in the guide. Scale meaning: +3.000 = most conservative, 0.000 = neutral or procedural, −3.000 = most liberal. Actual scores run from about −2.0 to +3.0.
