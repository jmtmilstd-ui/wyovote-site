Interim committees finish bill drafts before the 2027 General Session

News

By WyoVote

Week of Nov 16, 2026

Panels finish drafts before Jan. 12. Education meets Nov. 17. Blockchain meets Nov. 19-20. Council meets Nov. 23.

CHEYENNE — Three legislative panels meet in Cheyenne this week to finish interim bill drafts before the 2027 General Session.1 The 69th Legislature opens that session at 2 p.m. on Jan. 12 in the House and Senate chambers.7, 9 A joint session is scheduled for noon on Jan. 13 in the House chamber.7 A general session is limited to 40 legislative days.8

Education, Nov. 17

The Joint Education Committee meets at 8:30 a.m. on Nov. 17 in Capitol Extension Room E005.3 It will consider draft legislation on a study of funding for nonpublic special-purpose schools, a change in how charter-school resources are calculated in the education resource block grant model, and charter-school eligibility for state educational grants.3 The draft bills on the agenda are 27LSO-0168, 27LSO-0166, and 27LSO-0167.3

Blockchain Committee, Nov. 19-20

The Select Committee on Blockchain, Financial Technology and Digital Innovation Technology meets at 8:30 a.m. on Nov. 19-20 in Capitol Room W110.4 The committee will receive an update from the Office of U.S. Senator Cynthia Lummis.4 It will also consider bill drafts on the Wyoming Stable Token, digital identifiers, data privacy, automatic license plate readers, the Model Money Transmission Modernization Act, and public utilities.4

Management Council, Nov. 23

Management Council meets at 8 a.m. on Nov. 23 in the Historic Supreme Court Chamber, State Capitol Room E202.5 The first item on the posted agenda is the 2027 General Session calendar.5 The council will also hear reports from other interim panels and consider additional bill drafts.5 Nov. 26 is a state holiday.1

Revenue and the Forecast

The Joint Revenue Committee meets at 8:30 a.m. on Nov. 30-Dec. 1 in Capitol Extension Room E005 to finalize interim work.6 The committee will consider bill drafts and receive information on residential, governmental, and industrial property tax; motor-vehicle property-tax relief; vehicle sales-tax collection; sales- and use-tax exemption reporting; penny rounding; linear infrastructure taxation; modified netback; and severance tax.6

Those tax discussions follow the October 2026 report of the Consensus Revenue Estimating Group.10 After required investment transfers, fiscal year 2026 General Fund collections that remained in the fund totaled $1.461 billion, or $49.9 million above the January 2026 forecast.10 The same report increased the General Fund revenue outlook for the 2027-2028 biennium by $257.3 million, after required transfers and automatic appropriations, compared with the March 2026 fiscal profile.10 The Joint Appropriations Committee meets Dec. 7-11 at 8:30 a.m. in State Capitol E301.1

Meetings are open to the public in Cheyenne and by livestream at wyoleg.gov.1, 2 A limited number of people may sign up to comment remotely on a listed agenda item by using the Legislature’s public-comment form.1
