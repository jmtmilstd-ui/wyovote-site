Jeffrey Haggit

Jeffrey Haggit (Constitution, Mt View): Recently retired instrumentation technician after 49 years at the same company in southwest Wyoming’s minerals-extraction industry, including trona processing and stack monitoring (self-described, October 2026). Lost the Nov. 3, 2026 general election for U.S. Representative with 4.3% of the vote.

United States Representative · Constitution

General result: U.S. House, Constitution, 7,961 votes (4.3%), Lost.

Background

Has lived in Wyoming since 1976; mailing city on the candidate filing is Mountain View (Uinta County). Third campaign for U.S. House on the Constitution Party line (also 2020 and 2024). Former chairman of the Constitution Party of Wyoming. Also served on the United Way of Southwest Wyoming’s Uinta Advisory Council, according to Wyoming Public Media.

EducationGraduate, Western Wyoming Community College
Websitehttps://HAGGITFORHOUSE.COM
Issue positions

Federal power and the Tenth Amendment

Wants to restrain the federal government and return power to the states under the Tenth Amendment, saying Washington has “got too big for their britches.”

Data centers

Opposes the current pace of data-center development, citing water and electricity use and concern they could support a surveillance state; says communities should not give tax breaks and that the issue is for states to work out.

Property taxes

Opposes property taxes in principle and supports the 50% residential cut ballot measure, while favoring a Uinta County 1% sales tax for fire apparatus if it offsets lost property-tax revenue.

Energy and foreign policy

Supports continuing coal jobs and clean-burn technology from his stack-monitoring work; said the country needs another oil refinery. Opposed foreign military alliances such as those he associated with the NDAA and said the United Nations should be removed from the country.
