Carl Manning

Carl Manning (Republican, Riverton): Fremont County School District 25, At-large, –Present; Fremont County School District #25 Board of Trustees. Unopposed in the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 55.

House District 55 · Republican

Primary result: Unopposed

General result: State House HD55, Republican, 2,334 votes (100%), Unopposed.

Research

ResidenceRiverton, Fremont County
Background

I am a lifelong Riverton resident and have been married to my wife, Tanis, since 1980. Together we have two daughters and five grandchildren. Family and community have always been central to my life and my commitment to public service. For more than 30 years, I have served on the Fremont County School District #25 Board of Trustees, working with educators, parents, students, and taxpayers to make thoughtful decisions that support our schools and communities. That experience has given me firsthand knowledge of budgeting, policy development, long-range planning, and the importance of responsible government. …

Prior officesFremont County School District 25, At-large; Fremont County School District #25 Board of Trustees
Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

One of the biggest challenges facing Wyoming today is the loss of civility and the growing difficulty of working together to solve problems. Too often, political disagreements get in the way of progress, and important issues are left unresolved. I also see this as one of Wyoming’s greatest opportunities. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Healthcare access and affordability are critical issues for Wyoming, especially in our rural communities. We must ensure that basic healthcare services remain available while also addressing the funding challenges. One area that deserves immediate attention is mental health. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

No. Wyoming’s public lands are a vital part of our economy, our heritage, and our way of life. They support agriculture, energy development, recreation, tourism, and public access opportunities that benefit Wyoming residents and future generations. I do not support efforts to sell off public lands. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I understand the public’s desire for property tax relief, but we also have to be honest about the consequences of reducing revenue that funds essential local services. Wyoming’s counties are different, and what works in one community may not work in another. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

Economic development is critical to Wyoming’s future. The Legislature should help create an environment where businesses can grow, invest, and create jobs while allowing communities to shape their own economic priorities. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I believe voters deserve transparency and honesty when it comes to campaign financing. People have a right to know who is supporting candidates and where campaign money is coming from so they can make informed decisions at the ballot box. The last election cycle highlighted concerns that should not be ignored. …
