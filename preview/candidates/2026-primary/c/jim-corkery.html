Jim Corkery

Jim Corkery (Republican, Casper): Business person (account servicing, compliance and incentive-payment management roles). Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State Senate District 27 with 16.9% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost

Background

Ran on property-tax reform, government accountability and school choice. Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for Senate District 27 (Casper): Kevin Helling 1,928 (53.1%), Marcia Neumiller 1,093 (30.1%), Jim Corkery 612 (16.8%).

I am a husband, father, business person, and community volunteer. While in business, I managed various roles from account servicing, compliance, and delivering incentive payments to internal and external business partners. In this role, I worked with auditing staff from all levels of government. I also managed staff in each of the roles I held. I have also been involved in various community service efforts. I was the head of a mens group within my church, which on an annual basis put on a fundraiser for a local food bank and participated in a program to provide winter coats to children in need. I am volunteer National Ski Patroller up at Casper Mountain. …

Websitewww.jimcorkeryforwyo.com
Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

The biggest challenges for Wyoming is reliance on mineral revenues which tend to fluctuate, the need to diversify economic opportunities, and spending at all levels of government. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

The first effort is looking at the ability to deliver services. I believe Telehealth is one option that can assist and we want to make sure there are no impediments in the use of this option. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I don't support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming.

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I support the most recent legislative efforts of property tax relief as they have attempted to balance relief to the tax payer while providing funding for local revenue needs. The legislature has offset cuts with backfill of other revenue. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

I think the Legislature should create an economic, regulatory and educational environment that would incentivize business to develop in Wyoming and encourage existing business to strongly consider moving operations and jobs to our state. And I think there are many opportunities to do so. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I believe in complete transparency when it comes to campaign donations. I think the current Wyoming system works well with the exception of the timing. I think the timing of disclosure be shown earlier than a week before in-person voting.
