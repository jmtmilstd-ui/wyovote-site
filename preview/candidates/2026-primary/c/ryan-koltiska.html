Ryan Koltiska

Ryan Koltiska (Republican, Sheridan): Former pastor (Wesleyan Church, 15 years); former executive director, Sheridan KidsLife; former marketing director, Sheridan County Chamber of Commerce. Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 30 with 62.7% of the vote.

House District 30 · Republican

Primary result: Won

General result: State House HD30, Republican, 2,963 votes (100%), Unopposed.

Background

I have spent most of my life serving others and helping strengthen the communities where I live. For 15 years, I served as a pastor in the Wesleyan Church, walking alongside families through life's challenges and learning the value of servant leadership. As Director of Marketing for the Sheridan County Chamber of Commerce, I gained a firsthand understanding of the importance of a strong business community and the role collaboration plays in solving local challenges. Later, as Executive Director of Sheridan KidsLife, I worked closely with children, giving me an appreciation for the challenges they face. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

One of the greatest challenges facing Wyoming today is ensuring that our economy provides enough opportunity for the people to stay, work, and raise a family here. Too many people leave Wyoming in search of better job opportunities, while many employers struggle to find the workforce they need. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

My family has experienced the rising cost of healthcare firsthand. With four kids, our second largest expense each year, after housing, is healthcare. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

Wyoming's public lands are one of our state's greatest assets. I’ve spent a lifetime enjoying them and would love to see my children and future generations do the same. They support agriculture, energy production, outdoor recreation, wildlife habitat, tourism, and our way of life. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I understand why many homeowners are asking for relief. I own my home and the cost of living affects us daily. At the same time, property taxes help fund essential local services such as schools, roads, law enforcement, and fire protection. These services affect us daily as well. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Legislature should focus on the fundamentals that make Wyoming an attractive place to invest and create jobs. That includes maintaining a business-friendly regulatory environment, investing in critical infrastructure, supporting workforce development, and ensuring that housing is available and affordable for …
