Background

I've gained an extensive understanding of the oil & gas industry having 30+ years of front-line experience. Managing large budgets, complex projects, working within teams holding dizzying arrays of diverse complexities, I'm well prepared to represent my district's multifaceted perspectives. District 56 is one of the most politically mixed in the State. As such, effective representation should position in the center. I'm willing to reach in both directions so that the votes made in Cheyenne best represent the constituents rather than myopic views of the representative. What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

One perspective I've recently brushed with is the current pay scale of healthcare providers in our State to ensure we're providing up to date and competitive rates to our providers as a means of stabilizing this community and keep employees 'in-house' with reduced turnover. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

Open dialog is a must when land management changes are proposed. I'll emphasize transparency of information prior to decisions being made. Broadly, public land is a treasure to Wyoming which must be protected so that citizens can enjoy and cherish these lands without jeopardizing its long term health and access. …

The reductions to our assessed residential property values via exemptions has allowed significant tax relief for us. The big bump we had in our tax bills was largely driven by inflation and the exemptions have eliminated this bump. Good! However, increased prices are still there. We feel it as does the government. …
