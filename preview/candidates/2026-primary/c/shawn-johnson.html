Shawn Johnson

Shawn Johnson (Libertarian, Thermopolis): Attorney in Wyoming, primarily family law and criminal defense, with some civil litigation; former Natrona County deputy sheriff (about 13 years); Army National Guard combat medic, 21 years: seven years in the Colorado National Guard and the remainder in the Wyoming National Guard; combat deployment to Iraq, 2003–2004. Joined junior year of high school. Lost the Nov. 3, 2026 general election for U.S. Representative with 7.5% of the vote.

United States Representative · Libertarian

General result: U.S. House, Libertarian, 13,667 votes (7.5%), Lost.

Background

Born in Casper; spent his teenage years in the Denver area and graduated high school in Westminster, Colorado, then returned to Wyoming. Married, with two stepdaughters and a son; mailing city and current residence Thermopolis (self-reported). Also ran as a Libertarian for House District 28 in 2020.

Issue positions

Role of Congress

Wants an independent Wyoming voice who can form coalitions, check executive power, and represent the state rather than follow party or White House direction. As a third-party member he said an evenly split Congress could give Wyoming leverage.

Economy, war, and health care

Cited cost of living and an “unnecessary Middle Eastern war” with Iran as linked problems he would want to end; also cited high health-care costs, lost rural-hospital funding after changes to the Affordable Care Act, and the need for a replacement that lowers insurance costs.

Limited government

Campaign site emphasizes individual liberty, careful use of tax money, and sending Americans into harm’s way only as a last resort.
