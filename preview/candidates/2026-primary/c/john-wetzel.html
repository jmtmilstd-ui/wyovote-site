Research

ResidencePowell, Park County
Background

Living in Powell for 34 years, I am rooted in the community. I have spent 20 years as Powell Mayor and City Council Representative. I also served as President of the Powell Chamber of Commerce. Those roles were working positions that put me at the table for real decisions affecting real people. Beyond city government I have represented Powell on the Wyoming Association of Municipalities board, served on the Park County Travel Council, and aided citizens from the Yellowstone County Assistance Network board. …

Prior officesMayor of Powell / Powell City Council
Websitewetzel4wyo.com
Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Wyoming faces challenges with an economy heavily dependent on mineral revenues that fluctuate, a workforce shortages and communities working hard to remain viable as demographics shift. The opportunity is that Wyoming still has choices other states have already lost. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

When the nearest specialist is hours away and premiums consume a growing share of a family's income, that is a real problem with real consequences for working people. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I do not support the broad sale or transfer of Wyoming's public lands, and I say that as someone who has spent years championing outdoor recreation and public land access in Park County. Public lands are part of Wyoming's identity and its economy. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

Property tax relief is a concern for many Wyoming families, particularly long-term residents on fixed incomes. Local services — roads, emergency response, schools — depend on a revenue base that cannot be legislated away from Cheyenne without consequences. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Legislature's most valuable contribution is creating the conditions where private enterprise can take root and grow. That means keeping taxes low and predictable, maintaining a regulatory environment that doesn't punish investment, and building the infrastructure — roads, water systems, workforce pipelines through …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I believe in full transparency. Voters deserve to know who is funding campaigns and in what amounts. Wyoming's current reporting system is functional, but I am open to reforms that improve timeliness and accessibility of disclosure data. …
