Edis Allen (Republican, Glenrock): Dry-cleaning franchise owner (Martinizing of Casper) and commercial real estate developer; U.S. Marine Corps veteran. Lost the Aug. 18, ate House District 62 with 39.3% of the vote.

Background

Edis Allen is a Casper entrepreneur and Marine Corps veteran who owns and operates Martinizing of Casper (franchise owner since 2004) and works in commercial real estate development; BallotReady also lists past service on the Newcastle City Council. He ran for Casper City Council Ward 2 in 2020. In the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for House District 62 he lost to Kevin Campbell by six votes, 788 (49.6%) to 794 (49.9%). In 2026 he challenged now-incumbent Campbell again on a platform of limited government, support for Wyoming families and congressional term limits, and is a Leadership Wyoming participant; he lost the Aug. 18, 2026 rematch 729 (39.2%) to 1,126 (60.5%).
