Issue positions

What is your position on parental rights?

Parents have the right to know, to be involved, and to make the best decisions for their children.

What is your position on truth and transparency in government?

Honest information. Open government. No hidden agendas. The people deserve the truth.

What is your position on accountability of elected officials?

Elected officials work for you. Accountability, integrity, and results are non-negotiable.
