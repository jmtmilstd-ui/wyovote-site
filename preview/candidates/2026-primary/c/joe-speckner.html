Joe Speckner

Joe Speckner (Republican, Lingle): Attorney (Torrington) and family farmer/cattle feeder (Lingle). Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 5 with 76.3% of the vote.

House District 5 · Republican

Primary result: Won

Research

ResidenceLingle, Goshen County
OccupationAttorney (Torrington) and family farmer/cattle feeder (Lingle)
Background

I am a lifelong Goshen County resident and third-generation family farmer from Lingle. I graduated from Lingle-Fort Laramie High School and then attended EWC before earning my undergraduate and law degrees from the University of Wyoming. I currently practice law in Torrington while actively working on my family’s cattle feeding and farming operation near Lingle. My hands-on experience as an agricultural producer and attorney gives him practical, firsthand knowledge of the challenges facing Goshen County families, including private property rights, water rights, taxes, and regulatory burdens. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

The two greatest opportunities before Wyoming today are our people and our resources. The world is hungry for the energy, agricultural products, and natural resources Wyoming produces, positioning our state to power the economy of tomorrow. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

In our vast rural state, too many families face high prices, long travel distances, and shortages of doctors and specialists. As a legislator, I will work to make care more affordable and accessible by expanding telemedicine so Wyomingites can access providers even in rural communities, strengthening our healthcare …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I do not support the wholesale sale of Wyoming's public lands to private interests. These lands belong to the people and must serve the needs of the people. I support a multiple use strategy including energy development, agricultural use, recreation, and hunting. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

The government has constitutional obligations to the public. Taxes should never be more than is necessary to meet these obligations. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Wyoming Legislature's role in economic development is to create an environment where businesses, entrepreneurs, and families can thrive without picking winners and losers with taxpayer subsidies. Government's job is to get out of the way. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

When it comes to campaign donations, transparency, integrity, and respect for free speech is important. I welcome support from individuals, businesses, and organizations who share my commitment to high moral values, fair tax policy, and responsible resource development. …
