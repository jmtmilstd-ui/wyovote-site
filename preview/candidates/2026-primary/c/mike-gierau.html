Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

To me our challenges are the desire to have a way of life we love in a time when all is changing around us. To take advantage of opportunities and to do it on our terms. Thanks to years of hard work by our predecessors we have the financial capacity to do anything! Not Everything but anything.

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

As our population ages this question is at the top of our list. No single answer but we are working this summer to drill down and move forward.Nothing should be off the table.

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I have a record on this one and I can say the key is working with all parties. The beautiful thing is we still have the vast majority of our state in public hands. The energy industry, the Tourism industry, the Agriculture industry All have a critical role to play. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

Four years ago the number one issue for my constituents was tax reform. Today every homeowner has the ability to access programs to provide relief. Now we must keep watch and adjust where necessary to fund local governments, and all special districts. I do not believe the ballot measure on taxes is good idea. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

Today we as a State compete in a global market place. We must leverage our strengths whenever possible. Folks need to remember government working for economic development has been going on for a while. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I have told every donor to me the same thing. “Your support is welcome but if you expect anything more than a thank you might be disappointed.” That said a main focus for us should be pay legislators. We are at a crossroads here. To serve in the Legislature is a great honor. It also comes at a great cost. …
