Roland Luehne

Roland Luehne (Republican, Thermopolis): Operator of the Star Plunge hot springs pool in Thermopolis. Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 28 with 4.8% of the vote.

House District 28 · Republican

Primary result: Lost

Research

ResidenceThermopolis, Hot Springs County
OccupationOperator of the Star Plunge hot springs pool in Thermopolis
Background

Finished fourth of four in the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for House District 28 with 150 votes (4.8%); Allen Hogg won with 1,525 (49.2%), Marshall Keller 828, Carlin Jones 594.

Age 62 as of July 2026. Lived in Thermopolis since he was 10 and said he has been a resident of House District 28 for 51 years. Has been involved in a dispute with the State of Wyoming over a Star Plunge lease in Hot Springs State Park; he said people asked him to run because of that situation and that he had seen executive-branch overreach.

Issue positions

Property taxes

Supports responsible property-tax relief that helps homeowners while maintaining essential local services; also told Cowboy State Daily he would like to cut property taxes “as far back as humanly possible” and question why a paid-off home should still be taxed.

Economy and local control

Priorities include protecting property rights, supporting small business, tourism, agriculture and energy, reducing unnecessary regulations, and keeping local control for counties, cities and school boards.

Freedom Caucus and social issues

Said he would not “be owned” by the Freedom Caucus though he might vote with it at times. Described himself as pro-life but up to about 90 days’ gestation, adding, “I like women to be able to choose.” On data centers, said Wyoming should strike a balance and “make the right deal.”
