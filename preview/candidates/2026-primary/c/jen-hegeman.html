Jen Hegeman

Jen Hegeman (Republican, Evanston): Evanston City Council member. Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State Senate District 15 with 8.5% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost

Background

Evanston city council member. Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for Senate District 15 with 350 votes (8.5%); incumbent Wendy Davis Schuler won with 2,551 (61.8%), Tina Johnson 1,041, Chris Katzl 184.

Current Evanston City Council member (identified as such at the July 9, 2026 Evanston Chamber of Commerce forum and in post-primary coverage). The same week’s Herald also recorded her among council members voting at a city meeting. A personal introductory sentence in the forum story (“Hegeman said she comes from a …”) was cut off by a page jump and was not applied.

Representation

At a July 9, 2026 Evanston Chamber of Commerce candidate forum she encouraged constituents’ active engagement: “It’s not what I think, it’s what you all think, and I have to hear from you.”

Who she works for

“It doesn’t matter what I think, it matters what everybody in this room and everybody in this county thinks,” she said. “You are my boss. You’re my boss on council, and you would be my boss as a senator.”
