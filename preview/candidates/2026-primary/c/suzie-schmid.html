Suzie Schmid

Suzie Schmid (Democratic, Rock Springs): Has worked with at-risk youth (self-described on her campaign website). Lost the Nov. 3, 2026 general election for State Representative H48 with 29.0% of the vote.

House District 48 · Democratic

General result: State House HD48, Democratic, 639 votes (29%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceRock Springs, Sweetwater County
OccupationHas worked with at-risk youth (self-described on her campaign website)
Background

Lifelong Wyomingite and Rock Springs resident (mailing city on the candidate filing). Says she grew up in the oil and gas industry and saw boom-and-bust cycles firsthand. Ballot name Suzie Schmid.

Websitehttps://SUZIESCHMID.COM
Issue positions

Affordability and work

Wants the “Wyoming Dream” to remain attainable — housing, meaningful work, and enough security to retire and weather an emergency. A strong economy, she wrote, should be measured by whether people who work here can afford to build a life here. Work should provide stability, not merely survival.

Education

Supports ending public subsidies for private education and reinvesting those dollars in public schools; reduce class sizes and administrative overhead so more money reaches classrooms.

Health care

Would address structural barriers to practicing medicine in Wyoming, including insurance, reimbursement, and medical liability; called tort reform a major legislative priority alongside insurance reform.
