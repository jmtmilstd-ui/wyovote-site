Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Biggest challenges: Adequate workers for jobs, Affordable housing, Public Land, Education: Addressing them: survey needs for jobs and educate for them, Finding ways for citizens to afford reasonable housing, Do not allow the sale of public land, Continue to be innovative and move towards the Profile of a Graduate …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Affordable healthcare comes with more insurance companies in the state so their is competition. And hospitals getting more pay for Medicare and Medicaid. Currently getting 45 to 60 cents on the dollar for reimbursement isn't adequate. Certainly less coverage in the hospitals is not the answer. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I do not support the sale of public lands and will do everything in my power to protect our public lands for our use and enjoyment. We have to be diligent in our efforts to manage and oversee those maintaining the lands.

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

First of all, I am opposed to the 50% reduction in property tax. We are the second lowest property taxed state in the United States. Property tax goes to the citizens of the counties and the municipalities and education. Our students are our future so cutting education doesn't make sense. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

Incentives to bring in businesses in is a must and yes, economic development should have a role in our Legislature. It never hurts to evaluate a program and look for changes that would enhance the success of economic development. The state needs to consider paying a little so there is a bigger gain in the end. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

Yes, Wyoming needs a few reforms but, campaign donations should be at the top. The amount coming in from who knows where and the size of those donations clearly leaves the middle class citizens who would consider running out of the picture. …
