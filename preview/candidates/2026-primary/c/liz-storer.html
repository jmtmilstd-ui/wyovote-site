Background

Liz Storer lives in Jackson, Wyoming. Her career experience includes working as the leader of her family's foundation, the George B. Storer Foundation.

I am the Democratic state representative for Wyoming House District 23 and the President and CEO of the George B. Storer Foundation. I was first elected in 2022 to represent Teton County, where I focus on practical, bipartisan solutions that reflect Teton County values and strengthen communities across our state. I lead my family’s foundation, which supports early childhood education, climate and conservation work, and Wyoming communities, and I’ve helped modernize its governance, investment policy, and strategic direction since 2008. Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Wyoming’s biggest challenges are economic dependence on boom‑and‑bust fossil fuel revenues, workforce and housing pressures, and protecting land, water, and communities amid rapid change. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

As a legislator, I would do the following: Expand Medicaid with a Wyoming‑specific plan to cover thousands of low‑income adults, cut uncompensated care and stabilize premiums and rural hospitals. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I do not support large‑scale sale or transfer of Wyoming’s public lands to private interests or away from public ownership. Our lands are the backbone of our economy, outdoor heritage and communities, and once they’re sold, we don’t get them back. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I support targeted relief that keeps people from being taxed out of their homes without blowing a hole in county and school budgets. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Legislature absolutely has a role in smart economic development that serves Wyoming people, not just the next boom. I believe we should be more aggressive and strategic in using a portion of our permanent funds for rotating, low‑interest loans through SLIB and the Business Council to build community …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I believe transparency and accountability in campaign finance are essential to earning and keeping the public’s trust. I support taking donations that reflect Wyoming values, disclosing them clearly and on time, and refusing money that comes with strings attached or undermines public confidence. …
