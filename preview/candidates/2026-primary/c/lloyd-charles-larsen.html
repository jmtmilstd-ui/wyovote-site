Background

My wife Rebecca and I have been married for 47 years come August, with six children, all graduating from a university, five from the University of Wyoming. We have 23 grandchildren. Only 2 of the five children live in Wyoming. My brother, father and I owned a construction company serving the petroleum industry for 35 years, starting with 3 employees and growing to 100. All the afore mentioned background prepared me well to serve in the legislature. I am in my 14th year of service to the citizens of House District #54 and the State of Wyoming. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

To begin with we need to understand some of the data. For example Wyoming ranks 4th in the number of hospitals per 1,000 people with 29 hospitals in the state in 2024, and it ranks 8th in the nation for the same time period for the number of staffed beds available at 1875. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

We have always had the ability through a variety of ways to sell public lands or transfer lands through an exchange process. I think the objective should be to make sure there is a public benefit in the transaction. A case in point was the sale of the Kelly parcel in Teton County to the federal government. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

The legislature has responded to the public's desire for property tax relief by passing seven bills (2023 - 1, 2024-3, and 2025-3) in an attempt to provide relief. We need to give the legislation some time to see if the bills produce the intended results. …
