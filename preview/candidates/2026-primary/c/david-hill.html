David Hill

David Hill (Republican, Cody): Attorney. Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 50 with 55.2% of the vote.

House District 50 · Republican

Primary result: Won

General result: State House HD50, Republican, 3,213 votes (86%), Won.

Background

David Hill earned a bachelor's degree from Brigham Young University–Idaho in 2013 and a graduate degree and law degree from the University of Wyoming in 2019. His career experience includes working as an attorney.

I am an attorney, former Drug Court Judge, and community leader who has dedicated my career to public service, the rule of law, and strengthening Wyoming communities. I earned an MBA and law degree and have spent years helping individuals, families, businesses, and local governments navigate complex legal issues. I currently serve as President of the Cody Country Chamber of Commerce and have been recognized for professional excellence by various groups including being one of the Wyoming Chamber of Commerce's 40 Under 40. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Wyoming faces three major challenges: rising costs for families and businesses, economic uncertainty tied to the energy industry, and a growing disconnect between government and local communities. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Healthcare in Wyoming is expensive for many of the same reasons it is expensive across the country: a lack of competition, excessive bureaucracy, and policies that drive up costs without improving care. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I do not support the sale or large-scale transfer of Wyoming’s public lands. Public lands are an important part of our state’s heritage, economy, and way of life. They support recreation, hunting, grazing, tourism, and multiple-use access that benefits Wyoming residents and visitors alike. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

Wyoming taxpayers deserve meaningful property tax relief, but we must also recognize that no two counties are the same. Counties that experienced dramatic increases in property valuations should not be treated the same as counties that saw little change. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Legislature has an important role in creating an environment where businesses, workers, and communities can succeed, but it should not try to pick winners and losers in the marketplace. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

Campaign contributions should be transparent so voters know who is supporting candidates and political causes. Wyoming's elections should be decided by Wyoming voters, not by outside organizations or special interests seeking to influence our state's future. …
