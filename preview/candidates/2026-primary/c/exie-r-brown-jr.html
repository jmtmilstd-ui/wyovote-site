Exie R. Brown, Jr.

Exie R. Brown, Jr. (Republican, Cheyenne): Businessman; U.S. Air Force, 1996–2018. Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 9 with 37.8% of the vote.

Background

Exie Brown was born in North Platte, Nebraska. He served in the U.S. Air Force from 1996 to 2018. He earned an associate degree from the Community College of the Air Force in 2010 and a bachelor's degree from Ashford University in 2017. His career experience includes working as a businessman.
