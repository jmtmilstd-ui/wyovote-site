Matthew R Legler

Matthew R Legler (Republican, Powell): Electrical engineer; former Navy officer (Naval Reactors, Naval Sea Systems Command); U.S. Navy officer (years not stated). Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 25 with 48.2% of the vote.

Background

I am a third-generation Wyomingite. I was born in Cody and grew up in Powell. I attended Northwest College, then the University of Wyoming and earned a bachelor's in electrical engineering. I joined the Navy as an Officer after college and worked at Naval Reactors and Naval Sea Systems Command designing ships, submarines, and their systems. I have received many awards for my leadership and hard work for our Nation's security including the Department of Defense Value Engineering Award and the Department of the Navy Cyberspace/IT Person of the Year. I am a Wyoming Republican Party Platform candidate. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

I will start by saying we live in the most amazing and beautiful State in the Union. We have a wealth of public lands which we can all enjoy. There are forces attempting to take and sell these lands. I will fight to keep our lands public. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

I remember how affordable health care was before Obamacare, so the obvious answer is rolling that back. Unfortunately, that is a federal issue to tackle. What we can do is cut unnecessary regulation that forces overhead in the health care systems in Wyoming. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

Public land in Wyoming needs to remain public land. I would support transfer of the land from the Federal government to the State government provided there was a plan to continue to support the land financially.

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I fully support property tax relief. Wyoming has one of the most pro-business environments in the nation. We should be able to attract business to Wyoming to offset the property tax relief. I will make this a priority. We need to offer more local jobs to keep our youth here. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

Wyoming has an incredible business environment. We need to evaluate why businesses aren't flocking here like they are to Texas and Florida. I am sure part of it is infrastructure and part of it is perception that hiring will be challenging in a low population area. We need to address these issues with industry. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

My values are absolute transparency at all times. I am new this year, but from what I can tell Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations is reasonable.
