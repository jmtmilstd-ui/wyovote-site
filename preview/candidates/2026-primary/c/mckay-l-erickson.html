Background

McKay Erickson earned a bachelor's degree from Utah State University in 1992. His career experience includes working as an educator and in timber and powerline services.

I have a deep love for the State of Wyoming that was instilled by my family who established the Valley and Western Wyoming. I taught/coached and advised at Star Valley High School for 29 years after teaching in Utah for one year. I was also the high school teacher representative to the administration and school board for most of those years. I also was active in many community activities and committees during that time including recreation, multiple use, forest collaborations and fish and game. I then served as a precinct committeeman for the Lincoln County Republican Party. I later served as the vice chair of that committee. I was then elected to House District 21 Representative.

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Wyoming faces some major issues going forward but has many things in its favor. I like to focus on the many good people that have Wyoming's best interest in mind. I believe that good ideas and leadership come from many different groups and factions. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

We must make this a priority in word and deed. We have to work together to recognize and fund our unique health care problems. We have to get over comparing our state to that others. We are unique but can make it better if we move this up on list of urgent issue to solve. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I do not support efforts to sell public land. They are very important to our way of life. We must make a strong stand on this and not let national interests overwhelm us that live here.

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I have been very active in approving major steps to provide tax reform. I will continue to do this. I also recognize that we must fund our schools, towns, counties and special districts because they are what makes Wyoming great.

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

I believe that more oversight and accountability can be provided. I think the State can and should be a partner in selected projects to allow growth and development under tight and fair guidelines.

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

Campaign donations should be closely monitored. Out of State donors must be identified and transparent.
