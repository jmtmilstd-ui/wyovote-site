Greg Schreurs

Greg Schreurs (Republican, Gillette): Retired (2016) from private business as a partner in K2 Technologies in Gillette, working with computers and software. Previously Director of Information Services for Campbell County (seven years, left 2005) and senior systems analyst for the State of South Dakota; Gillette City Council (appointed to complete an unexpired term, two years), since 2020; U.S. Air Force, 1972–1992; retired as a major. Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 53 with 39.2% of the vote.

Background

Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for House District 53 (Gillette) to incumbent Christopher Knapp, 525 (39.1%) to 815 (60.7%).

Moved to Gillette in 1998 after retiring from the Air Force. Married to Sharon (53 years as of 2024), two daughters and four grandchildren. Has served on the Pioneer Manor/Legacy advisory board, Council of Community Service executive board, Northeast Wyoming Airport board, and Senior Center Foundation board. Licensed pilot with the Wyoming wing of the Civil Air Patrol.

Issue positions

Subsidies

Generally does not support subsidies; could support limited, temporary incentives for basic research, national security and first-of-a-kind technologies with accountability and a sunset date.

Party platform

Generally supports the Republican Party position; would vote against a party position he opposes.

Representation and trust

Said his main focus is restoring and strengthening trust between voters and their representatives in Cheyenne, with constituents coming first, while identifying as a conservative Republican.

Health care

Cited rising health-insurance prices and rural hospitals struggling because Medicare and Medicaid often do not pay enough; said the state needs to fill that gap to avoid losing more rural hospitals.

University of Wyoming

Said lawmakers have a role in the university’s governance chain because of funding authority.
