Zeta Nuckolls Anderson

Zeta Nuckolls Anderson (Republican, Devils Tower): Learning coach; community volunteer and board member; raised on a family sheep and cattle ranch in Crook County. Lost the Aug. 18, ate House District 1 with 37.2% of the vote.

Background

Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for House District 1 to Troy Claycomb, 1,380 (37.2%) to 2,329 (62.8%).

I'm a lifelong resident of Crook County, raised at the base of the Little Missouri Buttes on my family's sheep and cattle ranch started by my grandfather in 1917. I graduated from Hulett High School, earned my associate's degree at Casper College, and went on to earn my bachelor's and master's degree at the University of Nebraska. But Wyoming has always been home, and I couldn't wait to get back. I've been married to my hometown guy, Gary, for over 28 years. We raised two wonderful daughters here, and I'm now a proud grandmother of three. I've spent my life serving this community; as a volunteer, a learning coach, a board member, and a servant leader.
