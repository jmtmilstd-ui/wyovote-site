Background

Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for House District 61 (Cheyenne) to Andrew Server, 400 to 543. Campaign slogan: "Wyoming Focused. Wyoming First."

Seth Bard’s family came to Wyoming in 2010 and made Cheyenne home — the place where they put down roots and ultimately retired. It’s where Seth earned his Eagle Scout, completing his Eagle Project right here in the community he’s now asking to serve. Seth is a third-generation military member, continuing a family tradition of service as a member of the Wyoming Air National Guard, where he works as a Radar, Airfield, and Weather Systems Technician — maintaining the radar, communications, and airfield systems that keep operations running safely. He’s also pursuing his education at a Wyoming college, investing in the same opportunities he wants every young person in our state to have.

Issue positions

Wyoming’s access to healthcare

Wyoming’s healthcare needs are rising, making critical care hard to find. Wyoming’s access to care must be addressed to ensure Wyoming’s future for years to come.

Strong economy for Wyoming and its workers

With energy, minerals and tourism, Wyoming is OPEN FOR BUSINESS. A strong economy means a strong workforce and strong Wyoming communities.

Support education and parental rights

Parents are Wyoming’s first educators and have a right to spearhead their student’s learning. Parents and educators must collaborate together for the best educational outcomes of all Wyoming students.
