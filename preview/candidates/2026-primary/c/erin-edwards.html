Erin Edwards

Erin Edwards (Republican, Cheyenne): Risk manager. Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 41 with 72.2% of the vote.

House District 41 · Republican

Primary result: Won

Background

Erin Edwards earned a high school diploma from Cheyenne Central High School. She earned an associate degree from Laramie County Community College in 2002 and a bachelor's degree from the University of Wyoming in 2005. Her career experience includes working as a risk manager.

I'm a lifelong Wyoming resident, a volunteer firefighter, and dedicated public servant. I have earned degrees from Laramie County Community College and the University of Wyoming. My life has been built around service coming from a military family. As a firefighter of 24 years, I've answered calls when my neighbors needed help most. I served as a Department of Defense contract firefighter to support our troops during Operation Iraqi Freedom. My experience as a firefighter taught me the importance of teamwork and accountability. It shaped my commitment to serving others with integrity, courage, and common sense. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

One of the biggest challenges Wyoming faces is that both the State and its citizens continue to face increased costs that started several years ago. Wyoming families are paying more for groceries, housing, insurance, utilities, and everyday necessities. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Access to affordable healthcare is one of Wyoming's biggest challenges, especially in our rural communities. The Legislature should work to reduce healthcare deserts by encouraging a growth in heatlhcare systems and servicecs in underserved areas. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I do not support selling public land unless there is an incredibly valid reason to do so. Before considering any proposal to transfer ownership, we must carefully evaluate the financial impact, long-term management responsibilities, public access, and protections for future generations. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I believe property tax relief should be pursued in a way that is targeted, sustainable, and balanced with local government needs. Homeowners are feeling the pressure of rising property valuations and increasing tax bills. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Legislature should keep the state predictable, competitive, and business-friendly, while avoiding unnecessary regulatory or tax burdens that discourage investment. At the same time, economic development in Wyoming cannot be “one-size-fits-all.” What works in one county may not work in others. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

Wyoming’s system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures should be simple, transparent, and low-burden for both candidates and the public. …
