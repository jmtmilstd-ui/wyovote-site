Dan McIntosh

Dan McIntosh (Republican, Cheyenne): Economist; U.S. Air Force Reserve. Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 11 with 79.3% of the vote.

House District 11 · Republican

Primary result: Won

ResidenceCheyenne, Laramie County
OccupationEconomist
Background

I went to Deming, Miller, McCormick, Central High, and LCCC. I worked in the trades in my teens and 20s, then earned a bachelor's in business and a master's in engineering at the University of Colorado, with doctoral work in economics at the University of Hamburg. These days I direct the Wyoming Center for Business and Economic Analysis at LCCC, teach economics and public policy there, and serve as a space operations officer in the Air Force Reserve. Outside of that I'm a small business owner, I coach cross country at East High, and I help out with the Unaccompanied Student Initiative, the Children's Museum, and Rooted in Cheyenne. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

One of the best parts of running for office is going door to door and learning what matters to voters in HD 11, and many of those issues aren't the ones making headlines. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Healthcare is expensive here partly because demand keeps rising while supply stays thin. Wyoming is rural, providers are spread out, and there aren't many options. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

No. I don't support selling off our public lands or a wholesale transfer to the state. Around ninety percent of Wyomingites use public land to hunt, fish, work, and get outside, and once it's sold it's gone for good. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

This is where my day job and homegrown knowledge of Laramie County both come in. The recent spike in tax bills wasn't a rate hike, it was inflation pushing up home values and assessments with them. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Legislature's job is to set the table, not pick the winners. We do the most good by keeping the climate competitive for everyone: a simple tax structure, sensible regulation, reliable power and water, and a workforce trained for the jobs we actually have. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

Voters deserve to know who's paying for and backing the campaigns asking for their vote. I'd rather run on what matters to HD 11 and what I actually believe and let voters see exactly where my support comes from. Sunlight does more good than complicated limits the well-connected always find a way around. …
