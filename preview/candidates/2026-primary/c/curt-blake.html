Curt Blake

Curt Blake (Republican, Centennial): Rodeo production, general contractor and real-estate developer. Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for Governor with 1.7% of the vote.

Background

Curt Blake, of Centennial, works in rodeo production and as a general contractor and real-estate developer. He said he would not accept donations, endorsements or debate invitations. He received 710 votes (1.4%) in the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for governor, won by Eric Barlow (22,041).
