Tony Niemiec

Tony Niemiec (Republican, Green River): Law enforcement; Wyoming House of Representatives District 60, 2023–2025; U.S. Marine Corps. Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 60 with 65.7% of the vote.

Background

Tony Niemiec was born in Steubenville, Ohio. Niemiec graduated from Green River High School in 1987. He served in the U.S. Marine Corps and reached the rank of Sergeant. His career experience includes working as a patrol corporal in the Sweetwater County sheriff's office and owning the Tomahawk Tavern.

I graduated from Green River High School in 1987 and proudly served our nation in the United States Marine Corps. After my military service, I spent more than 22 years with the Sweetwater County Sheriff's Office, serving as a law enforcement officer and Bomb Squad Commander. I previously had the honor of serving as State Representative for House District 60, where I served on the Joint Judiciary Committee and the Joint Transportation, Highways and Military Affairs Committee. As a Marine, law enforcement officer, business owner, and legislator, I understand the importance of public safety, responsible government, fiscal accountability, and economic opportunity. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Wyoming's biggest challenge is strengthening and diversifying our economy while protecting the industries that built our state, including energy, mining, agriculture, and tourism. We must create opportunities that keep young people in Wyoming and attract new businesses without sacrificing our values. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Healthcare affordability and access are major concerns, especially in rural Wyoming where many residents must travel long distances for care. The Legislature should focus on practical solutions that increase access while controlling costs. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I support keeping Wyoming lands in public hands. Our public lands are one of Wyoming's greatest assets, providing opportunities for hunting, fishing, recreation, grazing, energy development, tourism, and public access. These lands belong to the people and should remain available for future generations. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

Property tax relief is important, but it must be done responsibly. No two Wyoming counties are the same, and many local governments rely heavily on property tax revenue to fund essential services such as public safety, roads, emergency response, and other community needs. …
