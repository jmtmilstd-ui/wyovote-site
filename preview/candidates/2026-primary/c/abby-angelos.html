Background

Abby Angelos was born in Buffalo, Wyoming. Angelos graduated from Wright High School in 1998. Her career experience includes working as a women's ministry director.

I am a wife, mother of three daughters and a small business owner in Gillette. I've worked in Property and Casualty insurance and several ministry positions over the last 20 years that included, Youth Ministry, Young Adults and Women's Ministry. Currently, I am serving my second term in the Wyoming House of Representatives for House district 3 and am honored to serve and represent my constituents.

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

I get calls weekly from constituents concerned with non-transparent economic development. They feel they will be left out of a decision that could change their communities, property values and quality of life. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

This is a top issue and one that anytime the government gets involved it tends to skyrocket the price. My own health insurance costs are expensive as a small business owner. We as a nation are living with the consequences of the federal governments decision to meddle in the healthcare prices. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

Public Lands needs to be multi use although complicated. We see minerals being produced and that revenue flowing into the state and the enjoyment of hunters and outdoorsmen on our public lands. As far as selling state land to the feds, I'm against it. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

This is an issue that has been wrestled with during every debate for property tax relief. Some counties can weather the decrease and some of our hardship counties could potentially struggle. Every property tax exemption has to be looked at from each counties perspective and we have done that. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

This has been a topic of discussion over the last year and as a member of the Appropriations committee we are discussing this over this summer. There does seem to be a desire to foster an environment to diversify our economy. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I believe in transparency in donations, all donations I receive are filed with the Secretary of State. I currently do not see concerns in how donations are monitored. If you receive a donation you are required to report it.
