Ted Hanlon

Ted Hanlon (Democratic, Cheyenne): Chemical process consultant; owner of Hanlon Process Consulting, LLC (since 2013). Unopposed in the Aug. 18, 2026 Democratic primary for State Senate District 5.

Senate District 5 · Democratic

Primary result: Unopposed

Research

ResidenceCheyenne, Laramie County
OccupationChemical process consultant; owner of Hanlon Process Consulting, LLC (since 2013)
Background

I graduated from Cheyenne East High School in 1997. When I was growing up, my family had a small ranch south of Encampment Wyoming. I spent every weekend and summer there. That is where I developed my love of the mountains. The ranch borders Medicine Bow National Forest, which was a very big back yard! I received a BS in Chemical Engineering from the University of Wyoming. I worked in the oil business for 10 years and for a chemical manufacturing company for 12. I worked for several small consulting companies before starting my own consulting company, Hanlon Process Consulting, LLC, in 2013. My background gives me unique insight into the industries that drive Wyoming's economy. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

We are challenged by a constitutional mandate and supreme court rulings to fund a quality public education.Developing the holistic funding and oversight mechanisms to ensure that we provide every child with the best education possible without a contentious and divisive disagreement every year is a challenge I am ready …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

We can take advantage of the federal support available to us to maximize coverage and reduce premium costs. Wyomingites have paid the federal taxes that fund these programs. It is foolish to reject the funding and make life difficult for so many Wyoming families. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

Everyone in Wyoming (with the possible exception of a few politicians who have been influenced by East Coast agendas) opposes the sale or transfer of public lands. No other issue brings us all together as one community in the same way. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

The legislature must ensure that the tax structure developed by the state has a high enough "ceiling" that each local government can provide the services that their constituents require.

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The purpose of government is to secure collective stability, protect individual rights, and manage systemic risks, creating a predictable framework where people can safely cooperate and prosper. If the legislature takes that purpose seriously, they will always have a central role in economic development.

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

The system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures seems to work pretty well. It would be nice if all campaigns were funded the same, but that isn't the way the world works and it certainly isn't something that I have the power to change.
