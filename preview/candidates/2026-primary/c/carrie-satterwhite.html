Carrie Satterwhite

Carrie Satterwhite (Libertarian, Ralston): Certified instructor for Love and Logic parenting classes through Park County School District 1; also a CASA volunteer in the Fifth Judicial District and a foster grandparent at Westside Elementary in Powell. Lost the Nov. 3, ate Representative H50 with 14.0% of the vote.

House District 50 · Libertarian

General result: State House HD50, Libertarian, 523 votes (14%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceRalston, Park County
OccupationCertified instructor for Love and Logic parenting classes through Park County School District 1; also a CASA volunteer in the Fifth Judicial District and a foster grandparent at Westside Elementary in Powell
Background

Ralston resident (mailing city on the candidate filing) running as a Libertarian for House District 50.

Has lived in Ralston more than 40 years. This was her second campaign for House District 50; she ran as a Libertarian in 2022 and lost the general election to incumbent Rachel Rodriguez-Williams.

Leads Love and Logic parenting classes through Park County School District 1 and said she was one test away from national certification as a parenting trainer. For about 10 years she has volunteered as a foster grandparent at Westside Elementary in Powell, recently working mostly with special-education students. She is a CASA volunteer in the Fifth Judicial District, working with abused children and children removed from their homes, and helps transport neighbors to free food giveaways.

EducationLeft Powell High School without graduating and earned a GED. Later earned a business degree with a minor in accounting, a degree in office administration, and an honors certificate at Northwest College
Issue positions

Health care

Priorities include health-care access. She said Wyoming has not expanded Medicaid and that many people cannot get care; free clinics cannot handle more complicated needs, and there are not enough treatment beds for mental illness or addiction.

Property taxes and cannabis

Said shifting the property-tax burden to a higher sales tax or a new state tax would still be a tax. She wants Wyoming to legalize cannabis for medical and recreational use and direct that revenue to property-tax relief and other needs. She said she lobbies lawmakers in Cheyenne every year on the issue.

Role of government

Said government should focus on core services such as police and fire and stay out of people’s private lives. Other stated priorities include veterans’ benefits and public lands.

Public lands and tourism

Said the state must protect the landscapes and Old West feel that draw visitors while still attracting industry and good-paying jobs. She supports keeping gates closed on backcountry roads.
