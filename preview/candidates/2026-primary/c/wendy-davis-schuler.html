Wendy Davis Schuler

Wendy Davis Schuler (Republican, Evanston): Retired Educator, Retired; Wyoming Senate District 15, 2019–Present. Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State Senate District 15 with 61.8% of the vote.

Background

I’m a 5th generation lifelong resident who grew up on a ranch outside of Mt. View. We also ran a construction company where I learned valuable skills. I graduated from UW with an Education degree and taught and coached in Cheyenne, Lyman H.S. and Evanston H.S.for 40 years. I served 2 terms on the Evanston City Council and 2 terms in the Wyoming Senate. I have served on a number of local boards: Uinta Senior Center Board, EYC (Evanston Youth Board) SWOT (SW Outdoor Trails, Soroptimist Biard,etc.

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Economic diversification, housing, health care, and outmigration of our youth.I think we need to look at incentives and better marketing to let people know we are open for business. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

We have to be proactive with the health transformation funds but we need to also bump up our Medicaid rates, and support our hospitals as best we can. Many of our issues are due to CMS regulations and insurance but the Legislature needs to think outside of the box to come up with solutions to those problems.

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I am NOT in favor of selling our public lands. They belong to the public. I will support any and all legislation to ensure that our public lands remain in public hands.

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I think we need to stop and really get the data from all of the property tax legislation we have passed in the last few years before we move on any other property tax legislation. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

We should be a part of the conversation. Even if the WBC is reformed or changed drastically the Legislature should have skin in the game along with funding issues. We need to encourage businesses to come to Wyoming and the Legislature can be an important stakeholder.

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

The system we have probably needs some reforming. I think it would behoove whomever is the new Sec. of State along with input from the Legislature to take a deep dive into what other states do and come up with the best system possible. …
