Background

Jeremy Haroldson was born in Wheatland, Wyoming. Haroldson earned a degree in power plant technology from Bismarck State College in 2006 and a degree in Biblical theology from Global University in 2016. His career experience includes working as a senior pastor of Impact Ministries.

I am born and raised in Wheatland I worked as a mechanic and welder for 12 years after that I became a pastor at Impact Ministries. I served on a number of different boards through the years and I have now served house district 4 for the last 6 years.

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

One of the biggest challenges today in Wyoming is diversity of economy and keeping our young people in the state. CTE is the backbone of Wyoming's workforce. We need to inform our youth of the job opportunities in the state and how they can make a living in these feilds. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Health care has to become a private market again. Obama care has killed health care and made it finically unattainable for many people. We dont need more govornment trying to fix what the government broke in the first place. Also we need to adress the insurance/PBM issue for pharmaceuticals.

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

No I am not for selling public lands. I feel that we have had a transfer system in place for many years that has worked and we don't need to allow it to open up any more. We need to as a state make sure that we don't allow property sale or transfer to be used for the finical gain of a select group of people.

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I agree every county is different, As i have been in this discussion of property tax I feel that it is time to consider the sales tax increase and elimination of property tax on residential. this might not be possible but needs to be looked at further. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

We need to assure that the regulatory framework for the state is favorable to economic development. We also need to make sure that the infrastructure of our communities is capable of supporting development.

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I only take donations from people. I have not taken pac money and so feel that is the best way to stay transparent.
