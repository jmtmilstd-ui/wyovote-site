Tamara Trujillo

Tamara Trujillo (Constitution, Cheyenne): Administrator and auditor in the private sector; worked 17 years for a large oil company prominent in Wyoming, including policy writing, process improvement, and contracts (self-described, 2026). Lost the Nov. 3, 2026 general election for Secretary of State with 8.4% of the vote.

Secretary of State · Constitution

General result: Secretary of State, Constitution, 15,507 votes (8.4%), Lost.

Background

Constitution Party nominee for secretary of state in 2026 after serving in the Wyoming House (District 44) from 2023 to 2024. Lost a 2024 Republican primary to Lee Filer. In the 2026 interview she described herself as an administrator and auditor with 17 years at a large Wyoming oil company.

Prior officesWyoming House District 44, 2023–2024; Wyoming House of Representatives District 44, 2023–2025
Websitehttps://WWW.VOTETAMARANTRUJILLO.COM (leaves WyoVote)
Issue positions

Why the Constitution Party

Said she first aligned with the Republican Party but later chose the Constitution Party because it better fit her Christian and conservative values and a preference for less regulation; she wanted to help people rather than be stuck to a party.

Elections

Supports election security and availability for U.S. citizens; supports mail-in ballots and hand counting of ballots; does not support secure ballot drop boxes; does not support absentee voting for anyone who wants it; supports letting voters change party at any time.

Federal role in elections

Would work with the federal government only as little as needed for voter integrity, emphasizing state control of elections.

Business filings

Cited both revenue benefits and fraud risks in Wyoming’s easy business-registration system; supports more transparency about business owners and upgrading technology to prevent address misuse.
