Background

I'm a small business owner, mother, and Sheridan neighbor who believes the best government is the smallest one, and the one closest to home. For over a decade, my husband Nate and I have built our life here including two small businesses and children who love Wyoming as much as we do. As owner of Whirly Girl Flowers, I've learned to listen first, work with what's available, and tend to the details that matter. My professional experience includes budgeting, bookkeeping, and hands-on operations for human services non-profits, practical skills suited to representing District 29. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Wyoming's biggest challenges are also its greatest opportunities: a revenue base tied to extractive industries, rising healthcare costs, and the pressure to grow without losing what makes Wyoming worth living in. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Every Wyoming family deserves access to honest, affordable care; whether you're self-employed, ranching an hour from the nearest clinic, or quietly struggling in ways that don't always show. As a small business owner without employer-sponsored insurance, this isn't abstract to me. I've navigated those costs firsthand. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

Wyoming's public lands are part of what makes this place worth protecting. They support ranching, hunting, recreation, and the rural way of life that defines us. I do not support broad efforts to sell or transfer federal public lands out of public ownership. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

Property tax policy has to reflect that different communities have different resources available and needs to fill. I believe in keeping the government as close to home as possible which means local communities should have real input into how relief is structured and how revenue needs are met. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

Wyoming's economy is strongest when the government gets out of the way and lets hardworking people build. My role as a legislator isn't to pick winners or engineer outcomes, it's to remove unnecessary barriers, protect property rights, and create conditions where entrepreneurs and small businesses can thrive. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I'm accountable to my neighbors in Sheridan, no one else. That principle shapes how I approach campaign donations. I've focused on building a grassroots campaign funded by people in this community who know me, trust me, and want a representative who answers to them. …
