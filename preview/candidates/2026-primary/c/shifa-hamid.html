Shifa Hamid

Shifa Hamid (Democratic, Cheyenne): Law clerk, City Attorney (Rawlins). Unopposed in the Aug. 18, 2026 Democratic primary for State Senate District 31.

Senate District 31 · Democratic

Primary result: Unopposed

General result: State Senate SD31, Democratic, 1,479 votes (31.8%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceCheyenne, Laramie County
OccupationLaw clerk, City Attorney (Rawlins)
Background

First-time candidate; Wyoming resident for almost 20 years (self-reported).

Websitehttps://www.laramiecountydemocrats.org/
Issue positions

Biggest issues

Education, human rights (voting, LGBTQIA+ and Indigenous rights) and climate change, addressed through legislation.

Property taxes

Does not support increasing property taxes; would raise excise taxes on alcohol and cigarettes and impose an income tax on corporations only.

Budget

Supports larger budgets for the Department of Health, Department of Family Services and the University of Wyoming.
