Background

My husband Corey and I have been fortunate to live in Goshen County for most of our lives. We have raised our family and built our businesses here among family, friends and neighbors. We have a deep appreciation for our independent rural way of life, our community and the land. Senator Steinmetz represents Goshen, Niobrara, and Weston counties. She began serving in the Wyoming State Senate in 2019. Previously, Senator Steinmetz represented House District 5 from 2015 to 2018.

Issue positions

Government overreach and regulation

Responsible regulation places limits on government not citizens, businesses and private property. Government must be simple, fair and responsive to citizens. I will work to review and repeal existing laws that over-regulate citizens, burden employees and inflate bureaucracy.

Local control of education

I will work to restore local control of education to parents, teachers and school boards.

Individual freedom and free enterprise

I will apply foundational law and principles in evaluating current law and pending legislation to ensure that individual freedom and free enterprises flourish.
