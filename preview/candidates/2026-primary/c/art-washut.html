Background

Art Washut was born in Casper, Wyoming. He earned an associate degree from Casper College in 1978, a bachelor’s degree from Chadron State College in 1978, and a master's degree in public administration from the University of Wyoming in 1994. His professional experience includes working as a police officer, police administrator, and college instructor. His organizational affiliations include the St. Anthony Manor board and the Wyoming Medical Center Foundation Heroes' Fund Committee.

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Challenges involve - workforce housing, good paying jobs and a skilled workforce. Another huge issue is aging. Also the lack of water and the condition of the highways. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

This is a complex problem that defies a simple answer. Transfering the cost to government, is a possibility but it is expensive. Relying on funding from a national government that will soon be $40 Trillion in debt is not sound sustainable. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

Selling off the public lands in a wholesale fashion is a horrible idea. There may be, however, a few parcels of public land that, after public discussion and study, might make sense to sell or transfer. We already do something similar from time to time. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

We had a proposal in 2025 that had great merit which we voted for and then reversed course. The idea was to base the correction in property taxes to the percentage of tax increases that had actually occurred in each county. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

We have over the years created legislative mandates for the Wyoming Business Council. Review all of that and retain what makes sense today and repeal the rest. The free market approach that many long for is simply no longer a reality. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

The system is fine. The individuals giving and receiving donations need to look in the mirror and do the right thing. Voters need to ask candidates during a campaign how much PAC money they are accepting and who are the folks making the big donations? …
