Eamon O'Toole

Eamon O'Toole (Republican, Baggs): Co-owner and operator of Ladder Livestock Company, a multi-generational cattle, sheep and hay ranch near Savery in the Little Snake River Valley; manages the ranch’s cattle operation; Carbon County Planning and Zoning (board service, as listed in a January 2026 congressional witness biography); Vice chair, Savery–Little Snake River Conservancy District (as of a 2021 speaker biography). Unopposed in the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 47.

House District 47 · Republican

Primary result: Unopposed

General result: State House HD47, Republican, 2,523 votes (100%), Unopposed.

Background

Little Snake River Valley resident. Ladder Livestock, operated with his parents Pat and Sharon O’Toole and sister Meghan O’Toole Lally, is a six-generation ranch near the Colorado-Wyoming border. A 2021 Farm Foundation biography says he is married to Megan and has two sons. Also listed intern work in the office of U.S. Sen. Mike Enzi and ranch-operations work at Spiegelberg Ranch.

EducationLowell Whiteman Preparatory School, high school diploma, 2004; bachelor’s degrees in geography and in environment and natural resources, University of Wyoming (Farm Foundation speaker biography; a January 2026 U.S. House witness biography lists University of Wyoming “Bachelor’s Studies, 2009”)
