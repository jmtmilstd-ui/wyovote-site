Jacob Wasserburger

Jacob Wasserburger (Republican, Cheyenne): Wyoming House District 11, 2025–Present. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 11 with 55.5% of the vote. Won the Nov. 5, 2024 general election with 54.5% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Won, 457 votes (55.5%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD11, Republican, 1,774 votes (54.5%), Won.

Research

Background

Was 32 at the November 2024 general election, a first-time legislative candidate and the nephew of former Gillette legislator Jeff Wasserburger. Cowboy State Daily reported endorsements from Gun Owners of America and the National Rifle Association.

Prior officesWyoming House District 11, 2025–Present
Positions

Property taxes, regulation and schools

Wants significant property-tax reform, including assessing property taxes at acquisition value, and to reduce overall government regulation. He also supports school choice.

Abortion and party

Described as anti-abortion but not supporting government regulation of the matter. After the 2024 general election he said he hoped to bridge the divide in the Wyoming Republican Party and was open to working with anyone to get legislation passed.
