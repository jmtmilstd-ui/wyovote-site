Mike Schmid

Mike Schmid (Republican, La Barge): President and CEO; Wyoming House District 20, 2025–Present; Wyoming House of Representatives District 20, 2025–Present. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 20 with 63.0% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · La Barge, Lincoln, Sublette Counties County

Primary result: Won, 1,755 votes (63%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD20, Republican, 4,354 votes (100%), Unopposed.
