Ken Pendergraft

Ken Pendergraft (Republican, Sheridan): Contractor; Wyoming House District 29, 2023–Present. Unopposed in the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 29. Won the Nov. 5, 2024 general election with 69.4% of the vote.
