Kevin Campbell

Kevin Campbell (Republican, Glenrock): Oilfield Services; Wyoming House District 62, 2025–Present. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 62 with 50.2% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · Glenrock, Converse, Natrona Counties County

Primary result: Won, 794 votes (50.2%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD62, Republican, 3,496 votes (100%), Unopposed.

OccupationOilfield Services
Background

Kevin Campbell's career experience includes working in oilfield services. He has served with the Jesse Martin American Legion Post #009, Wyoming Wool Growers, the Navajo-Churro Sheep Association, the Benevolent and Protective Order of Elks #1353, and the Wyoming Historical Society.

Prior officesWyoming House District 62, 2025–Present
Positions

What committee assignments would you be most interested in and why?

I would be interested in working with the revenue, appropriations, and judiciary committees. I say this because they have their fingers on the pulse of the issues concerning our people the most.

How would you prioritize capital spending/infrastructure in the state?

Roads/bridges and water infrastructure are top priorities for me. Most of our State revenue is dependent on our roadways, most notably energy and tourism. Energy, agriculture, recreation, and the citizens of Wyoming all depend on having safe well-maintained roads. Wyoming must address this sooner than later.

What is a state government issue that you think is overlooked?

Transparency in State Government spending. I believe that the taxpayer should be able to follow how their tax dollars are being spent in Cheyenne. Currently our monies are spent in an overly complicated process where money goes into a bucket, that overflows into two more buckets and a bottle, which in turn flows into a barrel and a jug. It is just nuts! We need to streamline the flow chart where the common man can understand it. Wyoming has room to reduce spending in some sectors, then they could then redirect these monies to areas where it is needed more, such as infrastructure. Transparency would highlight where this is possible.

What can the Legislature do to improve public health?

Our population is getting older. We need to work with the Aging Division of our Health Department to find best practices that ensure the quality of life for our elders and disabled. I support keeping folks in their own homes and communities through In Home Care and Respite programs. We should study what is working, and the cost associated with these programs to plan how to successfully move forward. Attached is a list of interim committee topics. Which of those do you have a specific interest in? I am interested in finding long-term property tax solutions that work for everyone. I am interested in identifying and prioritizing the needs of our state’s infrastructure. Wyoming’s mineral wealth is world class. Ever since the Uranium One deal I have been watchful of foreign interest having control over Wyoming’s resources. We need to be mindful of who we do business with, and I would be interested in learning how we deal with foreign entities concerning our energy and agricultural industries.
