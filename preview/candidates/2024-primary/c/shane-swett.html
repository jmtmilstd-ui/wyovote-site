Shane Swett

Shane Swett (Republican, Laramie): Small business owner. Unopposed in the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 13. Lost the Nov. 5, 2024 general election with 47.0% of the vote.

Republican · Laramie, Albany County

Primary result: Unopposed, 484 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD13, Republican, 1,718 votes (47%), Lost.
