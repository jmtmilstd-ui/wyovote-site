Laura Taliaferro (formerly Pearson)

Laura Taliaferro Pearson (Republican, Kemmerer): Rancher and Business Owner; Wyoming Senate District 14, 2025–Present. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State Senate District 14 with 46.8% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · Kemmerer, Lincoln, Sublette, Sweetwater, Uinta Counties County

Primary result: Won, 2,212 votes (46.8%), 1st of 3.

General result: State Senate SD14, Republican, 8,025 votes (100%), Unopposed.
