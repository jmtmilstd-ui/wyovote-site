Bob Davis

Bob Davis (Republican, Baggs): Business Owner; Wyoming House District 47, 2023–2026; Wyoming Senate District 11, 2027–Present; Wyoming National Guard, 9 years. Unopposed in the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 47. Won the Nov. 5, 2024 general election with 84.0% of the vote.

Background

Robert Davis was born in Omaha, Nebraska, and lives in Wyoming. His career experience includes working in the commercial construction business, as a public works director, as a business owner, and as a rancher. Davis volunteered as a commissioner for Carbon County, as a member of the Carbon County Development Board, on the Noyes Health Care Clinic Board, and on the Wyoming Army National Guard 133 Combat Engineers.
