Jim Anderson

Jim Anderson (Republican, Casper): Machinery product support; Wyoming Senate District 28, 2013–Present; U.S. Army, 1969–1970. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State Senate District 28 with 50.6% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · Casper, Natrona County

Primary result: Won, 1,304 votes (50.6%), 1st of 2.

General result: State Senate SD28, Republican, 5,354 votes (100%), Unopposed.

Positions

How, within the framework of a lawmaker's powers and duties, would you address the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today?

I would collaborate with the person or group that has the best idea to fix the problem

As a state lawmaker, what will guide your approach to local control? How do you determine when a decision should be left to local leaders or made by the Wyoming Legislature?

I always defer to local control if at all possible

Wyoming has wrestled for years with issues of housing, health care, education and economic development. What do you believe the state's role should be in supporting the well-being of Wyoming citizens and their families?

The state should promote economic development to improve the lives of wyoming citizens

How willing are you to compromise with legislators and other officials with different perspectives?

For 12 years I have always considered others opinions and worked with everyone to pass good legislation

How do you balance the often competing demands of wildlife and open spaces versus energy, industrial and real estate development?

There is room for everyone, I believe in multiple use for almost all land
