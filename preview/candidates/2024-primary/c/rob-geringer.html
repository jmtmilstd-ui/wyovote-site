Rob Geringer

Rob Geringer (Republican, Cheyenne): Business owner; Wyoming House District 42, 2025–Present. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 42 with 50.9% of the vote. Won the Nov. 5, 2024 general election with 79.3% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Won, 876 votes (50.9%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD42, Republican, 3,270 votes (79.3%), Won.

Background

Rob Geringer earned a bachelor's degree in civil engineering from the University of Wyoming in 1995 and a master's degree in engineering from the University of Colorado in 2000. His career experience includes working as a business owner. He has been affiliated with the Cheyenne Housing Authority Board.
