Darin M. McCann

Darin M. McCann (Republican, Rock Springs): Physician assistant; Wyoming House District 48, 2025–Present. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 48 with 55.7% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · Rock Springs, Sweetwater County

Primary result: Won, 617 votes (55.7%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD48, Republican, 2,715 votes (100%), Unopposed.
