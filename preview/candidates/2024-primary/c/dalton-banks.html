Dalton Banks

Dalton Banks (Republican, Cowley): Maintenance Operator, Rancher; Wyoming House District 26, 2023–Present. Unopposed in the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 26. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · Cowley, Big Horn, Park Counties County

Primary result: Unopposed, 1,626 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD26, Republican, 3,942 votes (100%), Unopposed.
