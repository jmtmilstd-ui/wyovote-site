Cayd Batchelor

Cayd Batchelor (Republican, Cheyenne): Railroad worker in Cheyenne (as of 2024). Lost the Aug. 20, ate House District 8 with 3.3% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 78 votes (3.3%), 3rd of 3.

Research

OccupationRailroad worker in Cheyenne (as of 2024)
Background

Cayd Batchelor is proud to announce his candidacy for the Republican nomination for Wyoming House Seat 8. A staunch conservative with a clear vision for Wyoming's future, Cayd brings a wealth of experience from his career as a railroad conductor and a deep commitment to serving the people of our great state. Through his work as a conductor for the railroad, Cayd has developed strong leadership skills and a deep understanding of logistics and operational management—skills he will leverage to effectively represent our district in the State House. Cayd's platform centers on key issues crucial to Wyoming's prosperity: education, economic growth, and upholding conservative values. …

Was 22 in June 2024. Moved to Wyoming from Georgia about 18 months earlier with his wife and two young daughters to work on the railroad in Cheyenne. He said it was his first election and that he would bring a younger perspective missing at the Capitol.

Positions

How, within the framework of a lawmaker's powers and duties, would you address the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today?

Wy's economy heavily relies on coal, natural gas, and other resources, and I would advocate for policies that support responsible extraction while balancing environmental concerns. …

As a state lawmaker, what will guide your approach to local control? How do you determine when a decision should be left to local leaders or made by the Wyoming Legislature?

I believe that decisions should be made at the lowest level of government feasible, where local leaders are most attuned to the specific needs and preferences of their constituents. …

Wyoming has wrestled for years with issues of housing, health care, education and economic development. What do you believe the state's role should be in supporting the well-being of Wyoming citizens and their families?

I believe Wyoming's role in supporting the well-being of its citizens should prioritize fostering an environment where individuals and families can thrive through self-reliance and local community support. …

How willing are you to compromise with legislators and other officials with different perspectives?

Of course there are some issues where compromise is possible but as with anything there are absolute truths that cannot be compromised. It would have to be an issue by issue basis.

How do you balance the often competing demands of wildlife and open spaces versus energy, industrial and real estate development?

I prioritize responsible stewardship of Wyoming's wildlife and open spaces while promoting energy, industrial, and real estate development essential for economic growth. This balance requires sensible regulation that safeguards environmental health without stifling innovation or job creation. …
