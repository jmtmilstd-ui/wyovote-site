Jon R. Conrad

Jon R. Conrad (Republican, Mountain View): Public Affairs Manager; Wyoming House District 19, 2023–2024; Wyoming House of Representatives District 19, 2023–2025; U.S. Air Force, 1981–2002. Lost the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 19 with 43.7% of the vote.

Republican · Mountain View, Uinta County

Primary result: Lost, 792 votes (43.7%), 2nd of 2.

Research

OccupationPublic Affairs Manager
Background

Jon Conrad was born in Findlay, Ohio. Conrad served in the U.S. Air Force from 1981 to 2002 and reached the rank of master sergeant. Conrad earned an associate degree in aircraft avionics from the Community College of the Air Force in 1995, a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering from Weber State University in 1995, and an M.B.A. from the University of Phoenix in 2002. His career experience includes working as an environmental and governmental affairs manager. Conrad has volunteered as an executive officer of the Boy Scouts of America, Troop/Pack 7798, and served as a board member of the Wyoming Economic Development Association. He has been affiliated National Rifle Association (NRA).
