Aaron Clausen

Aaron Clausen (Republican, Douglas): Fourth-generation rancher and businessman, south of Douglas on LaBonte Creek (self-described, 2016); Wyoming House of Representatives District 6, 2017–2023. Lost the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 6 with 47.6% of the vote.

Republican · Douglas, Converse County

Primary result: Lost, 1,162 votes (47.6%), 2nd of 2.

Research

OccupationFourth-generation rancher and businessman, south of Douglas on LaBonte Creek (self-described, 2016)
Background

Fourth-generation rancher and businessman who, in 2016, said he lived south of Douglas on LaBonte Creek. He wrote that he had been president of the Converse County Farm Bureau for 12 years, president of the Wyoming Ag Leadership Council for three years, and on that council’s board for six years. Served in the Wyoming House from District 6 from Jan. 10, 2017, to Jan. 10, 2023. Wikipedia lists his birthplace as Douglas, Wyoming, and his date of birth as May 30, 1977.

Prior officesWyoming House of Representatives District 6, 2017–2023
Positions

State budget (2016 campaign)

In a 2016 Livestock Roundup questionnaire he said that if elected he would focus on a state budget that provides for what’s important to Wyoming while cutting the fat, keep the business environment friendly by keeping taxes and regulations at a minimum, and protect the Wyoming way of life for generations to come.
