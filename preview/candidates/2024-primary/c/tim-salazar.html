Tim Salazar

Tim Salazar (Republican, Riverton): Wyoming House District 34, 2017–2020; Wyoming Senate District 26, 2021–Present; U.S. Army, 1989–2015. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State Senate District 26 with 60.3% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · Riverton, Fremont County

Primary result: Won, 2,626 votes (60.3%), 1st of 2.

General result: State Senate SD26, Republican, 7,266 votes (100%), Unopposed.
