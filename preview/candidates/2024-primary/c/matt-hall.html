Matt Hall

Matt Hall (Republican, Cody): Mayor of Cody (two terms / eight years as of 2024); president of the Wyoming Association of Municipalities (as of 2024); Mayor of Cody, Wyoming. Lost the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 24 with 46.7% of the vote.

Republican · Cody, Park County

Primary result: Lost, 1,233 votes (46.7%), 2nd of 2.

Research

OccupationMayor of Cody (two terms / eight years as of 2024); president of the Wyoming Association of Municipalities (as of 2024)
Background

Sitting Cody mayor (two terms / eight years as of 2024) and president of the Wyoming Association of Municipalities. This was his first campaign for the Wyoming Legislature; outgoing Rep. Sandy Newsome publicly supported his candidacy.

Prior officesMayor of Cody, Wyoming
Positions

Local control, housing and taxes

In a May 2024 interview he said his priorities included advocating for local control over certain issues, finding housing solutions, and addressing increases in property taxes. He also said he wanted to continue serving the community on statewide issues.

Budget and tourism

He touted maintaining a fiscally conservative budget while Cody’s population grew and pledged to bring the same fiscal discipline to the House. Cowboy State Daily reported he vowed to continue outgoing Rep. Sandy Newsome’s focus on outdoor recreation and tourism in Park County and Wyoming.
