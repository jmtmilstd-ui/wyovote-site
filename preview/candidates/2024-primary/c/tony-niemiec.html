Tony Niemiec

Tony Niemiec (Republican, Green River): Law enforcement; Wyoming House of Representatives District 60, 2023–2025; Wyoming House District 60, 2023–2024; U.S. Marine Corps. Lost the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 60 with 47.9% of the vote.

Republican · Green River, Sweetwater County

Primary result: Lost, 520 votes (47.9%), 2nd of 2.

Research

OccupationLaw enforcement
Background

Tony Niemiec was born in Steubenville, Ohio. Niemiec graduated from Green River High School in 1987. He served in the U.S. Marine Corps and reached the rank of Sergeant. His career experience includes working as a patrol corporal in the Sweetwater County sheriff's office and owning the Tomahawk Tavern.
