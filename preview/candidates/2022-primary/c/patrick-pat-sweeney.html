Patrick PAT" Sweeney"

Patrick PAT" Sweeney" (Republican, Casper): Self-employed; previous hotel and restaurant ownership (2022 League of Women Voters legislative directory). Wikipedia lists his profession as businessman; Wyoming House of Representatives District 58, 2017–2023. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 58 with 37.1% of the vote.

Republican · Casper, Natrona County

Primary result: Lost, 806 votes (37.1%), 2nd of 2.

Research

OccupationSelf-employed; previous hotel and restaurant ownership (2022 League of Women Voters legislative directory). Wikipedia lists his profession as businessman.
Background

Born June 16, 1954, in Jamestown, New York. The 2022 LWV legislative directory listed a B.S. in food service and hotel administration from Penn State, prior hotel/restaurant ownership, Presbyterian affiliation, and membership in the Casper Downtown Business Association and Casper Area Chamber of Commerce. He served in the House from January 2017 to January 2023.
