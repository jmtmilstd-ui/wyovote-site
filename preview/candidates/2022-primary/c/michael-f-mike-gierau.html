Michael F. 'Mike' Gierau

Michael F. 'Mike' Gierau (Democratic, Jackson): Business owner (since 1980); Wyoming House District 16, 2017–2018; Wyoming Senate District 17, 2019–Present. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Democratic primary for State Senate District 17. Won the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 62.9% of the vote.

Democratic · Jackson, Teton County

Primary result: Unopposed, 330 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State Senate SD17, Democratic, 5,142 votes (62.9%), Won.

Background

I am proud to have served the residents of Teton County since 1988, when I was appointed to the Town of Jackson Planning Commission. I served for four years on the Jackson Town Council and six years as a Teton County Commissioner. I have been fortunate enough to be a business owner here in Jackson Hole for 37 years. I am the Vice-President of Jedediah Corporation, which has been in business locally since 1980. I am also the founding chairman of Jackson Hole Air Improvement Resources. Elected positions include Wyoming Senate District 17 (2019–present) and Wyoming House District 16 (2017–2018).
