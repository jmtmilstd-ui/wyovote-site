Ogden Driskill

Ogden Driskill (Republican, Devils Tower): Rancher and Tourism; Wyoming Senate District 1, 2011–Present. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State Senate District 1 with 39.9% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.
