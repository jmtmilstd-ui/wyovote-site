Harriet Hageman

Harriet Hageman (Republican, Cheyenne): Lawyer; U.S. House Wyoming At-large District, 2023–Present. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for U.S. Representative with 66.3% of the vote. Won the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 69.8% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Won, 113,079 votes (66.3%), 1st of 5.

General result: U.S. House, Republican, 132,206 votes (69.8%), Won.

OccupationLawyer
Background

Harriet Hageman was born in Douglas, Wyoming in 1962. She earned a bachelor's degree in business administration from the University of Wyoming in 1986 and a law degree from the University of Wyoming College of Law in 1989. Hageman's career experience includes working as a law clerk for federal appeals judge James E. Barrett, as a lawyer, and as an adjunct professor. She co-founded the Wyoming Conservation Alliance.

Positions

Do you support or oppose federal legislation to reduce dependency on extractive energy industries? Please explain.

I oppose any such efforts to reduce our ability to explore for, produce, use, and export our oil and gas, coal, uranium, trona and other resources. Wyoming is the largest coal producer in the nation, with over 1.6 trillion tons in our state alone. It is clean, accessible, affordable, and makes our lives better in terms of providing affordable energy. Our fossil fuels are the foundation of our prosperity, a fact that is proven on a daily basis as Biden's failed energy policy results in ever-increasing inflation, catastrophic problems with our supply chain, and shortages of basic goods. Energy security is national security. It is not the government's role to adopt policies that are intended to increase the cost of energy.
