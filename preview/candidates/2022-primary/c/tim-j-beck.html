Tim J. Beck

Tim J. Beck (Republican, Lovell): Rancher; owns and operates Double Dollar Cattle Company south of Lovell (Black Angus cattle and Boer goats). Earlier work included masonry and independent contracting, and helping start the family-operated Country Bakery just south of Lovell; Big Horn County Weed and Pest Board; Big Horn County Farm Bureau board / county president and Northwest District chairman, Wyoming Farm Bureau Federation. Lost the Aug. 16, ate House District 26 with 30.6% of the vote.

Republican · Lovell, Big Horn County

Primary result: Lost, 882 votes (30.6%), 2nd of 4.

Background

A Big Horn County native, Beck grew up in Basin, served an LDS mission in Alaska, and moved to Lovell in 1992 with his wife Lorilyn. After masonry and contracting work and helping start Country Bakery, he took over the family ranch about 15 years before the 2022 race. He also cited about 30 years with Big Horn County Search and Rescue, a prior term as a write-in GOP precinct committeeman, and a college basketball path that included Northwest College, a short stay at BYU, Valley City State (including the NAIA tournament), and a coaching stint at SUNY Cobleskill before returning home.

EducationBasin High School, 1980; attended Northwest College (then Northwest Community College) and briefly Brigham Young University; B.A. in education, Valley City State University (North Dakota); master’s in education, Wayne State College (Nebraska).
Prior officesBig Horn County Weed and Pest Board; Big Horn County Farm Bureau board / county president and Northwest District chairman, Wyoming Farm Bureau Federation
Positions

Agriculture and representation

In his March 2022 announcement he said Big Horn County needed an agricultural voice in Cheyenne, emphasized that a representative should have been raised among the people represented, and described himself as conservative but wary of “group think,” preferring “consensus” over “compromise.” Citing advice from former Sen. Ray Peterson, he said he would not pre-commit to legislation without reading the details.
