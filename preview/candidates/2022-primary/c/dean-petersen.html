Dean Petersen

Dean Petersen (Republican, Cheyenne): Filmmaker; U.S. Army, 2011–2014. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 9 with 22.0% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 489 votes (22%), 3rd of 3.

OccupationFilmmaker
Background

Dean Petersen served in the U.S. Army from 2011 to 2014. Petersen earned a bachelor's degree from the University of Utah in 2004 and a graduate degree from Regis University in 2018. Petersen's career experience includes working as a filmmaker.
