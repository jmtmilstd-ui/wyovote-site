James Scott Quick

James Scott Quick (Republican, Douglas): Business owner; U.S. Marine Corps. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for Governor with 2.9% of the vote.

Republican · Douglas, Converse County

Primary result: Lost, 4,728 votes (2.9%), 4th of 4.

Research

OccupationBusiness owner
Background

James Quick graduated from Douglas High School in 1983. Quick served in the U.S. Marine Corps. His career experience includes owning an oilfield service company.

After graduating from Douglas High School, I went into the Marines. I chose to come back to Wyoming after my honorable discharge. I'm deeply rooted in the state of Wyoming. I've worked in the energy business--oil, uranium, coal, pipeline and I've owned my business for 14 years and am still working. The last few years have been hard on everybody. I feel we've lost our voice in Wyoming. I refused to be silenced anymore. We need someone to step up and be the voice for the people.

EducationHigh school: Douglas High School, 1983
MilitaryService / branch: U.S. Marine Corps
Positions

What specific measures would you propose to enhance state revenues?

We need to open our drilling back up. We need to fight DC on that and we need to fight more to get the coal out Let's cut back some of the regulations; that will invited good businesses to come to Wyoming.

How would you work with the legislature to prepare Wyoming for wildfires and drought?

We need to open our logging industries; that will help manage our forests better and increase our revenue. We need to protect our water rights at all costs. Let's just implement good common sense suggestions and work together. If legislators have good ideas for the people of Wyoming, let's get it done.

What are your top two issues for the Wyoming government to address in the next four years?

The first thing I going to do is get rid of the reservation only system on our state parks. I want to keep 10-15 percent of the spots that can be reserved for tourists. I want the reservations taken in-state. I wanted to protect the jobs we have in Wyoming first and worry about new jobs second. I want to keep the coal fired power plants open. Could Wyoming citizens form a co-op to take them over and keep them open (that was my wife's and other We need to open u our forest an)? I'm not against nuclear power, but a lot more is not being shared with the general public.
