Rex Rammell

Rex Rammell (Republican, Rock Springs): Veterinarian. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for Governor with 5.7% of the vote.

Republican · Rock Springs, Sweetwater County

Primary result: Lost, 9,378 votes (5.7%), 3rd of 4.

Research

OccupationVeterinarian
Background

Rex Rammell graduated from Teton High School in 1979. Rammell earned a doctorate in veterinary medicine from Kansas State University in 1989. His career experience includes working as a veterinarian with Wyoming Veterinary Center, a rancher, and an author.

Conservative constitutionalist, veterinarian small business owner, rancher, sportsman, faith based family man.

EducationOther: Kansas State University, 1989
Positions

What specific measures would you propose to enhance state revenues?

State control over all our natural resources.

How would you work with the legislature to prepare Wyoming for wildfires and drought?

Appropriate money to prepare for fires and advocate for fire prevention measures like controlled burns.

What are your top two issues for the Wyoming government to address in the next four years?

State sovereignty over all our natural resources and education reform.
