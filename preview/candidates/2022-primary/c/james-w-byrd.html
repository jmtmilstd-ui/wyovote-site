James W. Byrd

James W. Byrd (Democratic, Cheyenne): Geologist; Wyoming House of Representatives District 44, 2009–2019. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Democratic primary for State House District 11 with 35.7% of the vote.

Democratic · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 90 votes (35.7%), 2nd of 2.

Research

OccupationGeologist
Background

James Byrd earned a high school diploma from Cheyenne Central High School and attended both Laramie County Community College and the University of Wyoming. Byrd worked in the following positions: Security Consultant, Computer Network Design, Exploration Geophysicist for the Compagnie Générale de Géophysique and American Oil Company (British Petroleum/Amoco), Contract Computer Project Manager for Communication Systems Interactive, and Software for American Express, Cargill, and Network Assessment and Optimization Group.
