Sarah Butters

Sarah Butters (Democratic, Mountain View): Teacher in Uinta County School District #4, Mountain View (described in a 2023 University of Wyoming release as teaching K-8; a Mountain View Elementary faculty page describes her as a longtime third-grade teacher who also coaches middle-school volleyball, girls basketball, and track). Unopposed in the Aug. 16, ate House District 19. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 17.6% of the vote.

Democratic · Mountain View, Uinta County

Primary result: Unopposed, 72 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD19, Democratic, 374 votes (17.6%), Lost.

Background

The 2022 SOS roster listed Sarah Jessica Butters of Mountain View as the Democratic nominee for HD19. A 2023 UW Teacher-Mentor Corps release named Sarah Butters as teaching K-8 in Uinta County School District 4 in Mountain View, and a Mountain View Elementary faculty page (which does not mention the campaign) says she was in her 15th year teaching there, starting in kindergarten and then third grade, with a UW bachelor’s in elementary education, a master’s in curriculum and instruction/educational leadership, a UW reading/literacy endorsement, and coaching duties. Contact details on the faculty page were not stored.

EducationB.A. in elementary education, University of Wyoming; master’s in curriculum and instruction / educational leadership; additional endorsement in reading and literacy from the University of Wyoming (Mountain View Elementary faculty biography).
