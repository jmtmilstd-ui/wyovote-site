Jordan Evans (Democratic, Cheyenne): Government. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Democratic primary for State House District 7. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 38.6% of the vote.

Democratic · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Unopposed, 163 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD07, Democratic, 1,282 votes (38.6%), Lost.

Background

Jordan Evans was born in Cheyenne, Wyoming. He earned a bachelor's degree from the University of Wyoming in 2011. His career experience includes working as a government mapmaker. He has been affiliated with Laramie County Democratic Party and Miles of Smiles Wyoming.
