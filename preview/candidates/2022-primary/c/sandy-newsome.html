Sandy Newsome

Sandy Newsome (Republican, Cody): Business owner (Sunlight Sports in Cody, with her husband Bob) for over 30 years; previously a meat inspector for the Wyoming Department of Agriculture serving the Big Horn Basin; Wyoming House of Representatives District 24, 2019–2025. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 24 with 51.2% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.

Republican · Cody, Park County

Primary result: Won, 1,810 votes (51.2%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD24, Republican, 2,809 votes (100%), Unopposed.

Background

Sandy moved to Cody in 1982 after graduating from Colorado State University with a B.S. in Animal Science. She was hired by the Wyoming Department of Agriculture as the meat inspector serving the Big Horn Basin. In 1983 Sandy met her husband, Bob, and they married in 1989. At that time, Sandy joined Bob full time at Sunlight Sports, the outdoor store that he started in 1971. As a business owner in Cody for over 30 years, Sandy has worked on budgets, financial projects and personnel issues. She was appointed twice by Governor Mead to statewide boards, serving on the Wyoming Tourism Board from 2011 to 2017 and as Chairman in 2016, and on the Commission of Judicial Conduct and Ethics.
