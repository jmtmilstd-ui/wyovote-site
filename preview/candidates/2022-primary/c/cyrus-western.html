Cyrus Western

Cyrus Western (Republican, Big Horn): Wyoming House of Representatives District 51, 2019–2025. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 51 with 51.8% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.

Republican · Big Horn, Sheridan County

Primary result: Won, 2,010 votes (51.8%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD51, Republican, 3,631 votes (100%), Unopposed.
