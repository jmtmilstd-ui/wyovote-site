Don Odom

Don Odom (Republican, Cheyenne): Professional truck driver in the oilfields of Wyoming. Lost the Aug. 16, ate House District 61 with 25.2% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 336 votes (25.2%), 3rd of 3.

Research

OccupationProfessional truck driver in the oilfields of Wyoming
Background

I'm a professional truckdriver in the oilfields of Wyoming. I believe more blue collar average Americans need to get involved in our government. I've been a conservative since 1980 when I voted for Ronald Reagan in my first election. I intend to serve for We The People, not we the politicians, not we the government, and not even we the Republican party. My motto is: Say what you mean and mean what you say.

Positions

Rights, school choice, and individual freedom

I am passionate about protecting 2nd amendment rights, the life of the unborn, school choice, and individual freedom.

Property taxes, income tax, and government overreach

I support reforming the Wyoming property tax system, and resisting any effort to pass a state income tax, government overreach, and mandates of any kind.
