Liz Cheney

Liz Cheney (Republican, Wilson): Attorney; U.S. House Wyoming At-large District, 2017–2023. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for U.S. Representative with 28.9% of the vote.

Republican · Wilson, Teton County

Primary result: Lost, 49,339 votes (28.9%), 2nd of 5.

Research

OccupationAttorney
Background

Cheney earned a B.A. from Colorado College and a J.D. from the University of Chicago Law School. She practiced law and later served at the U.S. State Department as a deputy assistant secretary of state for the Middle East. She has worked as a Fox News analyst and co-authored a book called Exceptional: Why the World Needs a Powerful America with her father, former Vice President Dick Cheney (R). She was first elected to represent Wyoming’s At-Large Congressional District in 2016.
