Marcie Kindred

Marcie Kindred (Democratic, Cheyenne): Real estate professional. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Democratic primary for State Senate District 7. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 42.9% of the vote.

Democratic · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Unopposed, 364 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State Senate SD07, Democratic, 2,437 votes (42.9%), Lost.

Research

OccupationReal estate professional
Background

My roots run deep through the intermountain west: Born in Idaho, graduated high school in Utah, chose to raise a family in the beautiful square-state of Wyoming. My love for my community and serving the people in it led me to pursue a fulfilling career in real estate. When I’m not playing home matchmaker, you can find my husband Bo and me working on our dream “fixer-upper” home in the Avenues of SD7. Our four children (all boys) ensure our home is filled with laughter, joy, and many impromptu wrestling matches.

EducationGraduated high school in Utah
Positions

Public service and compassionate leadership

Politics is a public service. I am here to serve my community. Politics can unite, rather than divide when we show up, work hard, and fight for one another. Compassionate, effective leadership is grounded in our shared values, not party or partisanship.

Economy, health care, housing, and education

I will represent the people of SD7 by fostering bi-partisan relationships to bolster our economy, improve access to healthcare & affordable housing, and strengthen our education system in ways that honor and sustain our Wyoming way of life.
