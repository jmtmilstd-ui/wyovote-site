Drew Perkins

Drew Perkins (Republican, Casper): Attorney; Wyoming State Senate District 29, 2007–2022. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State Senate District 29 with 47.2% of the vote.

Republican · Casper, Natrona County

Primary result: Lost, 2,531 votes (47.2%), 2nd of 2.

Research

OccupationAttorney
Background

Perkins earned his B.S. in accounting from Brigham Young University in 1980. He then earned his M.S. in taxation from Southeastern University in 1983. In 1992, he earned his J.D. from the University of Wyoming. After earning his degree, Perkins worked as chief financial officer/controller for Satron Corporation from 1980 to 1989. He worked for Brown, Drew, Massey as an attorney from 1992 to 1996. Perkins has worked as an attorney for Perkins and Powers Professional Corporation since 1997. Perkins previously was vice chair/commissioner for Natrona County Commission from 2002 to 2004. He also served as chair/commissioner for Natrona County Commission from 2003 to 2006.
