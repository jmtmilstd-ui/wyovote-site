Steve Duerr

Steve Duerr (Republican, Moran): Jackson attorney; general counsel for Lower Valley Energy (30-plus years as of 2022); former executive director of the Jackson Hole Chamber of Commerce (1999–2006) and of the Murie Center (2008–2012). Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State Senate District 17. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 32.0% of the vote.

Republican · Moran, Teton County

Primary result: Unopposed, 3,313 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State Senate SD17, Republican, 2,615 votes (32%), Lost.

Background

Moved to Jackson Hole from Minnesota with his family in 1985. As of his 2022 announcement he had practiced law for 42 years, served as general counsel for Lower Valley Energy for more than 30 years, and previously led the Jackson Hole Chamber of Commerce (1999–2006) and the Murie Center (2008–2012). Civic roles listed in the announcement included founding board member of the Jackson Hole Center for Global Affairs, 30-year Jackson Hole Rotary member, co-chair of Community Entry Services and court-appointed guardian, hospice volunteer at St. John’s Hospital, and board member of the Curran-Seeley Foundation.

Positions

Growth, infrastructure and Teton County’s voice

In his April 2022 announcement he said Teton County was “at a tipping point,” with commercial and tourism growth and public-revenue challenges straining infrastructure, and that the county must “invest in solutions that will balance growth with conserving the resources that make Teton County a destination.” He argued Teton County needs a seat at the table in Cheyenne, saying bills out of the House had hurt local residents, and called for “thoughtful, respectful Republican leadership on the important committees.”
