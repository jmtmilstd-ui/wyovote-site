Rick Coppinger

Rick Coppinger (Republican, Cheyenne): In a 2020 WTE candidate questionnaire he listed his profession as facilities specialist for Spectrum Communications. A 2024 mayoral candidate capsule listed work managing three stations for Great Lakes Airlines, property management for a communications company, a Cellular One startup, and a leadership role in aviation with the U.S. Navy; U.S. Navy; a 2024 WTE mayoral capsule described a leadership role in aviation with the Navy, and his 2020 questionnaire said he returned to Cheyenne after serving his country in the Navy. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 7 with 43.2% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 1,151 votes (43.2%), 2nd of 2.

Background

The same Cheyenne resident later completed a 2020 WTE municipal questionnaire (facilities specialist for Spectrum Communications; Central High; Navy veteran; described himself as a conservative, constitutionalist, and Christian) and appeared in a 2024 mayoral guide (age 63; some LCCC coursework in international banking and finance; Leadership Cheyenne 2004; Great Lakes Airlines station management and communications-company property management). Those later questionnaires’ city-office issue answers were not applied as 2022 House positions. A 2022 WTE Laramie County preview reported that he did not comment and did not have a campaign website for the HD7 race.

EducationCheyenne Central High School (2020 WTE questionnaire). A 2024 WTE mayoral capsule added some college courses in international banking and finance at Laramie County Community College and graduation from the Leadership Cheyenne program in 2004.
MilitaryU.S. Navy; a 2024 WTE mayoral capsule described a leadership role in aviation with the Navy, and his 2020 questionnaire said he returned to Cheyenne after serving his country in the Navy.
