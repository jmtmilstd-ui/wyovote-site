Wayne B. Pinch

Wayne B. Pinch (Republican, Laramie): Inventor and entrepreneur. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 13. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 40.0% of the vote.

Republican · Laramie, Albany County

Primary result: Unopposed, 938 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD13, Republican, 933 votes (40%), Lost.

Research

OccupationInventor and entrepreneur
Background

I’m an inventor and entrepreneur. I’ve been a student of politics for a long time. I have the ability to bring people together which I believe is needed right now.

Positions

HOW WOULD YOU MEET THE NEEDS OF WYOMING’S AGING POPULATION WHILE MAINTAINING ADEQUATE GOVERNMENT SERVICES FOR ALL AGES?

I think our public transportation is inadequate, so work on that. We have nice facilities that offer fitness and activities but they don’t do much good if you can’t get there.

WHAT ARE YOUR PRIORITIES IN RESPONDING TO CATASTROPHIC DISASTERS AFFECTING OUR STATE?

Hold the Feds feet to the fire. Water and energy could be disasters, stop giving ours away.

DO YOU SUPPORT WYOMING’S TRIGGER LAW REGARDING ABORTION? WHY OR WHY NOT?

No, I believe in a woman’s right to choose in the first trimester.

What, if anything, would you support to improve reasonable access to health care in Wyoming?

I am for Medicare for all. Wyoming can access more federal funds. I would also look at Utah as an alternative approach.

What, if any, specific measures would you propose to generate future state revenue?

As the nation shifts away from fosil fuels we need to reevaluate our stance on what our energy is worth to others. I would entertain tolls. I would entertain a tenant tax. We deficit spend on public services, why?

Do you agree or disagree with this statement: “Current school funding is adequate.” Please explain your answer.

Neither Agree nor Disagree. We are 2nd in the nation on dollars spent per student. We are 30th in discipline.
