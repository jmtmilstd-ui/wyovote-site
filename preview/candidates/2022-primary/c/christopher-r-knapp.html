Christopher R. Knapp

Christopher R. Knapp (Republican, Gillette): Business owner; Wyoming House District 53, 2020–Present; Campbell County Commissioner. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 53. Won the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 85.5% of the vote.

Republican · Gillette, Campbell County

Primary result: Unopposed, 1,534 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD53, Republican, 1,647 votes (85.5%), Won.

Background

Christopher Knapp's career experience includes owning a business.

I am a fourth generation Wyoming resident. Occupation: Managing Member/ Rocky Mountain Business Equipment LLC 20 years; President/ VISTA Leasing Co. Inc. 20 years. The 12 years serving as Campbell County Commissioner really prepared me to learn a wide range of issues. I helped to create reserve accounts, depreciation and maintenance set aside for every County building and create fleet programs to ensure emergency equipment replacement and tracking.

Prior officesWyoming House District 53, 2020–Present; Campbell County Commissioner
Positions

What is something you would like to change if elected?

I would support legislation for medical freedom, including privacy of medical information and that the right to treatment is between the individual and the doctor as supported by the Wyoming Constitution. No medication or treatment should be mandated or restricted by government unless it is formally banned as an illegal substance. I would also support freedom of energy use and commerce, and protection of 2nd Amendment rights.
