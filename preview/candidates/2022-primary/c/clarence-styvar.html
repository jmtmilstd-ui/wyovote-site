Clarence Styvar

Clarence Styvar (Republican, Cheyenne): Railroad supervisor; Wyoming House District 12, 2018–Present; U.S. Army veteran, 10 years as an infantryman, rank of staff sergeant; Bronze Star recipient (self-reported). Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 12 with 66.9% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Won, 831 votes (66.9%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD12, Republican, 1,377 votes (100%), Unopposed.

OccupationRailroad supervisor
Background

Raised in southwest Wyoming; lifetime Wyoming resident. Lived in House District 12 for 16 years as of 2020. Lifetime member of the VFW; former VFW State Chief of Staff and District 5 commander (self-reported). Father of a special-needs child; has advocated on IEP, 504, and health-care issues. Age 53 as of a May 2018 profile.

EducationLyman High School; various United States Army schools; some college
MilitaryU.S. Army veteran, 10 years as an infantryman, rank of staff sergeant; Bronze Star recipient (self-reported)
Prior officesWyoming House District 12, 2018–Present
Positions

Taxes and budget (2020 questionnaire)

No new taxes; live within the state’s means; fiscal responsibility, accountability and transparency; look at cutting administrative costs and restructuring government without raising taxes.

Economy and natural resources (2020 questionnaire)

Protect and support coal, mining, agriculture and tourism; reduce and update regulations holding back industry; support getting products to buyers, including coal shipments overseas; economic development including technology, manufacturing and hospitality.

Health and human services (2020 questionnaire)

Improve care, supports and services for the elderly and for citizens with developmental and mental-health needs.

Social issues (2020 questionnaire)

Pro-life; preserve rights under the U.S. and Wyoming constitutions.
