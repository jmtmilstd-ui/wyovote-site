Lynn Hutchings

Lynn Hutchings (Republican, Cheyenne): Retired Military-42 ½ years’ service in the Air National Guard; Wyoming House District 42, 2013–2014; Wyoming Senate District 5, 2019–Present; U.S. Air National Guard. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State Senate District 5. Won the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 70.8% of the vote.
