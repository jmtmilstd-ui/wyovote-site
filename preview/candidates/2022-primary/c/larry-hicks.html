Larry Hicks

Larry Hicks (Republican, Baggs): Natural Resources Manager; Wyoming Senate District 11, 2011–Present. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, ate Senate District 11. Won the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 79.0% of the vote.

Republican · Baggs, Carbon, Sweetwater Counties County

Primary result: Unopposed, 3,694 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State Senate SD11, Republican, 4,153 votes (79%), Won.

Research

OccupationNatural Resources Manager
Background

Hicks studied crop science at the University of Wyoming, earning a bachelor of science, and went on to earn an M.S. in Agronomy from Montana State University. He later returned to the University of Wyoming where he completed Ph.D. coursework in Agronomy. Hicks worked as a natural resources coordinator for the Little Snake River Conservation District.

EducationBachelor's: University of Wyoming, 1983; Ph.D: University of Wyoming; Graduate: Montana State University, 1986
