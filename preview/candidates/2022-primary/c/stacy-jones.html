Stacy Jones

Stacy Jones (Republican, Rock Springs): Realtor; Wyoming Senate District 13, 2023–Present. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State Senate District 13 with 67.9% of the vote. Won the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 76.0% of the vote.

Republican · Rock Springs, Sweetwater County

General result: State Senate SD13, Republican, 3,859 votes (76%), Won.

Background

Stacy Jones was born in Laramie, Wyoming, and lives in Wyoming. Jones earned a bachelor's degree in elementary education from the University of Wyoming. Her career experience includes working as a realtor. Jones joined the National Rifle Association (NRA), becoming a lifetime member. She also served as vice president of the Rock Springs Urban Renewal Agency board, treasurer of the Sweetwater One Public School Foundation board, and member of the Rock Springs Chamber of Commerce board.
