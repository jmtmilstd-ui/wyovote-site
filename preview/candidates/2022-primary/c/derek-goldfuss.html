Derek Goldfuss

Derek Goldfuss (Republican, Cheyenne). Lost the Aug. 16, ate House District 12 with 33.1% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 412 votes (33.1%), 2nd of 2.

Research

Background

A May ory identified Derek Goldfuss as president of LCCC’s student government, a Burns Junior/Senior High graduate who returned to college after finding work and daily life unfulfilling, and a udying molecular biology. He received the president’s plaque for service to the college and mentioned work on the Pathfinder Building dedication, the Ludden Library reopening, and student-club budgets.

EducationBurns Junior/Senior High School; Laramie County Community College graduate, class of 2019 (served as student-government president). At commencement he said he would continue his education in molecular biology; a later completed degree in that field was not confirmed in that article.
Positions

Second Amendment and education

At a July 2022 Politics in the Park forum, Cap City News reported that Goldfuss said he wanted to focus primarily on preserving Second Amendment rights and helping improve education in the state.
