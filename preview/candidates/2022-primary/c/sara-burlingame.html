Sara Burlingame

Sara Burlingame (Democratic, Cheyenne): Wyoming House of Representatives District 44, 2019–2021. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Democratic primary for State House District 44. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 39.7% of the vote.

Democratic · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Unopposed, 166 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD44, Democratic, 616 votes (39.7%), Lost.

Research

Background

In a 2022 candidate Q&A she was 47 and listed prior service in the Wyoming House from District 44 in 2018–19. She described a record of building bipartisan working-class coalitions and emphasized investment in South Cheyenne.

EducationSome college (self-described, 2022)
Prior officesWyoming House of Representatives District 44, 2019–2021
Positions

Education, public lands and the South Side

Priorities were fully funding public education, maintaining access to public lands, and bringing investment to Cheyenne’s South Side. She said schools were not adequately funded and that Wyoming needs to diversify revenue outside of energy.

Health care and Medicaid

Said she was passionate about passing Medicaid expansion, which she argued would get people back to work, drive down preventive-care costs, and had overwhelming support in House District 44. She also said the state had not adequately funded mental health, particularly suicide prevention.

Abortion and LGBTQ rights

Said the choice to terminate a pregnancy is a personal decision that should be made with family and medical providers, not the Legislature. She supported nondiscrimination protections for LGBTQ residents, calling them consistent with the Wyoming Constitution and an economic imperative.

Energy and climate

Said she is committed to supporting coal, natural gas and oil as well as wind and solar. She wrote that climate change is a fact and that the state can develop alternative energy without abandoning workers or fuels still crucial to the economy.
