Background

Landen earned his A.A. from Casper College. He then earned his B.S. from the University of Wyoming. He went on to earn his M.P.A. from the University of Wyoming. Landen has worked as Associate Vice-President for Student Services at Casper College.

A 2007 Wyoming Legislature member profile listed his occupation as associate vice-president for student services at Casper College, his place of birth as Riverton, his family as spouse Robyn and three children, and additional graduate study at Colorado State University alongside an A.A. from Casper College and B.S. and M.P.A. degrees from the University of Wyoming. Ballotpedia likewise describes him as having worked as associate vice-president for student services at Casper College.
