Robert Bob" Davis"

Robert Bob" Davis" (Republican, Baggs): Business Owner; Wyoming House District 47, 2023–2026; Wyoming Senate District 11, 2027–Present; Wyoming National Guard, 9 years. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 47 with 57.5% of the vote. Won the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 80.1% of the vote.

Republican · Baggs, Carbon, Sweetwater Counties County

Primary result: Won, 1,447 votes (57.5%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD47, Republican, 2,636 votes (80.1%), Won.

Background

Robert Davis was born in Omaha, Nebraska, and lives in Wyoming. His career experience includes working in the commercial construction business, as a public works director, as a business owner, and as a rancher. Davis volunteered as a commissioner for Carbon County, as a member of the Carbon County Development Board, on the Noyes Health Care Clinic Board, and on the Wyoming Army National Guard 133 Combat Engineers.
