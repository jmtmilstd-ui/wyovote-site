Privacy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

WyoVote is a static website. You do not need an account to use it, and WyoVote does not ask you to log in.

Browsing the site

WyoVote does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not use advertising networks. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, may set a technically necessary security cookie on some visits. If that occurs, WyoVote does not use it to identify you or to show ads.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most hosts, Cloudflare processes the technical information needed to deliver the pages, such as IP address and browser type, in server logs. That processing is described in Cloudflare’s privacy policy. WyoVote does not use those logs to identify visitors.

Analytics

WyoVote uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate traffic statistics, such as page views. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics is cookie-free: it does not collect or use visitors’ personal data, and it does not use its measurements to track people from site to site. See Cloudflare’s About Web Analytics and data collection notes.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites, or to documents hosted elsewhere. Those sites have their own privacy practices. WyoVote is not responsible for them.

Contact

If you send a message through the Contact page, the form is delivered to WyoVote’s inbox. WyoVote uses your name, email address, subject, and message only to reply. Messages are not shared or sold, and they are not used for marketing.

The form is processed by Web3Forms, which emails the submission to WyoVote and may keep a copy in its dashboard. See the Web3Forms privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be posted on this page and the effective date will be updated.

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Contact page.
