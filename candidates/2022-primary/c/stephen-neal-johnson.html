Stephen Neal Johnson

Profile · 2022 Primary

Stephen Neal Johnson (Republican, Cheyenne): General contractor; Wyoming House District 8, 2025–Present; Wyoming House of Representatives District 8, 2025–Present. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 8 with 19.3% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost, 693 votes (19.3%), 3rd of 4.
