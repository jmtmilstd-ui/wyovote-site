Lee Ann Stephenson (Democratic, Riverside). Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Democratic primary for State House District 47. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 19.9% of the vote.

Democratic · Riverside, Carbon County

Primary result: Unopposed, 129 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD47, Democratic, 656 votes (19.9%), Lost.

Research

Background

Lee Ann Stephenson earned an associate degree from Laramie County Community College in 1987 and a bachelor's degree from the University of Wyoming in 1989. Her professional experience includes co-owning a seasonal campground in Riverside, Wyoming. She served on the Carbon County Senior Services Board for 10 years and was appointed to the state senior services board, where she served for 3 years. Stephenson has also served as the secretary/treasurer of the Sierra Madre Water and Sewer Joint Powers Board and the Carbon County Specific Purpose Tax Joint Powers Board.
