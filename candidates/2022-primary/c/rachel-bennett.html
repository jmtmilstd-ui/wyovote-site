Rachel Bennett

Profile · 2022 Primary

Rachel Bennett (Republican, Cheyenne): Wyoming National Guard (the Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported she had previously worked at Wyoming Army National Guard Joint Force Headquarters and was a Guard veteran). Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State Senate District 7 with 30.6% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost, 1,283 votes (30.6%), 2nd of 3.

Research

ResidenceCheyenne, Laramie County
Background

Had spent the previous 20 years as a Wyoming resident at the time of the 2022 Senate District 7 race. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle published a photograph of her at Wyoming Army National Guard Joint Force Headquarters, where it said she previously worked, and described her as a Guard veteran.

MilitaryWyoming National Guard (the Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported she had previously worked at Wyoming Army National Guard Joint Force Headquarters and was a Guard veteran)
Websitehttps://www.rachelforwyoming.com/ (leaves WyoVote)
Issue positions

Guns, public lands and government

The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported she is pro-Second Amendment, supports hunting and fishing rights and transparent government, and is opposed to federal overreach. The paper also reported she said she had made efforts to address wrongdoing in the Guard.

2022 primary resultLost, 1,283 votes (30.6%), 2nd of 3.
