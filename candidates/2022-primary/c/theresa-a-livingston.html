Theresa A. Livingston

Theresa A. Livingston (Democratic, Worland): Paraprofessional; U.S. Air Force, 1972–1981. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Democratic primary for Governor with 71.2% of the vote. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 16.8% of the vote.

Democratic · Worland, Washakie County

Primary result: Won, 4,993 votes (71.2%), 1st of 2.

General result: Governor, Democratic, 30,686 votes (16.8%), Lost.

Research

OccupationParaprofessional
Background

Theresa Livingston served in the U.S. Air Force from 1972 to 1981. Livingston's career experience includes working as a paraprofessional in special education. She has served as a member of Team Rubicon since 2015.

72 years of being a human on this earth. 28 years of that time is residing in Wyoming including Lander, Cheyenne, & Worland. I have visited most of the state during those years. I served in the USAF for 9 years living in Germany, Turkey, & Spain. I also lived in other states. As a teenager, I lived in Taiwan with my parents. My dad was in civil service. He was in the Army/air corps. WW2. I worked for the DOI/BLM for 15 years so I have a background in land management. Being a Para in Special Education gives me a good background in Education. I often bring a different perspective to life from living in so many places.

Age 72 as of the September 2022 WTE questionnaire. Said she had previously run for the State Senate and worked to encourage young people to vote and get involved.

EducationA.A. degree; USAF community college; U.S. Air Force basic training, supervisor training and leadership school; USAF and Midwestern University Medical Laboratory school; continuing education in many subjects (self-reported, 2022)
MilitaryService / branch: U.S. Air Force; Years of service: 1972 - 1981
Positions

What specific measures would you propose to enhance state revenues?

Outdoor recreation that brings in revenue. It's working already. tourism is up. Also, rare minerals could bring in revenue. Get the younger generation to help up with ideas to bring in revenue. They are amazing.

How would you work with the legislature to prepare Wyoming for wildfires and drought?

I worked in wildfire for the BLM so I have some knowledge of the process. It was wonderful to see how all the agencies state, county, & federal work together during fires. They also work together to prevent fires through prescribed burns & other methodologies. The ties between all the fire agencies working together are amazing. I was there to get them what they needed. Being that closed to the fire was endless education. The legislature needs to keep up the funding they give to the fire programs. They also should work on educating the citizens of Wyoming to understand what is being done especially

What are your top two issues for the Wyoming government to address in the next four years? Rex Wilde

Health care for women without restrictions. Medicare expansion. I want to include better mental health care. Healthy people make for a healthy state. This is good for everyone. The second issue is project-based education with an emphasis on aptitude testing. We need to help students find their “WHY” so they will be happy & productive in life. I think this will make for happy teachers which will be a great out come for all.

Why running (2022)

Ran because no one was running for women’s reproductive rights, and for Medicaid expansion to help keep hospital services open and provide mental health care.

Medicaid expansion

Supports Medicaid expansion, saying it will help hospitals stay solvent and keep services, including maternity wards.

Abortion

No restrictions on women’s health care of any kind (self-described).

LGBTQ residents

Supports protecting the rights of LGBTQ residents and broader acceptance, saying many young people leave the state because of fear and because it is too conservative.
