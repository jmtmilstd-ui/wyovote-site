Larry Williamson

Profile · 2022 Primary

Larry Williamson (Constitution). Lost the November 8, 2022 general election for State House HD53 with 279 votes (14.5%).

General result: State House HD53, Constitution, 279 votes (14.5%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceGillette, Campbell County
OccupationHighway construction (laborer, operator, quality control, and plant manager, since 1998)
Background

Larry Williamson ran as the Constitution Party nominee for House District 53 in 2022 and lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election. His Secretary of State general-election filing listed a Gillette mailing address.

In an Oct. 19, 2022 County 17 candidate questionnaire, Williamson said he was 43 years old, had worked in highway construction since 1998 as a laborer, operator, in quality control, and as a plant manager, and that his education consisted of job-specific certifications and life experience. He wrote that he was originally from Georgia.
