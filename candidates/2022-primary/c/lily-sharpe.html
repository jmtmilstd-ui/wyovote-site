Lily Sharpe

Lily Sharpe (Republican, Cheyenne): Attorney; served as staff attorney for the Wyoming Attorney General’s Office, the U.S. District Court, and the Wyoming Legislature, and as State Court Administrator for the Wyoming Supreme Court. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 8 with 28.4% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 1,021 votes (28.4%), 2nd of 4.

Background

Lily grew up in Lander, Wyoming working on the family ranch. She attended high school in Lander. After high school, Lily received a bachelor’s degree from the University of Wyoming. She went on to receive her law degree from Notre Dame Law School. At Notre Dame, she met her husband, Steve Sharpe. Lily has enjoyed an extensive career in the law. She served as a staff attorney for the Wyoming Attorney General’s Office, the U.S. District Court, and the Wyoming Legislature. Lily also served as the State Court Administrator for the Wyoming Supreme Court where she oversaw the transformation of the Wyoming Courts to digital courts.

EducationAttended high school in Lander; bachelor’s degree from the University of Wyoming; law degree from Notre Dame Law School
Positions

Priorities for Cheyenne, Laramie County and Wyoming

A vibrant economy for Cheyenne, Laramie County and Wyoming is my top priority. I will work hard to: Fight Tax Increases; Fight Inflation; Fight for Jobs; Fight for Limited Government; Fight for our State Employees, Teachers, and our Laramie County Workforce; Fight for our Mineral and Energy Industries; Fight to Preserve our Wyoming Way of Life. As a fiscal conservative, I will fight every day to keep government small, efficient, and responsible.
