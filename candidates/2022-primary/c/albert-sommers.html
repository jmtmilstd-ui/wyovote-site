Albert Sommers

Profile · 2022 Primary

Albert Sommers (Republican, Pinedale): Rancher; Wyoming House of Representatives District 20, 2013–2025. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 20 with 57.6% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.

Primary result: Won, 2,139 votes (57.6%), 1st of 3.

General result: State House HD20, Republican, 3,273 votes (100%), Unopposed.
