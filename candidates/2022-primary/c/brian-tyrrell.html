Brian Tyrrell

Brian Tyrrell (Republican, Cheyenne). Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 8 with 17.0% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 611 votes (17%), 4th of 4.

Research

Background

Born and raised in Cheyenne. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported his 2022 campaign website as tyrrellforwyoming.com.

Positions

Taxes, schools, transparency and Medicaid

His priorities included property rights and taxation, K-12 funding, and transparency and communication between state agencies. He told the Wyoming Tribune Eagle, “I’m a big believer that we need to get Medicaid expansion. We, as a state, have left $600 million roughly on the table. That’s hurting the most vulnerable people in our state, and it’s putting pressure on all businesses.” He said he decided to run after seeing legislators waste taxpayer money during a special session, and that the state should support people and businesses of Wyoming, not the other way around.
