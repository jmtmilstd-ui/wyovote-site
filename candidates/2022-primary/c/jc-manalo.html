JC Manalo

Profile · 2022 Primary

JC Manalo (Republican, Cheyenne): A June 2026 WTE profile of the same Cheyenne candidate listed professional experience in wholesale and retail management, insurance, gasoline-station operations, real estate, and banking, and said he had most recently served as district manager of the Wyoming Tribune Eagle before working as a benefits adviser with Aflac; City of Cheyenne Committee on Housing and Community Development, since 2021; City of Cheyenne Transit Advisory Committee. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State Senate District 7 with 17.1% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost, 717 votes (17.1%), 3rd of 3.

Background

Had previously run for a Laramie County Commission seat, according to the Wyoming Tribune Eagle’s 2022 primary preview.

A June 2026 WTE announcement of his later City Council campaign reported a bachelor’s in management of financial institutions from De La Salle University; career work in wholesale/retail, insurance, gasoline stations, real estate, banking, and as Wyoming Tribune Eagle district manager; membership in Knights of Columbus Council 801, past Rotary membership, and Greater Cheyenne Chamber of Commerce ambassador/Red Carpet Committee service; and 2021 appointment to the city’s housing and community development committee plus service on the transit advisory committee. (2026 council platform planks were not applied as 2022 Senate positions.)

EducationBachelor’s in management of financial institutions, De La Salle University (reported in a June 2026 WTE profile of his Cheyenne City Council campaign).
Prior officesCity of Cheyenne Committee on Housing and Community Development, 2021; City of Cheyenne Transit Advisory Committee
Positions

Economy and accessibility

He told the Wyoming Tribune Eagle he decided to run to provide an accessible lawmaker to the community. His priorities included rebuilding the economy, inviting investors to do business in Wyoming, and promoting tourism.
