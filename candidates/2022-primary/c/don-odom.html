Don Odom

Profile · 2022 Primary

Don Odom (Republican, Cheyenne): Professional truck driver in the oilfields of Wyoming. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 61 with 25.2% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost, 336 votes (25.2%), 3rd of 3.

Background

Don Odom of Cheyenne ran as a Republican in the 2022 primary. In his 2022 voter-guide statement he identified himself as a professional truck driver in Wyoming’s oil fields and said he believed more blue-collar Americans should take part in government. He wrote that he had been a conservative since voting for Ronald Reagan in 1980, in his first election.
