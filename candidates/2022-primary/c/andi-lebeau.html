Andi LeBeau

Andi LeBeau (Democratic, Riverton): Assistant General Manager, Wind River Hotel and Casino; Wyoming House of Representatives District 33, 2019–2023. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Democratic primary for State House District 33. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 44.6% of the vote.

Democratic · Riverton, Fremont County

Primary result: Unopposed, 133 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: Lost, 867 votes (44.6%), 2nd of 2.

Research

OccupationAssistant General Manager, Wind River Hotel and Casino
Background

I was raised by strong women who prepared me to serve my community and people. I keep our traditional ways and use my voice to amplify all of my constituents. It has been my honor to serve and I ask for your vote so we can continue to work together.

EducationGraduate: University of Wyoming
Prior officesWyoming House of Representatives District 33, 2019–2023
Positions

What, if anything, would you support to improve reasonable access to health care in Wyoming?

I support Medicaid Expansion. The people in my district have suffered from lack of adequate care. I have been proud to work with my colleagues in the House and Senate to promote equity in health care for everyone.

What, if any, specific measures would you propose to generate future state revenue?

As a member of the Northern Arapaho I have worked with our leaders and partners to bring hemp production to the reservation. HD 33 is full of entrepreneurs and creators, and the creative economy is the backbone of our future revenue. Energy production has taken us far but the future will need diversification.

Is current school funding adequate? Why or why not?

School funding is inadequate for our rapidly changing economy. My grandchildren are growing up in a new world and they deserve an investment in their future.
