Background

Bruce A. Jones is a Douglas resident who ran as an independent for House District 6 in 2022. He lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election. His Secretary of State general-election filing listed a Douglas mailing address.

A March 1, 2022 Sierra Nevada Ally excerpt from Michael P. Branch’s book On the Trail of the Jackalope identified Jones as mayor of Douglas and described an interview with him and his wife, Cathy, about the town’s jackalope identity. No public-record education or military history was confirmed for this Profile beyond that interview and the 2022 election filings.
