John Bear

John Bear (Republican, Gillette): Business owner; Wyoming House District 31, 2021–Present; U.S. Navy. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 31. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.
