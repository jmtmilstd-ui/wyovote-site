R. Ray Peterson

R. Ray Peterson (Republican, Cowley): Wyoming State Senate District 19, 2004–2019. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State Senate District 19 with 25.9% of the vote.

Republican · Cowley, Big Horn County

Primary result: Lost, 1,557 votes (25.9%), 3rd of 3.

Research

Background

Peterson attended Northwest College from 1977 to 1978. He went on to attend Brigham Young University from 1981 to 1982. He has worked as Vice President for Office Shop Incorporated since 1984. Peterson served on the Town Council of Cowley from 1986 to 1992. He then served as County Commissioner for Big Horn County in from 1992 to 2000. In 2002, Peterson ran for the Wyoming State Senate but was defeated. He was then appointed to the Wyoming State Senate in 2005.
