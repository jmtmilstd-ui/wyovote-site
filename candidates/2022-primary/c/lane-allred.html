Lane Allred

Profile · 2022 Primary

Lane Allred (Republican, Afton): Former teacher (Cody, Wyoming; Star Valley, Wyoming; and Nephi, Utah) and school counselor (Gooding, Idaho), per a Lincoln County School District #2 board biography posted in 2020; Wyoming House of Representatives District 21, 2023–2025; Lincoln County School District #2 Board of Trustees, 2012-12-01–2020-12-01. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 21 with 58.6% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.

Primary result: Won, 1,745 votes (58.6%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD21, Republican, 3,066 votes (100%), Unopposed.

Background

Lane Allred was born in Afton, Wyoming. Allred earned a bachelor's degree from Brigham Young University in 1971 and a master's degree from the University of Phoenix in 2004. Allred's volunteer involvement includes serving on the Fairview Cemetery board, the Fairview Irrigation District, the Star Valley Historical Society, and the Lincoln County School District #2 board of directors.

A 2020 Lincoln County School District #2 board biography listed a bachelor’s of education from Brigham Young University and a master’s of education from the University of Phoenix, prior teaching in Cody, Star Valley, and Nephi, Utah, and school-counselor work in Gooding, Idaho, and residence in Fairview.
