Robyn M. Belinskey

Profile · 2022 Primary

Robyn M. Belinskey (Republican, Sheridan): Paralegal. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for U.S. Representative with 0.8% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost, 1,306 votes (0.8%), 5th of 5.

ResidenceSheridan, Sheridan County
OccupationParalegal
Background

Robyn M. Belinskey of Sheridan ran as a Republican for Wyoming’s U.S. House seat in 2022. In a 2022 statement, she said she had worked as a paralegal in litigation matters including land use, real estate, worker’s compensation, and wills and trusts, and that the work had given her a wide-ranging background in law.

Do you support or oppose federal legislation to reduce dependency on extractive energy industries? Please explain.

I oppose federal legislation to reduce dependency on extractive energy industries. We still need oil, coal and gas in the state of Wyoming. The US should not be dependent on foreign extractive energy resources.
