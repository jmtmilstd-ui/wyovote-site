John Eklund

Profile · 2022 Primary

John Eklund (Republican, Cheyenne): Rancher; Wyoming House District 10, 2011–2025; Wyoming House of Representatives District 10, 2011–2025. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 10 with 74.2% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.

Primary result: Won, 2,134 votes (74.2%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD10, Republican, 3,197 votes (100%), Unopposed.
