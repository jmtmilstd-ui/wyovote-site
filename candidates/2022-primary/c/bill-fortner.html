Bill Fortner

Bill Fortner (Republican, Gillette): Coal, oil and gas industry worker (45+ years); cattle and sheep operation; Wyoming House of Representatives District 52, 2021–2023. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State Senate District 1 with 27.2% of the vote.

Republican · Gillette, Campbell County

Primary result: Lost, 1,736 votes (27.2%), 3rd of 3.

Research

OccupationCoal, oil and gas industry worker (45+ years); cattle and sheep operation
Background

Bill Fortner was born in Gillette, Wyoming.

I'm Bill Fortner, a 5th generation Campbell county native on my mother's side, 4th generation on my father's side. Both sides were ranchers. I've worked in coal, oil, gas, and ranching most of my life. I retired from coal mining in 1999, and then I worked in the gas fields in Wyoming and Montana, as well as the oil industry where I still do contract work today.

Prior officesWyoming House of Representatives District 52, 2021–2023
Positions

Why have you decided to run for this office?

I'm running for senate district #1 because I don't feel the citizens are getting what they deserve. Someone who's truthful, honest, and transparent in leadership. I have been a house representative of district 52 for the last 2 years. I feel I know enough about the process to serve Crook, Weston, and Campbell equally, and responsibly.

What is your vision for the future of your district? What, if any, new initiatives would you like to implement if elected?

I would like to see government tax only what is absolutely necessary not build lavish saving and investment accounts on the backs of the hard working men and women. I will pursue bills I have ran the last 2 years in the house as representative district 52, including personal property tax depreciation, an education expense tax credit (school choice), visitation rights, and dual public employment and office holding. I will push for tax cuts and, in the event of the people being over taxed at the end of the year, they will be reimbursed with tax credits. I will pursue legislation to make senior citizens tax exempt at age 65.

Is there anything else voters should know about you as they determine whom to vote for?

All citizens in Senate District 1 will be equal in representation.
