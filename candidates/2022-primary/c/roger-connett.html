Roger Connett

Roger Connett (Republican, Sundance): More than 40 years in the mining industry, including equipment operator, mine manager, Western Regional Manager and Customer Support Manager for the U.S. and Mexico for a mining equipment company. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State Senate District 1 with 33.0% of the vote.

Republican · Sundance, Crook County

Primary result: Lost, 2,105 votes (33%), 2nd of 3.

OccupationMore than 40 years in the mining industry, including equipment operator, mine manager, Western Regional Manager and Customer Support Manager for the U.S. and Mexico for a mining equipment company
Background

I am Roger Connett, and I am running for Senate District 1. I graduated from high school in Riverton, Wyoming and attend Casper College. In 1979 I moved to Gillette to continue my mining career. I worked in the mining industry for over 40 years, as an equipment operator and then into management at various levels including as a mine manager. My career path changed when I accepted a position as the Western Regional Manager for a mining equipment company. After a short period of time, I was promoted to their Customer Support Manager for the US and Mexico. I have served on many boards and commissions both at state and local levels, including the Wyoming Mining Association Board of Directors, the State Unemployment Commission, and the Northeast Wyoming Economic Development Commission.

EducationGraduated from high school in Riverton; attended Casper College (degree not stated)
Positions

Why have you decided to run for this office?

I believe that for far too long the voice of the people has been ignored and many of our legislators have represented their opinion rather than that of their constituents. The legislature is full of individuals who know how to spend money, because we have been blessed with abundant revenue in the past, but those days are gone. We need legislators who know how to rein in spending and set priorities. Increasing taxes instead of good budget management and fiscal responsibility should not be an option.

What is your vision for the future of your district? What, if any, new initiatives would you like to implement if elected?

An important piece of business for our next legislature is to change the model used to assess property taxes. We currently use a “Market Value” model. You are then taxed on unrealized income, income that can only be realized by selling your home. We should look at an acquisition-based formula for determining taxes, where your property tax is based on what you paid for your property, with reasonable escalators.

Is there anything else voters should know about you as they determine whom to vote for?

I believe in the Right to Life and have been endorsed by the Wyoming Right to Life, the 2nd Amendment, Family Values and Parental Rights, School Choice, no new taxes and smaller government.
