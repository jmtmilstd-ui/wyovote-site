Dave Zwonitzer

Profile · 2022 Primary

Dave Zwonitzer (Republican, Cheyenne): Auctioneer; Wyoming House of Representatives District 8, 2023–2025; Wyoming House District 9, 2006–2016. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 8 with 35.3% of the vote. Won the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 69.1% of the vote.

Primary result: Won, 1,267 votes (35.3%), 1st of 4.

General result: State House HD08, Republican, 2,717 votes (69.1%), Won.

Background

David Zwonitzer was born in Holton, Kansas. Zwonitzer earned a B.S. from the University of Wyoming in 1975 and an M.S. in water resources from the University of Wyoming in 1978. His career experience includes working as an auctioneer and owner of Z's Home Furnishings.
