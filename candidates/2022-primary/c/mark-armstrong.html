Mark Armstrong

Profile · 2022 Primary

Mark Armstrong (Republican, Centennial): Legislative advocacy, business development, and legal/regulatory compliance work. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for Secretary of State with 9.3% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost, 14,304 votes (9.3%), 3rd of 3.

Background

Mark Armstrong of Centennial ran as a Republican for secretary of state in 2022. In his statement for the League of Women Voters of Wyoming 2022 statewide primary voter guide, he listed professional experience in legislative advocacy, business development, litigation mitigation, legal strategy, regulatory compliance, and related government-liaison and permitting work.

Issue positions

What changes, if any, would you recommend in Wyoming’s election laws?

“Absentee and mail-in ballots appear to be the source of the voter fraud in most cases. We all know that the day-of-election in-person voting is not the problem. So why after a major rewrite of the election code does the election code still read: 22-9-102. Who may vote by absentee ballot. (a) Any qualified elector may vote by absentee ballot. Shouldn't we be encouraging day-of-election in-person voting? And limiting mail-in and/or absentee voting to people with special circumstances (military, camp jobs, out of town, homebound, resident in nursing care facility, or other). Shouldn't we advocate for paper ballots? Stopping crossover voting with a three pronged approach.

What role should the Secretary of State play in increasing voter turnout in Wyoming?

When people believe that the vote counts, they will turn out to vote.

What role should the Secretary of State play in increasing voter turnout in Wyoming?

In Albany County we had our voting poll locations restricted due to Covid. We put together a team that was able to restore, for the most part, our rural polling locations.
