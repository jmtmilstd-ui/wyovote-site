Jim McCollum

Jim McCollum (Republican, Jackson): Carpenter since the 1980s; has been building custom log cabins since 1987. Also a published poet. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 16. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 26.0% of the vote.

Republican · Jackson, Teton County

Primary result: Unopposed, 1,058 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD16, Republican, 869 votes (26%), Lost.

Research

OccupationCarpenter since the 1980s; has been building custom log cabins since 1987. Also a published poet.
Background

Father of Lance Cpl. Rylee McCollum, a 20-year-old Jackson Marine killed in the Aug. 26, 2021, suicide bombing at the Kabul airport during the U.S. withdrawal from Afghanistan. He told KHOL he had not been seeking office but decided to run after his son’s death and a homecoming parade for Rylee’s remains, and that local Republicans also encouraged him to file. He said he wanted to be a voice for the voiceless as his son had been in service.

Positions

Housing, the environment and government spending

A lifelong Jackson resident, he told KHOL he considered his views right of center and called the U.S. Constitution “the best document ever written.” Issues he hoped to address included housing, the environment, and transparency in government spending. He said he had lived paycheck to paycheck despite steady carpentry work and understood working-class struggles as Jackson became more expensive, and that almost all Wyoming politicians were “sitting on a pretty cushiony bank account.” Jackson Hole Press quoted him saying the community needed a voice in Cheyenne “strong in conviction and resolute in representing the people.”
