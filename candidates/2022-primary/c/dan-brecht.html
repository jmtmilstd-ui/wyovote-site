Dan Brecht

Profile · 2022 Primary

Dan Brecht (Independent). Lost the November 8, 2022 general election for State House HD04 with 1,436 votes (36.1%).

General result: State House HD04, Independent, 1,436 votes (36.1%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceWheatland, Platte County
OccupationOwner, The Wandering Hermit Books and Gifts
Background

Dan Brecht is a Wheatland native who ran as an independent for House District 4 in 2022. He lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election. His Secretary of State general-election filing listed a Wheatland mailing address.

In a June 10, 2020 Alliance for Historic Wyoming profile of the store, Brecht wrote that he and his son Zach opened The Wandering Hermit Books and Gifts in 2014 in a 1903 building at 875 Gilchrist in Wheatland, and that they later expanded into the adjoining storefront, adding a toy store, a custom leather shop, and upstairs apartments.
