Bryan Miller

Bryan Miller (Republican, Sheridan): Owner of BEM Int’l, LLC, a Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business based in Sheridan specializing in energy, infrastructure, and land use consulting (started October 2010); retired U.S. Air Force Lieutenant Colonel and Instructor/Evaluator Pilot; Republican Party of Wyoming Chair, 2025–Present; United States Air Force, retired February 2011 at the rank of Lieutenant Colonel; Instructor/Evaluator Pilot with Command Pilot Wings and more than 2,500 flight hours; combat veteran (58 combat/combat support air-refueling missions across three campaigns, including Desert Storm). Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 51 with 48.2% of the vote.

Republican · Sheridan, Sheridan County

Background

I am married to Susan, my amazing wife of over 31 years. I graduated from Sheridan High School in 1984, the US Air Force Academy in 1988 (Bachelor of Science in Biology), was commissioned as a 2nd Lieutenant in the United States Air Force and worked my way through the ranks to become a USAF Instructor/Evaluator Pilot. I retired in February 2011 at the rank of Lieutenant Colonel. In October 2010 I started BEM Int’l, LLC, a Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business based in Sheridan, Wyoming.

Positions

Guiding principles and constitutional rights

I believe in God, the U.S. and Wyoming Constitutions. I will support and defend them, always. I believe that there is an assault on our 1st, 2nd, 4th and 10th Amendments which must be addressed. I believe free-market capitalism is the best economic system to ensure individual liberty and creative freedom. I believe in the Right to Life. I believe that no one in Washington has the right to tell us in Wyoming how to manage our water, air, wildlife, minerals and other natural resources.

Property taxes and tax policy

Property taxes are destroying Wyoming’s citizens’ ability to own property. Voters want change in how our property taxes are assessed. That is why I am supporting a change to an acquisition-depreciation based property tax system. Our most vulnerable residents will soon be taxed out of their own homes. Whether it is the built-in annual increase in property taxes or the attempts to raise Wyoming's fuel tax, or the concealing of taxes as increases in fees and licenses, this madness must end.

Limited government, elections, and the Second Amendment

Our State & Federal Governments are massive and out-of-touch. We must limit our government in size, function and reach. On election integrity, crossover voting and the inability of the Wyoming Legislature to pass a means to recall elected officials puts our ability to have faith in fair elections in jeopardy. On the Second Amendment, the words, 'shall not be infringed' are not optional.
