Shelly L. Duncan

Profile · 2022 Primary

Shelly L. Duncan (Republican, Lingle): Realtor/broker (2022 League of Women Voters legislative directory); listed as 2017 president of Wyoming Realtors; Wyoming House of Representatives District 5, 2019–2023. Lost the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 5 with 46.3% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost, 1,546 votes (46.3%), 2nd of 2.

OccupationRealtor/broker (2022 League of Women Voters legislative directory); listed as 2017 president of Wyoming Realtors
Background

Born March 24, 1968, in Beaumont, Texas. The 2022 LWV directory listed art/interior design studies at Lamar University, work as a realtor/broker, 2017 service as Wyoming Realtors president, and affiliations including the North Platte Valley Board of Realtors, Wyoming Realtors, the National Association of Realtors, Republican Women, Goshen Help, and Totes for Hope. Married to Eric, with three children. She was in her first House term (two years) at the time of the directory.
