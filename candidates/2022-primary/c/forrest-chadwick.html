Forrest Chadwick

Forrest Chadwick (Republican, Evansville): Agriculture; Wyoming House of Representatives District 62, 2023–2025. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 62 with 54.3% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.

Republican · Evansville, Natrona County

Primary result: Won, 1,312 votes (54.3%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD62, Republican, 2,518 votes (100%), Unopposed.

OccupationAgriculture
Background

Forrest Chadwick was born in Casper, Wyoming. Chadwick's career experience includes working in agriculture and managing Pesco Inc., a supply company. Chadwick also served as a county commissioner.

(Name, where you’re from, employment, hobbies, etc.) I am Forrest Chadwick, born, raised and educated in central Wyoming. Married for 52 years with two daughters and three grandchildren. I have found that a lifelong involvement in agriculture has been most rewarding. My career took me into the oil fields of the inter-mountain West where I spent 40 years, culminating in managing a highly successful supply company, Pesco Inc. In retirement I served as a County Commissioner for 8 years with 2 1/2 years as chairman and 2 1/2 years as vice chair. All of my experience will serve me well in the Wyoming State House.

In addition to those qualifications listed previously, I also served as Chairman of the Ag, Water and Public Lands Committee for the Wyoming County Commissioners Association as well as represented Wyoming as a director on the Western Interstate Region National Association of Counties Board of Directors.

Prior officesWyoming House of Representatives District 62, 2023–2025
Positions

Why have you decided to run for office and what do you hope to accomplish should you be elected?

I have always felt we each have a responsibility to return service to the community in which we live; this will be a continuation of that service. While the finances of the state have improved the future is uncertain and planning for it lies ahead.

How do you plan on accomplishing your goals?

By working with fellow legislators, state agencies, the governor, and others to accomplish the goals of the future for Wyoming.

Do you feel you could be a good steward with taxpayer dollars? Why or why not?

While serving as a county commissioner my record of fiscal responsibility is extensive.

On the issue of transparency, where do you stand on ensuring all public business is conducted openly and in a manner that encourages public attendance?

One of my greatest frustrations as a commissioner was the lack of media and public attendance at our meetings where I routinely commented on the goings-on not only of the commission but of the current status of ag, water and public lands issues.

Do you believe the office or board position you seek has been open and honest with the public? If yes, how can the entity remain open and transparent when conducting public business moving forward? If no, what changes would you implement to ensure that all future dealings are open and transparent?

Legislative sessions as well as interim committee meetings are open to both media and public.

If you were presiding over a meeting and a topic was being discussed that you didn’t fully understand, would you ask for a more detailed explanation during the meeting or would you seek the information after the meeting?

I would ask for a more detailed explanation during the meeting.

Should you be elected, or reelected, do you plan on seeking any major policy changes in your chosen office. If yes, what would those changes be? If no, why not?

I have no changes in mind, however am open to suggestions.
