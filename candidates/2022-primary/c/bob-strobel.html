Bob Strobel

Profile · 2022 Primary

Bob Strobel (Independent). Lost the November 8, 2022 general election for State House HD22 with 1,681 votes (42.9%).

General result: State House HD22, Independent, 1,681 votes (42.9%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceEtna, Lincoln County
OccupationOwner of See Jackson Hole and the Jackson Hole Computer Clinic; co-founder and former vice president of Jackson Hole Net
Background

Bob Strobel is an Etna resident who ran as an independent for House District 22 in 2022, seeking the seat left open when Rep. Jim Roscoe did not run again. He lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election. His 2022 campaign site said he moved to Jackson Hole from Twin Falls, Idaho, in 1995 at age 19, to be closer to his grandmother and the family ranch in Bondurant. He wrote that he co-founded Jackson Hole Net (later AllTrips.com) and served as vice president for 18 years before starting See Jackson Hole, a live-webcam tourism site, and that he also owns and manages the Jackson Hole Computer Clinic in Jackson.

A June 8, 2022 Jackson Hole News&Guide report identified him as 47 years old at the time of that interview, said he and his wife of 16 years had a 7-year-old son and a 6-year-old daughter, and noted that outgoing Rep. Roscoe had endorsed him. WyoFile’s Oct. 15, 2022 race preview likewise described him as an Etna resident and founder of SeeJH.com and the Jackson Hole Computer Clinic. His campaign site named his wife as Clara Harbeck and said the family lived in Etna full time.
