Joshua Thomas (J.T.) Larson

Joshua Thomas (J.T.) Larson (Republican, Rock Springs): Business manager; Wyoming House District 17, 2023–Present; Wyoming House of Representatives District 17, 2023–Present. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 17. Won the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 60.8% of the vote.

Republican · Rock Springs, Sweetwater County

Primary result: Unopposed, 1,322 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD17, Republican, 1,389 votes (60.8%), Won.
