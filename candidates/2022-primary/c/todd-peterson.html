Todd Peterson

Profile · 2022 Primary

Todd Peterson (Independent). Lost the November 8, 2022 general election for State House HD05 with 1,603 votes (42.2%).

General result: State House HD05, Independent, 1,603 votes (42.2%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceTorrington, Goshen County
OccupationRetired rural banker; former president of Pinnacle Bank in Torrington (retired 2021)
Background

Todd Peterson is a Goshen County native who ran as an independent for House District 5 in 2022. He lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election. His Secretary of State general-election filing listed a Torrington mailing address.

A Sept. 10, 2022 KNEB / Rural Radio announcement said Peterson was raised in Goshen County, graduated from the University of Wyoming with a degree in agricultural economics, and had worked more than 40 years in rural banking, beginning with Farm Credit and retiring in 2021 after 22 years with Pinnacle Bank, including service as president of Pinnacle Bank in Torrington. The same announcement said he served on Goshen Economic Development (including as a past president), the Wyoming Lottery Commission, the Eastern Wyoming College Foundation board, and the St. Joseph’s board, and that he was a past president of the Torrington Rotary club. It also said he graduated from Leadership Wyoming in 2006, and that he and his late wife Maggie, married 41 years, had four children and seven grandchildren.
