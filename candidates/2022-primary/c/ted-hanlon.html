Ted Hanlon (Democratic, Cheyenne): Chemical process consultant; owner of Hanlon Process Consulting, LLC (since 2013). Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Democratic primary for State Senate District 5. Lost the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 29.2% of the vote.

Democratic · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Unopposed, 178 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State Senate SD05, Democratic, 1,354 votes (29.2%), Lost.

I live at 808 West Dale Blvd. in Cheyenne. I have lived here since 2008. I have lived in Wyoming all of my life, except for a short stint in Denver from 1981-1982 and Anchorage in 1990. I was born in Cheyenne, Wyoming.
