Tom Walters

Profile · 2022 Primary

Tom Walters (Republican, Casper): Rancher; Wyoming House District 38, 2013–2024; Wyoming House of Representatives District 38, 2013–2025. Unopposed in the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 38. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.

Primary result: Unopposed, 1,944 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD38, Republican, 2,233 votes (100%), Unopposed.

OccupationRancher
Background

Wikipedia identifies him as a rancher who graduated from Kelly Walsh High School in 1994 and earned a B.S. from the University of Wyoming. He represented House District 38 from January 8, 2013, to January 2025, and was a candidate for Senate District 30 in 2004 and 2008. His spouse is listed as Kade Walters.

He was born Jan. 29, 1976, in Powell, and holds a B.S. in animal science/ag business, Methodist affiliation, past membership and chair of the Natrona County Conservation District and Natrona County Fair Board, and life membership in the NRA.
