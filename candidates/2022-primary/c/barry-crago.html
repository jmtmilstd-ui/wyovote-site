Barry Crago

Barry Crago (Republican, Buffalo): Attorney; Wyoming House District 40, 2021–2024; Wyoming Senate District 22, 2025–Present. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 40 with 60.5% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.

Republican · Buffalo, Johnson, Sheridan Counties County

Primary result: Won, 2,631 votes (60.5%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD40, Republican, 4,007 votes (100%), Unopposed.
