Ken Pendergraft

Ken Pendergraft (Republican, Sheridan): Contractor; Wyoming House District 29, 2023–Present. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 29 with 50.7% of the vote. Won the Nov. 8, 2022 general election with 65.0% of the vote.
