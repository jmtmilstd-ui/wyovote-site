David Northrup

David Northrup (Republican, Powell): Wyoming House District 50, 2013–2020; Wyoming House District 25, 2023–2024. Won the Aug. 16, 2022 Republican primary for State House District 25 with 41.1% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election.

Republican · Powell, Park County

Primary result: Won, 1,164 votes (41.1%), 1st of 4.

General result: State House HD25, Republican, 2,585 votes (100%), Unopposed.

Research

Background

David Northrup was born in Laramie, Wyoming. Northrup volunteered as a part of the following civic organizations: Willwood Irrigation District, Willwood Light and Power, Park County School District #1, Northwest College Ag advisory board, and Park County Republican chairman.

Born March 6, 1961, in Laramie. Wikipedia lists a B.S. from Montana Technological University (Montana Tech of the University of Montana). He represented House District 50 from January 2013 to January 2021 and House District 25 from January 2023 to January 2025.
