Background

Erin Weisman served as Teton County and prosecuting attorney after winning the November 2022 general election. The Jackson Hole News&Guide reported on July 24, 2024, that Gov. Mark Gordon appointed her as Teton County Circuit Court judge following Curt Haws' retirement, and that commissioners appointed Richard "Dick" Stout to fill the county attorney vacancy.
