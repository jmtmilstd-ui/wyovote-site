Background

Carrie S. Long served as Sublette County clerk after winning the November 2022 general election. A June 27, 2025, Pinedale Online notice from the Sublette County Republican Party, listing her as Carrie L. Long, stated that the Board of County Commissioners had declared a vacancy in the office of county clerk effective June 24, 2025, after her resignation. Official July 14, 2025, commissioners proceedings record the appointment of Jessica Rice as interim clerk.

Prior officesSublette County clerk (won the November 2022 general election; served until a vacancy effective June 24, 2025)
