Background

David Russell served as Platte County sheriff after winning the November 2022 general election. Cowboy State Daily reported on August 26, 2025, that the commission, with Chairman Steve Shockley and Commissioner Jeb Baker voting, accepted his resignation effective September 30, 2025, and that a reason was not given to the public at that meeting. The same report, citing his official county biography, said he grew up in Wheatland, started at the sheriff's office in 2012, served as a Wyoming park ranger for six seasons, earned a bachelor's degree in criminal justice from Colorado Technical University, and earned a master's degree from the University of San Diego. Anthony "Tony" Krotz was later appointed sheriff.
