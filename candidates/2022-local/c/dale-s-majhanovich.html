Background

Dale S. Majhanovich is the Sweetwater County coroner, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The Green River Star reported in April 2022 that he is a Rock Springs native, has been coroner since 1993, was first appointed deputy coroner in 1982, graduated from Rock Springs High School, attended Western Wyoming Community College and San Francisco College of Mortuary Science, and received a Wyoming State embalmer's license in 1979. He won the 2022 general election. Incoming coroner Travis R. Sanders takes office in January 2027.
