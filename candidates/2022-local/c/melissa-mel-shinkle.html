Background

Melissa "Mel" Shinkle is the Teton County assessor, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. She won the November 2022 general election. The Jackson Hole News&Guide reported that she opted against seeking another term. Incoming assessor Ashlee Nader takes office in January 2027.
