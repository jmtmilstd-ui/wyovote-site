Background

Colleen Renner is the Park County clerk, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The county's official clerk page lists her in that office. The Powell Tribune reported in 2026 that she is retiring after three terms and that her first deputy, Hans Odde, is succeeding her. Incoming clerk Hans Odde takes office in January 2027.
