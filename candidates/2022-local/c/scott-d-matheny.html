Background

Scott D. Matheny is the Campbell County sheriff, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The county's official sheriff's office page lists him as sheriff. County 17 reported in April 2026, when Lt. Dan Maul announced a campaign to succeed him, that Matheny had announced his retirement after 40 years in law enforcement. Incoming sheriff Dan Maul takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesCampbell County sheriff (won the November 2022 general election)
