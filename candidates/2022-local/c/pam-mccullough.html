Background

Pam McCullough served as Converse County clerk of district court after winning the November 2022 general election. The Douglas Budget reported on March 18, 2026, that the longtime clerk said she planned to give commissioners a formal resignation letter on March 17, that she was not planning to run for re-election, and that she thought it would be better to let a new clerk attend trainings for electronic changes coming to the office. Commissioners later appointed Barbara Harris as interim clerk.
