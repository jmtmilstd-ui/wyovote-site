Background

Tom Doyle is the Natrona County treasurer, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. He won the November 2022 general election. Oil City News reported that he announced his retirement and that deputy treasurer Heather Bryan sought the office; she takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesNatrona County treasurer (won the November 2022 general election; listed among current officers on the official elected officials page)
