Background

Jim Ford is a Campbell County commissioner and won a new term in the November 3, 2026, general election. The county's official commissioners page lists him on the board. In an August 2026 Gillette News Record candidate statement he wrote about the board's budget work during his time in office, including reducing the overall budget, prioritizing staff compensation, increasing funding to the fire department, transferring county-owned property to Gillette College, and creating a county finance director position. No separate public-record account of his private occupation or education was found in the records reviewed for this Profile.
