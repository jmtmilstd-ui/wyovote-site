Background

Willing "John" Johnson, also listed as John Johnson, is a Carbon County commissioner and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The official commissioners page lists him as John Johnson. The Rawlins Times reported that he received 2,553 votes in the general election, and earlier quoted him as Commissioner John Johnson when the board accepted Clerk Gwynn Bartlett's resignation in December 2024. The minted John Johnson record and the Willing "John" Johnson Profiles are for the same official.
