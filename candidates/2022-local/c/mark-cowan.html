Background

Mark Cowan, listed on the 2026 ballot as S. Mark Cowan, is the Sweetwater County treasurer and was re-elected in 2026. The Rocket Miner reported in November 2022 that he defeated incumbent Democrat Joe Barbuto, that he began as a financial services representative for New York Life in 1996 and later founded an insurance brokerage, spent several years in the hospitality industry as a corporate trainer, became a founding member of Radiant Manufacturing in Sweetwater County in 2007, and in 2016 started Wash and Glow Car Wash and is a partner in other car washes in Wyoming. The paper also reported that he was serving on the Sweetwater County Economic Development Coalition.

Prior officesSweetwater County treasurer (elected 2022; re-elected 2026)
