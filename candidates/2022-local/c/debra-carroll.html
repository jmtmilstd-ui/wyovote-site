Background

Debra Carroll, also listed as Deb Carroll, is the Park County clerk of district court, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The official clerk of district court page lists her in that office and names Jessica Goodwin as first deputy. The Powell Tribune reported in 2026 that she is retiring and that former deputy Lynell Preston is succeeding her. Incoming clerk Lynell Preston takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesPark County clerk of district court (won the November 2022 general election). The official office page lists her as District Court Clerk Deb Carroll.
