Background

Janet K. Montgomery is the Sublette County clerk of district court and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The official clerk of district court page lists her in that office and describes the duty to maintain and preserve records of cases filed with the Ninth Judicial District Court in Sublette County, dating back to 1923, along with processing court-ordered payments, appeals, and jury trials.

Prior officesSublette County clerk of district court (listed on the official clerk of district court page; she was re-elected in 2026)
