Background

Lloyd E. Thiel is vice chairman of the Park County Board of Commissioners and won another term in the November 3, 2026, general election. The official commissioners page lists him as vice chairman. The Powell Tribune reported in June 2026 that he was born in Red Lodge and is descended from early northwest Wyoming residents, that he has spent his career in contracting, taking over from his father and later handing much of the business to his son, and that he operates a ranch in Clark. The paper reported that in his first two terms he pushed for a county procurement policy and worked on planning, zoning, and the land-use plan.

Prior officesPark County commissioner (re-elected 2022 and 2026; the Powell Tribune reported in June 2026 that he was seeking a third term)
