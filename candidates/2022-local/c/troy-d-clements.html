Background

Troy D. Clements is the Campbell County assessor, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The county's official assessor page states that he is a Campbell County native for more than 45 years and has been with the Assessor's Office for over 20 years. He won the 2022 general election. Incoming assessor Erin Slattery, the office's chief appraiser, takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesCampbell County assessor (won the November 2022 general election)
