Background

Christy Schneider served as Washakie County clerk of district court after winning the November 2022 general election. The Northern Wyoming News reported on February 6, 2025, that she announced her resignation at the February 4 commission meeting and would serve as interim clerk through March 4 to help with the transition. The same newspaper later reported that she was moving to South Dakota and that commissioners appointed former deputy Mirian Hansen to the office.

Prior officesWashakie County clerk of district court (won the November 2022 general election; resigned February 2025)
