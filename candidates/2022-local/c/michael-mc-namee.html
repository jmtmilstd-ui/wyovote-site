Background

Michael McNamee is chairman of the Goshen County Board of Commissioners, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The official commissioners page lists him as chairman. The county's 2022-2023 and 2025-2026 salary publications (used here for job titles only) list him as a commissioner. Incoming commissioner Brent L. Anderson takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesGoshen County commissioner (won the November 2022 general election; listed as chairman on the official commissioners page)
