Background

Debra Lee is the Laramie County clerk, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported in April 2026 that she was appointed in 2016 after the death of Clerk Debbye Lathrop, was elected in 2018 and re-elected in 2022, and would not seek another term. The paper reported that she previously worked in the Wyoming Secretary of State's Elections Division and for the state treasurer, served in the Peace Corps in Yemen, and worked on elections abroad, including United Nations volunteer work on the 1999 East Timor referendum and elections in South Sudan in 2011. In a 2018 profile she described herself as a military brat and said her background included overseeing elections in Wyoming and abroad. Incoming clerk Misty Tinney, who won the 2026 election, takes office in January 2027.

EducationBachelor's degree in journalism from Northwestern State University of Louisiana; master's degree in international administration from the School for International Training in Vermont (2018 Wyoming Tribune Eagle candidate biography)
