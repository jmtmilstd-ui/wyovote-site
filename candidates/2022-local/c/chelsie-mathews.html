Background

Chelsie Mathews is the Albany County assessor and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The official assessor page lists her in that office and states that the assessor's constitutional duty is to list, locate, and value all property in Albany County. The Laramie Reporter reported that she, a Republican, ran unopposed and received 97% of the votes cast.
