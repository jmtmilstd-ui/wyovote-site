Background

Tammy Deisch is the Laramie County treasurer, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. Cap City News reported on December 21, 2022, that she was the chief deputy of the treasurer's office and would be sworn in on January 3, 2023, after running unopposed in the 2022 primary, as Treasurer Trudy Eisele retired. The Wyoming County Treasurer's Association welcomed her among new treasurers sworn into their first terms on January 3, 2023, and the Wyoming Tribune Eagle listed her among first-term officials sworn in that day. Incoming treasurer Trista Wood, who won the 2026 election, takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesLaramie County treasurer (elected 2022). Cap City News reported in December 2022 that she was then the chief deputy of the treasurer's office.
