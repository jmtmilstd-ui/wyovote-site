Background

Douglas W. Weaver, also listed as Doug Weaver, is the Platte County attorney, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. He won the November 2022 general election, and the official county directory lists him in that office. Incoming attorney Marel E. Bunker Roth takes office in January 2027. The minted Doug Weaver record and the 2022 Douglas W. Weaver Profile are for the same official.

Prior officesPlatte County attorney (won the November 2022 general election; listed as Doug Weaver in the official county directory)
