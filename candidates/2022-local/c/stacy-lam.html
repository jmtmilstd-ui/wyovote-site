Background

Stacy Lam is the Albany County clerk of district court, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The official clerk of district court page lists her in that office, with a hire date of 1992 and an appointment date of 2019. She won the November 2022 general election. Incoming clerk of district court Kayla Marroquin takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesAlbany County clerk of district court (hired 1992; appointed 2019, as listed on the official clerk of district court page; won the November 2022 general election)
