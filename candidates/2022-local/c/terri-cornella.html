Background

Terri Cornella served as Hot Springs County clerk of district court after winning the November 2022 general election. The Thermopolis Independent Record reported on December 28, 2023, that commissioners voted at their December 19 meeting to appoint Kristina McNeff to the district court clerk position.

Prior officesHot Springs County clerk of district court (won the November 2022 general election)
