Background

Barbara A. Poley, also listed as Barb Poley, is the Park County treasurer, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027, and is a Park County commissioner-elect who takes office that month, succeeding Dossie Overfield. The official treasurer page lists her as treasurer. On her campaign site she wrote that she is a Park County native, a graduate of Cody High School and Northwest College, was elected treasurer in 2010, has 32 years of local banking experience, and has served as a member of the Park County Leadership Institute steering committee and on the Cody Country Ambassadors, the Soroptimist board, and the American Red Cross board, and that she is a past president of the Wyoming Association of County Treasurers and of the Association of County Officials. Incoming treasurer Anne Lawler takes office in January 2027.
