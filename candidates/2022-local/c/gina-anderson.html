Background

Gina Anderson served as Big Horn County assessor from the end of 2006 until her retirement effective January 5, 2026. The Lovell Chronicle reported on January 8, 2026, that she took office at the end of 2006 after emerging from a three-way Republican primary and being elected to replace Jackie Payne, that she asked in a December 29 letter that her retirement take effect January 5, 2026, and that the commissioners accepted her resignation and declared a vacancy the following day.

Prior officesBig Horn County assessor (the Lovell Chronicle reported she took office at the end of 2006 after winning election to replace Jackie Payne, was re-elected including in 2022, and retired effective January 5, 2026)
