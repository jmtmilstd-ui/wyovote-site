Background

Sylvia Hackl is the Laramie County district attorney, a position she won in 2022 and was re-elected to in 2026. In a 2022 Wyoming Tribune Eagle candidate questionnaire she reported a bachelor's degree, summa cum laude, from Lewis and Clark College in Portland, Oregon, in 1977; a J.D. from the University of Wyoming in 1980; and a master's degree in public administration from the University of Wyoming (the questionnaire listed 1985; a June 2022 Tribune Eagle profile listed 1988). Those articles described a legal career in Laramie County beginning in 1980 that included work in the Wyoming Attorney General's Office, including as chief deputy; appointment by Governor Jim Geringer as state public defender in 1995, a role she held for six years; later service on the State Board of Equalization; work as a deputy county attorney; about two years as Cheyenne city attorney; and service as a staff attorney for U.S. District Judge Alan B. Johnson, handling prisoner litigation from September 2019 through September 2021. She was sworn in as district attorney on January 3, 2023, after defeating Thomas E. Callison in the 2022 Republican primary. The 2026 ballot listed her as Sylvia Lee Hackl; the 2022 ballot listed her as Sylvia Miller Hackl.

EducationB.A., Lewis and Clark College, 1977; J.D., University of Wyoming, 1980; M.P.A., University of Wyoming (Wyoming Tribune Eagle 2022 candidate questionnaire and June 2, 2022, profile; those articles differ on the M.P.A. year)
Prior officesLaramie County district attorney (took office January 2023; re-elected November 2026). She wrote in 2022 that she had previously run unsuccessfully for Cheyenne city council in the mid-1980s and for district attorney in 2002.
