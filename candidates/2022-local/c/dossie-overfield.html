Background

Dossie Overfield is a Park County commissioner, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The official commissioners page lists her on the board. The Powell Tribune reported in October 2026 that she was born in Maine, raised in Colorado, has lived in Park County since 1981, worked as a veterinary technician at Chadwick Veterinary Hospital and as an office assistant in the commission office, then spent 23 years working for and later managing the Northwest Rural Water District, and that she served on the Park County School District 6 board, including as chairman, spent 15 years on the Shoshone Recreation District board, and served on the Powell Library board. The paper reported that she did not seek a third commission term and ran unopposed for the Northwest College Board of Trustees, representing the Cody subdistrict, after trustee John Housel retired. Incoming commissioner Barbara A. Poley takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesPark County commissioner (two terms; not seeking re-election in 2026). The Powell Tribune reported prior service on the Park County School District 6 board, including as chairman, 15 years on the Shoshone Recreation District board, and the Powell Library board, and that she ran unopposed in 2026 for the Northwest College Board of Trustees, Cody subdistrict.
