Background

Kenneth Guille was elected Laramie County assessor in 2022 and later left the office. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported in April 2023 that he had submitted his resignation and had been appointed property tax administrator with the Wyoming Department of Revenue, and that in a statement he thanked voters and colleagues for support during his terms and through nearly 25 years with the county. Commissioners later appointed Todd Ernst to the vacancy. Guille's 2022 general-election Profile remains the canonical page for this record.

Prior officesLaramie County assessor (won the November 2022 general election; resigned effective May 1, 2023)
