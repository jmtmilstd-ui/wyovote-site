Background

Kayla White is the Albany County clerk and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The official clerk page lists her in that office and describes the clerk as clerk to the Board of County Commissioners, chief budget officer, and chief election officer. The Laramie Reporter reported that she, described as a one-term Democrat, defeated Republican R. Mark Armstrong, taking 8,596 votes (67%) to 4,246, and that her tenure has included the debut of vote centers.

Prior officesAlbany County clerk (won the November 2022 general election and was re-elected in 2026; the Laramie Reporter described her as a one-term Democrat seeking another term)
