Background

Bryan A. Skoric is the Park County and prosecuting attorney, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The official attorney page lists him in that office. The Powell Tribune reported in 2026 that he has held the position since early 2003, that after about 24 years he is leaving the post, and that two of his deputies, Ericka Smith and Marina Tucker, competed to succeed him. Incoming attorney Ericka Smith takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesPark County and prosecuting attorney. The Powell Tribune reported in May 2026 that he has held the office since early 2003 and that his current term would be his last.
