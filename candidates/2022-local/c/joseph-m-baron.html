Background

Joseph M. Baron, also listed as Joe Baron, served as Crook County attorney after winning the November 2022 general election. The Sundance Times reported on December 11, 2025, that friends and fellow officials gathered to mark his retirement effective December 31, that he had provided almost 30 years of service as county attorney, and that commissioners accepted his resignation with one year remaining in the term. DaNece Day was later appointed to fill the unexpired term.
