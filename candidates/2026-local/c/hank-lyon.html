Background

Hank Lyon ran in the 2026 Republican primary for Campbell County attorney. County 17 reported in April 2026 that he is a deputy county and prosecuting attorney who prosecutes child sexual-abuse and drug-distribution cases and serves in the Felony Treatment Court program, that he earned a law degree from Emory University, holds a Master of Business Administration, and has a background in his family's restaurant and real estate businesses, and that he lives in Gillette. He lost the August 18, 2026, Republican primary to Kyle Ferris.
