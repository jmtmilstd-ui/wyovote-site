Background

Kyle Ferris is the Campbell County attorney-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Nathan Henkes, who did not seek re-election. County 17 reported that Ferris is a Campbell County native who has spent 10 of his 15 years as an attorney in the County Attorney's Office, including prosecution of felony cases, and previously was in private practice from 2011 to 2014. A 2020 County 17 report on a prior vacancy and a 2024 Wyoming State Bar announcement state that he earned a B.A. in psychology from Wake Forest University and a law degree from the University of Wyoming. County 17 also reported prior service on the Campbell County Public Library Board and the City of Gillette Park and Beautification Board, and that he defeated fellow deputy attorney Hank Lyon in the August 18, 2026, Republican primary. He was unopposed in the November general election.
