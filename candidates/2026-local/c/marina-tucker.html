Background

Marina Tucker ran in the 2026 Republican primary for Park County attorney. The official attorney page lists her as a deputy in the office. The Powell Tribune reported in 2026 that she is an Ohio native who started in social work, earned a law degree in 2019, worked in civil practice, became a deputy prosecutor in Billings, Montana, and joined the Park County Attorney's Office in 2025, and that in Billings she had handled a much higher caseload than in Park County. She lost the August 18, 2026, Republican primary to fellow deputy Ericka Smith.

EducationLaw degree, 2019 (Powell Tribune, July 9, 2026; the article did not name the law school)
