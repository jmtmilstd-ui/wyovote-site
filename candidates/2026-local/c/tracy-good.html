Background

Tracy Good is the Natrona County clerk and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. Oil City News reported that at a July 8, 2026, Politics in the Park forum she said she was seeking a third term, had worked in the office for 32 years, and described the clerk as custodian of official county records, with duties that include preparing and maintaining the county budget, processing vehicle titles, and running elections.
