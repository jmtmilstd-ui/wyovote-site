Background

Mary B. Feagler is the Goshen County clerk and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The official clerk page lists her in that office. The county's September 2022 salary publication (used here for job titles only) listed her as chief deputy clerk, with Cindy Kenyon listed as county clerk; later 2022-2023 and 2025-2026 publications list Feagler as county clerk after she won the 2022 election.

Prior officesGoshen County clerk (won the November 2022 general election; re-elected 2026). The September 2022 county salary publication (used here for job titles only) listed her as chief deputy clerk, when Cindy Kenyon was listed as county clerk.
