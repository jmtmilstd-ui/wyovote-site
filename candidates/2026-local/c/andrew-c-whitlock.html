Background

Andrew C. Whitlock of Powell ran in the 2026 Republican primary for Park County commissioner. The Powell Tribune reported in February 2025 that he was among the Republicans who applied for appointment as interim Park County assessor after Pat Meyer retired, and that he is a Powell appraiser and real estate broker. He lost the 2026 Republican primary for commissioner.
