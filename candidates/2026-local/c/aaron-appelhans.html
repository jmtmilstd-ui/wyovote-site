Background

Aaron Appelhans is the Albany County sheriff and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The Laramie Boomerang reported on January 16, 2021, that the Albany County Board of Commissioners appointed him on December 11, 2020, to fill a vacancy left by retired Sheriff Dave O'Malley, and that he had grown up in the Westminster area of Denver, earned a civil engineering degree from the University of Wyoming in 2003, worked in the UW admissions office, and joined the University of Wyoming Police Department in 2010, serving as a crime prevention specialist and patrol officer, detective, and patrol sergeant. The same article reported that the appointment made statewide news as Appelhans became the first Black sheriff in Wyoming. His campaign Meet Aaron page states that he came to Laramie as a UW student in 1999, graduated in 2003, and has called Laramie home since. The official sheriff page lists him in that office. He won the November 2022 general election and was re-elected in 2026.
