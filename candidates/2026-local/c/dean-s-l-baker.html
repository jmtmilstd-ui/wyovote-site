Background

Dean S. L. Baker of Platte County ran in the 2026 Independent contest for county clerk and lost the November general election. The official general election summary lists him with 527 votes, while Republican Malcolm J. Ervin received 2,839. No public-record occupation, education, or military service was found in the official Platte County election results or the other public records reviewed for this Profile.
