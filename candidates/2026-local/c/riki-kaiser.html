Background

Riki Kaiser is the Weston County clerk of district court and was elected in the November 2026 general election. Official February 4, 2025, commissioners minutes record that the board appointed her as clerk of district court. The News Letter Journal reported that she was the deputy to Tina Cote, was the only remaining applicant after another candidate withdrew, and that in her letter of interest she said she had worked nearly five years as deputy clerk of district court. The same newspaper reported she was unopposed in the August 2026 Republican primary.

Prior officesDeputy clerk of district court (nearly five years, as she wrote in the letter of interest quoted by the News Letter Journal); Weston County clerk of district court (appointed February 4, 2025; elected 2026)
