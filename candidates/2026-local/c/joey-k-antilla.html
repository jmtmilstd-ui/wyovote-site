Background

Joey K. Antilla is the Lincoln County treasurer-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Jerry Greenfield, who is serving through the current term. The Antilla For Treasurer campaign site states that she is a lifelong resident of Kemmerer and has served as deputy treasurer for 24 years, including 18 years under Greenfield and six years under Kathy Tomassi. SVI-NEWS reported that she won the August 2026 Republican primary with 2,783 votes over Dawn Marquardt and that the general-election race was then unopposed.

Prior officesDeputy treasurer, Lincoln County (24 years, including 18 years under Jerry Greenfield and six years under Kathy Tomassi, as stated on the Antilla For Treasurer campaign site)
