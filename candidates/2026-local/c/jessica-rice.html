Background

Jessica Rice is the Sublette County clerk. Official July 14, 2025, commissioners proceedings record that the board interviewed Jerun Kerback, Jakobi Burbank, and Rice for the open clerk position after Carrie Long's resignation and appointed Rice as interim clerk until the next election; the minutes are attested by Rice as clerk. The official Board of County Commissioners page lists her as county clerk. A 2024 official county employee public notice published in the Pinedale Roundup listed her as chief deputy county treasurer (title only). She won the August 2026 Republican primary and the November 2026 general election.

Prior officesChief deputy county treasurer (title listed in the August 29, 2024, official county employee public notice); Sublette County clerk (appointed interim clerk July 14, 2025; elected November 2026)
