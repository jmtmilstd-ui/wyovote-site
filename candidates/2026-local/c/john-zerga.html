Background

John Zerga is the Fremont County sheriff-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Sheriff Ryan Lee, who County 10 reported had announced his retirement in January 2026. In a County 10 campaign announcement he said he was born in Lander, graduated from Lander Valley High School, graduated from the Wyoming Law Enforcement Academy in 1999, began as a seasonal park ranger at Boysen State Park, later served as a detention officer in Natrona County, then returned to the Fremont County Sheriff's Office as a patrol deputy in Dubois, later served on patrol in Lander, was promoted to detective sergeant and then patrol sergeant, and had served as a lieutenant for the previous seven years, with more than two decades in law enforcement. County 10 reported that he won the August 18, 2026, Republican primary in a four-candidate field and was unopposed in the November general election.
