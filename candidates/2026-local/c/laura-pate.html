Background

Laura Pate ran in the 2026 Republican primary for Laramie County clerk. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported in August 2026 that she was born in Colorado, that her family moved to Wyoming when she was 6 months old, that she grew up in Jackson and later lived near Saratoga, and that she earned an associate degree in criminal justice at Eastern Wyoming College in Torrington. The paper reported that she began working for Laramie County in 2002 in the auto titles office (about 11 years), then moved to the real estate recording office, and is the real estate recording manager, a position she had held for about nine years at the time of the article, and that she has assisted with elections since she began county work. She finished behind Misty Tinney in the August 18, 2026, Republican primary.
