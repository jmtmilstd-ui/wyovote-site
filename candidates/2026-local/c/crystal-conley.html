Background

Crystal Conley is the Campbell County treasurer-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Rachael Knust, who is serving through the current term. On her campaign About page she wrote that she is a Campbell County native, has worked in the Treasurer's Office for 13 years, and has served the last several of those years as chief deputy treasurer, overseeing daily operations including motor-vehicle registration and the collection of sales and property taxes. She was unopposed in the November 3, 2026, general election.
