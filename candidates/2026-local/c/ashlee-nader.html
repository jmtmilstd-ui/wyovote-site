Background

Ashlee Nader is the Teton County assessor-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Melissa "Mel" Shinkle, who the Jackson Hole News&Guide reported opted against seeking another term. The same newspaper reported that Nader, a Democrat, defeated Republican Norman Mellor III.
