Background

Connie Czarnecki ran in the 2026 Republican primary for Laramie County clerk. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported in August 2026 that she is an associate broker for eXp Realty, has more than 35 years of experience in health care, ran her own company, served as director of the surgical technology program at Laramie County Community College, and moved to Wyoming in 2015 after selling her company. The paper also reported that she has served on a school board and was elected a Republican committeewoman for the Laramie County GOP. She finished behind Misty Tinney in the August 18, 2026, Republican primary.
