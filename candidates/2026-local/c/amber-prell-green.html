Background

Amber Prell-Green, also listed as Amber Green and, in a April 10, 2026, News Letter Journal update, as Amber Prell, is the Weston County clerk and was elected in the November 2026 general election. WyoFile reported that at a June 4, 2026, district court hearing she was serving as interim clerk and said she had served in the clerk's office for seven years. Official July 29, 2026, commissioners minutes record that after Clerk James Michael Tooman resigned, the board appointed Deputy Clerk Amber Green as acting county clerk; she attested those minutes in that capacity. The official clerk page photograph identifies County Clerk Amber Green. The News Letter Journal reported that she won the August 2026 Republican primary after being appointed earlier that month following Tooman's resignation.

Prior officesWeston County clerk's office (seven years, including service as deputy and interim clerk, as she stated at the June 4, 2026, hearing reported by WyoFile); Acting Weston County clerk (official July 29, 2026, minutes); Weston County clerk (elected November 2026)
