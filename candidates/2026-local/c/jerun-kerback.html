Background

Jerun Kerback served as chief deputy county clerk, a title recorded in official July 14, 2025, commissioners proceedings, which also list her among the candidates interviewed for the open clerk position after Carrie Long's resignation. A 2024 official county employee public notice published in the Pinedale Roundup listed her as a deputy county clerk (title only). She ran in the August 2026 Republican primary for county clerk and lost to Jessica Rice.

Prior officesDeputy county clerk; chief deputy county clerk (titles listed in the August 29, 2024, official county employee public notice and in official July 14, 2025, commissioners proceedings)
