Background

Cindy J. Lovelace is the Campbell County clerk and was re-elected in 2026. In a 2022 County 17 candidate questionnaire she wrote that she was born and raised on a ranch in northern Campbell County, is a third-generation graduate of Campbell County High School, worked five years as a detention officer for the Campbell County Sheriff's Office, co-owned Monogramming Plus Screen-printing for seven years, later directed the Northeast Wyoming Red Cross, including disaster-recovery work after the 2006 tornado in Wright, and had owned and operated her insurance agency since 2006. The official clerk's page lists her as county clerk.
