Background

Randall Tryzbiak ran in the 2026 Republican primary for Sweetwater County sheriff. In a Green River Star voter-guide statement he wrote that he moved to Rock Springs almost 20 years earlier, had lived there continuously for 16 of those years, that his businesses are in the community, and that he is not a career law-enforcement officer. He lost the August 18, 2026, Republican primary.
