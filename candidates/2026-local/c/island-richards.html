Background

Island Richards is a Sweetwater County commissioner, first elected in 2022 and re-elected in 2026. On his campaign About page he wrote that he is a longtime Sweetwater County resident, is president and CEO of Kayar Distributing, Inc., a locally owned family business operating convenience stores in the county, and that he and his wife, Teresa, live in the county. He listed prior service on the White Mountain Water & Sewer District board and the Sweetwater County Planning & Zoning Commission, including as chairman of each, and later appointment to the Wyoming Air Quality Advisory Board. In Green River Star voter-guide statements he described himself as a fifth-generation Sweetwater County resident and local business owner.

Prior officesSweetwater County commissioner (elected 2022, re-elected 2026). He has also written that he served on the White Mountain Water & Sewer District board (2000–2010, including as chairman) and the Sweetwater County Planning & Zoning Commission (2012–2019, including as chairman).
