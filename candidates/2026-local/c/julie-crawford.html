Background

Julie Crawford, also listed as Julie Altaffer on the 2022 ballot, is the Crook County clerk of district court and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The official clerk of district court page lists Julie Crawford in that office, states that she assumed office in January 2023, and uses the email juliea@crookcounty.wy.gov. The 2022 Julie Altaffer Profile and the 2026 Julie Crawford Profile are for the same official.

Prior officesCrook County clerk of district court (won the November 2022 general election as Julie Altaffer, assumed office in January 2023, later listed as Julie Crawford on the official clerk of district court page, and was re-elected in 2026)
