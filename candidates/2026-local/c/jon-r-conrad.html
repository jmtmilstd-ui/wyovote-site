Background

Jon R. Conrad is a Uinta County commissioner-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Eric South, who is serving through the current term. He previously served in the Wyoming House of Representatives from House District 19 after winning the November 2022 general election and left the House after the 2023–2024 term, having lost the 2024 Republican primary.

Prior officesWyoming House of Representatives, House District 19 (won the November 2022 general election; served 2023–2024; lost the 2024 Republican primary)
