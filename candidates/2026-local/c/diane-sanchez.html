Background

Diane Sanchez is the Laramie County clerk of district court and was unopposed for another term in 2026. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported in May 2018, when she sought a second term, that she had decades of experience in the clerk's office beginning in 1990, that she was a Cheyenne East High School graduate, and that she had been clerk of district court since 2015. The paper reported that she served as chief deputy clerk during the 2014 Nathaniel Castellanos murder trial, a death-penalty case that required a sequestered jury, and that her office handles filings, probate, appeals, and jury organization. She was among the incumbents sworn in again on January 3, 2023.
