Background

Dan Maul is the Campbell County sheriff-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Sheriff Scott Matheny, who announced his retirement after 40 years in law enforcement. County 17 and the Gillette News Record reported in April 2026 that Maul is a lifelong Campbell County resident, graduated from Campbell County High School, attended Sheridan College, earned a bachelor's degree in criminal justice from Chadron State College, and began work at the sheriff's office in 2001. The papers reported that he has served in patrol, drug investigation, general criminal investigation, and as a DARE officer, has been a member of the Special Response Unit since its founding in 2003, serves as tactical commander, and is the office's lieutenant of operations. He and his wife raise cattle in northern Campbell County. He was unopposed in the November 3, 2026, general election.
