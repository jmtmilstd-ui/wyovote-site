Background

Sherry Bushman is a Sweetwater County commissioner-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Commissioner Robb Slaughter, who is serving through the current term. In Green River Star primary and general voter-guide statements she wrote that she serves on the Green River City Council, has volunteered extensively in the community, and has a particular commitment to supporting military veterans. She won one of the three commission seats in the November 3, 2026, general election.
