Background

Cody W. Gortmaker is the Park County coroner and was re-elected in the November 3, 2026, general election. The official coroner page lists him as Cody Gortmaker, CMDI, and names Myles Haines, Zach Thompson, and Charla Torczon as deputy coroners. The Powell Tribune reported in June 2026 that he was unopposed in his bid for a second term.

Prior officesPark County coroner (the Powell Tribune reported in June 2026 that he was unopposed in a bid for a second term)
