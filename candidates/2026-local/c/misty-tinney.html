Background

Misty Tinney is the Laramie County clerk-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Debra Lee, who is retiring at the end of the current term. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported in August 2026 that she is a fourth-generation Laramie County native who has worked for the county for 28 years, beginning in the Clerk's Office in 1998, later spending about nine to 10 years in the Treasurer's Office, and returning in 2012 as supervisor of auto titles and the Uniform Commercial Code department. The paper and her campaign website also reported that she holds an associate degree in public administration and a Certified Public Manager designation, and that she has been active in the Wyoming County Clerks' Association. She won the 2026 Republican primary over Laura Pate and Connie Czarnecki and was unopposed in the November general election.
