Background

Craig R. Shidler is the Big Horn County sheriff-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding interim sheriff Jason Beal. The Lovell Chronicle reported that he is of Lovell, was a Big Horn County sergeant who interviewed for the interim appointment in April 2026, and won the August 2026 Republican primary with 1,851 votes, then ran unopposed in the November general election.
