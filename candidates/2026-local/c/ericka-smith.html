Background

Ericka Smith is the Park County attorney-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Bryan A. Skoric, who is serving through the current term. The official attorney page lists her as a deputy in the office. The Powell Tribune reported in 2026 that she moved to Park County from Cheyenne in 2025 to take the deputy post in the Powell office, that she has practiced law in Wyoming for 26 years, grew up in Cheyenne, and earned a law degree at the University of Wyoming, and that her career has included work as an assistant attorney general on criminal appeals and Department of Corrections matters, about four years as a prosecutor in the Laramie County District Attorney's Office, about four years as a state public defender plus federal court-appointed defense work, private criminal-defense and civil practice including a firm in Cheyenne, work at the Petroleum Association of Wyoming, and service as associate general counsel at the University of Wyoming just before she came to Park County. In a Cody Journal voter-guide statement she wrote that she has lived in Wyoming since she was a child and that both her undergraduate degree and her law degree are from the University of Wyoming. She defeated fellow deputy Marina Tucker in the August 18, 2026, Republican primary and was unopposed in the November general election.
