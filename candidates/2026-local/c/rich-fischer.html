Background

Rich Fischer is the appointed Sweetwater County sheriff, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. The Green River Star's August 2026 sheriff voter guide said he was appointed to complete the unexpired term of retired Sheriff John Grossnickle, is a nearly 18-year veteran of the sheriff's office, had served as special services lieutenant, and brings more than three decades of public service in emergency services and law enforcement. He lost the August 18, 2026, Republican primary to Richard F. Kaumo, who takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesSweetwater County sheriff (appointed 2026; lost the 2026 Republican primary)
