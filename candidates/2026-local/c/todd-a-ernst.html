Background

Todd A. Ernst is the Laramie County assessor. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported in April 2023 that the Board of Commissioners appointed him to the unexpired term of Kenneth Guille, who resigned to become property tax administrator at the Wyoming Department of Revenue. The paper reported that Ernst had been with the Assessor's Office for 30 years, had served as deputy county assessor for the previous two years, is a member of the International Association of Assessing Officers, is permanently certified as a Wyoming property tax appraiser, and had served 30 years in the Army National Guard before retiring honorably. In a June 2026 Cap City News questionnaire he wrote that he had worked in the office more than 33 years and had served as assessor for the past three years, and that he has been a certified Wyoming tax appraiser since 1993. He was unopposed in the 2026 general election.
