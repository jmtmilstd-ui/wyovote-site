Background

James A. Anderson, also listed as Jim Anderson, is the Fremont County treasurer and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The official county biography states that he was appointed treasurer on October 6, 2020, was elected in 2022, had served as deputy treasurer and financial manager since February 2017, joined the county in June 2014, and serves on the county's Executive Health Committee and Capital Revolving Committee, with a background in governmental accounting.

Prior officesFremont County treasurer (the official county biography states he was appointed treasurer on October 6, 2020, and was elected in 2022; he was re-elected in 2026).
