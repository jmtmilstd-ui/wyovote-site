Background

Scott A. Steward is chairman of the Park County Board of Commissioners and won a second term in the November 3, 2026, general election. The official commissioners page lists him as chairman. The Powell Tribune reported in April 2026 that he grew up in Cody, graduated from Cody High School in 1985, served in the U.S. Marines, later lived in Ralston and on the South Fork and has lived for years in Wapiti, and that he retired as Park County sheriff after 32 years in the department, rising from dispatcher to detention and patrol deputy, lieutenant, undersheriff, and sheriff, before he was elected a commissioner in 2022. The paper also reported a period of ranch work in Meeteetse after he left the sheriff's office.
