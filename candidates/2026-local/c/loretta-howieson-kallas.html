Background

Loretta Howieson Kallas is the Uinta County attorney and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The official elected officials list names her as attorney. The Uinta County Herald reported that at the June 17, 2025, commission meeting she swore in Ashlee Richins as county assessor. She won the November 2022 general election.
