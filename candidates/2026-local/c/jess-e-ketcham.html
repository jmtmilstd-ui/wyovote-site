Background

Jess E. Ketcham is a Laramie County commissioner-elect and takes office in January 2027. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported in May 2026 that he is a fifth-generation Wyomingite whose family connection to the area dates to an 1867 homestead, that he grew up in Cheyenne, graduated from East High School, attended LCCC, and graduated from the University of Wyoming with a degree in agriculture business, and that he serves as administrator for the Wyoming State Budget Department. The paper reported that he has served about a decade as an LCCC trustee, including after receiving the most votes in a later trustee general election, and that his father served as a county commissioner for around 28 years. He lost a 2024 race for the county commission by nearly 300 votes, according to that article, and in 2026 finished first among the commissioner candidates in the November 3 general election. Incumbent Commissioner Linda Heath, who lost the 2026 Republican primary, serves through the current term.
