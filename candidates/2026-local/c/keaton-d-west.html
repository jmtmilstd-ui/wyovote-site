Background

Keaton D. West is a Sweetwater County commissioner, first seated in 2023 and re-elected in 2026. On his campaign website he wrote that he was born and raised in Rock Springs, has lived his life in Sweetwater County aside from attending college at the University of Wyoming, and listed a Bachelor of Applied Science in Organizational Leadership. The site lists prior service on the Rock Springs City Council, including as council president in 2022, and earlier service on the city's planning and zoning commission, the county parks and recreation board, and the Rock Springs Chamber of Commerce board, including terms as president of the latter two. The county's commissioner profiles list him among the current board; his campaign site says he has served as commission chairman.
