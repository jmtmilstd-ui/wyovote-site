Background

Anthony "Tony" Krotz is the Platte County sheriff. The official sheriff page states that he was appointed sheriff on October 17, 2025, becoming the 16th sheriff of Platte County, after David Russell's resignation. Cowboy State Daily reported on October 14, 2025, that the county Republican Party nominated him as one of three finalists and that he was then the county emergency management coordinator and had served as a police officer in Nebraska and Wyoming. He was elected in the November 2026 general election; the official summary lists him with 3,034 votes.

Prior officesPolice officer in Nebraska and Wyoming; Platte County emergency management coordinator (Cowboy State Daily, Oct. 14, 2025); Platte County sheriff (appointed Oct. 17, 2025, as stated on the official sheriff page, and elected in 2026)
