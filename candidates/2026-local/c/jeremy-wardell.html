Background

Jeremy Wardell is the Goshen County assessor-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Debra A. Surratt, who is serving through the current term. The official assessor staff directory lists him as deputy assessor. His official staff-directory biography states that he has worked for the Assessor's Office for about five years, holds a Permanent Property Tax Appraiser Certification from the State of Wyoming, served as a property data specialist for about two years, and has been deputy assessor for the last three years. He won the November 2026 general election.
