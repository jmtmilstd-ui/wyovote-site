Background

Ozzie Knezovich ran in the 2026 Republican primary for Sweetwater County sheriff. In a Green River Star voter-guide statement he wrote that he was born and raised in Sweetwater County, served in the U.S. Army, began a law-enforcement career here, later served nearly 17 years as an elected sheriff of a former agency, and had worked with the Wyoming Division of Criminal Investigation as volunteer police chief in Superior. He lost the August 18, 2026, Republican primary.

MilitaryU.S. Army (Green River Star, Aug. 6, 2026, voter-guide statement)
