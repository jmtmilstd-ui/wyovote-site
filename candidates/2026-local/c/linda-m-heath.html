Background

Linda Heath is a Laramie County commissioner serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. Her official county biography states that she has lived in Laramie County since 1991, was raised in western Nebraska, and is a graduate of Eastern Wyoming College and Chadron State College, with associate degrees in animal health technology and graphic arts technology. It states that she has worked in retail and advertising sales, as a printing press operator, in food service, in journalism, and as an interior designer; that she and her husband came to Laramie County via the Air Force and own Heritage Building Services, Inc.; and that she is a former vice chairwoman of the Laramie County Republican Party and has been a precinct committeewoman for more than 20 years. She lost the August 18, 2026, Republican primary. Commissioner-elect Jess E. Ketcham takes office in January 2027.
