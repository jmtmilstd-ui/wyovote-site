Background

Ella M. Riggs is the Fremont County assessor. After Assessor Tara M. Berg died suddenly in September 2026, County 10 reported that the Fremont County Republican Central Committee named Riggs, then an Assessor's Office employee, as the party's general-election ballot successor on September 9, and that the county commission selected her on September 29 to finish Berg's current term; she was sworn in that day. The official assessor page states that she was appointed interim assessor on September 29, 2026, that she has more than eight years of experience in Fremont County government, including more than five years in the Assessor's Office as a field appraiser and office appraiser, that she is a Wyoming Certified Property Tax Appraiser with 271 hours of appraisal education, that she holds an associate's degree in human resource management, that her family relocated to Wyoming in 1994, that she enlisted in the military in 2002 and lived out of state until returning to Lander in 2014, and that she has 20 years of service in the United States Air Force and National Guard. County 10 also reported that she previously worked in the County Clerk's Office, including on elections. She was unopposed in the November 3, 2026, general election.
