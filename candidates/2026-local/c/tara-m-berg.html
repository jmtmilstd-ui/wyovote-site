Background

Tara M. Berg served as Fremont County assessor and won the August 18, 2026, Republican primary for a new term. County 10 reported her sudden death in September 2026, after which Ella M. Riggs was named the party's general-election ballot successor and was later appointed to finish Berg's current term. Berg was not on the November general-election ballot.

Prior officesFremont County assessor (the clerk's office-holders list, drawn from the 2024 election, listed her in that office with a term ending in January 2027). She won the August 18, 2026, Republican primary.
