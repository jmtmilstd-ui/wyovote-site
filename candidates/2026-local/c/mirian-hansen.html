Background

Mirian Hansen is the Washakie County clerk of district court and was elected in the November 2026 general election. The Northern Wyoming News reported on February 20, 2025, that commissioners appointed the former deputy clerk on Tuesday (February 18) to replace Christy Schneider, who had resigned, and that Schneider would help with the transition as interim clerk. The official clerk of district court page lists Hansen in that office.

Prior officesDeputy clerk of district court, Washakie County (Northern Wyoming News, Feb. 20, 2025); Washakie County clerk of district court (appointed February 18, 2025, and elected in 2026)
