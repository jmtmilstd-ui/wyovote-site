Background

Lisa M. Smith is the Carbon County clerk and was elected in the November 2026 general election. The Rawlins Times reported on December 18, 2024, that commissioners unanimously appointed her interim clerk effective January 6, 2025, after Gwynn Bartlett resigned to become a commissioner, and that Bartlett said Smith was chief deputy clerk with 21 and a half years in the office. The Carbon County Comet reported on February 8, 2025, that commissioners swore in a new county clerk at their February 4 meeting. The official clerk page lists her in that office.

Prior officesChief deputy clerk, Carbon County (21 and a half years in the clerk's office, as reported by the Rawlins Times quoting Gwynn Bartlett); Carbon County clerk (interim appointment effective January 6, 2025; elected 2026)
