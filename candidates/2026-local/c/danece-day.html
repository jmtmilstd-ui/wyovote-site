Background

DaNece Day is the Crook County attorney and was elected in the November 2026 general election. The Sundance Times reported on December 25, 2025, that commissioners appointed her with unanimous approval to fill the unexpired term of Joe Baron from names put forth by the Crook County Democratic Party. In a February 26, 2026, profile, the same newspaper reported that she had practiced law in Gillette since 1997, came to assist the Crook County attorney's office, and later closed her Gillette firm.

Prior officesAttorney in private practice in Gillette (from 1997, as reported by the Sundance Times); Crook County attorney (appointed December 2025 to fill Joseph M. Baron's unexpired term, and elected in 2026)
