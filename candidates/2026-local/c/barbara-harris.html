Background

Barbara Harris, also listed as Barbie Harris, is the Converse County clerk of district court and was elected in the November 2026 general election. The Douglas Budget reported on April 15, 2026, that commissioners appointed her interim clerk on the previous Friday (April 10) to replace longtime clerk Pam McCullough, and that Harris had worked in the office for nearly two decades and had already said she planned to run for the office. The April 10, 2026, special-meeting minutes and the Wyoming Judicial Branch listing also identify her in that office.
