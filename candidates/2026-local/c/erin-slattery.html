Background

Erin Slattery is the Campbell County assessor-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Troy D. Clements, who is serving through the current term. The county's official assessor page lists her as chief appraiser. County 17 reported in May 2026 that she is a 19-year veteran of the Assessor's Office, a Campbell County High School graduate who has lived in the community more than 40 years, began property-assessment work in 2007, is a state-certified appraiser and a member of the International Association of Assessing Officers, recently graduated from the Gillette Area Leadership Institute, and serves on the board of the Harris user group. She was unopposed in the November 3, 2026, general election.
