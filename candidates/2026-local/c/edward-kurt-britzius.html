Background

Edward Kurt Britzius, also listed as Kurt Britzius, is the Albany County and prosecuting attorney and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The official attorney page lists him as Kurt Britzius and describes the office's combined county-attorney and prosecuting-attorney duties. The Laramie Boomerang and Laramie Reporter reported that he, a Democrat, defeated Republican Joel Defebaugh, a deputy county attorney and felony prosecutor in the same office, 6,639 votes (52.4%) to 6,005 (47.4%).
