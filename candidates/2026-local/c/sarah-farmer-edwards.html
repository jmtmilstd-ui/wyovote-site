Background

Sarah Farmer Edwards is a Campbell County commissioner-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Kelley McCreery, who is serving through the current term. In a County 17 election questionnaire she wrote that she was raised in Campbell County, graduated from Campbell County High School, has raised her family there with her husband, Craig, and has more than 25 years of experience working in and around the county's coal, oil, gas, agriculture, and real estate industries. She was one of the three Republican nominees elected in the November 3, 2026, general election.
