Background

Richard F. Kaumo is the Sweetwater County sheriff-elect and takes office in January 2027. On his campaign website and in a Green River Star voter-guide statement he wrote that he was born and raised in Rock Springs, has nearly two decades in law enforcement, served with the Sublette County and Sweetwater County sheriff's offices in detention, patrol, investigations, and specialized assignments, and has overseen the Sweetwater County Detention Center, including about 50 employees and court security, and serves as the office's search and rescue team leader. Appointed Sheriff Rich Fischer, who lost the Republican primary, serves through the current term.
