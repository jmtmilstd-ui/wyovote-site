Background

Adrian Keeler is the Johnson County sheriff-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Rod Odenbach, who is serving through the current term. Sheridan Media reported on May 28, 2026, that the Buffalo resident is the lead investigator for the sheriff's office, a position he has held for eight years, after 12 years with the Buffalo Police Department as a patrolman, school resource officer, and sergeant, and that he described 20 years of service as a certified Wyoming peace officer and work as a certified use-of-force and firearms instructor. The same outlet later reported that he advanced from the 2026 primary and was selected in the general election.

Prior officesLead investigator, Johnson County Sheriff's Office (eight years, as reported May 28, 2026); Buffalo Police Department (12 years as a patrolman, school resource officer, and sergeant, same report)
