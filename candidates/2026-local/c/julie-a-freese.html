Background

Julie A. Freese is the Fremont County clerk and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The official county biography states that she is a Fremont County native, attended Wyoming Indian Elementary (formerly known as Mill Creek), graduated from Lander Valley High School, earned an Associate of Arts degree from Casper College in 1980, began work in the Clerk's Office in summer jobs, became elections supervisor in 1982 and deputy county clerk in 1987, was first elected clerk in 1994, and has been re-elected multiple times; that she has served as vice president and president of the County Clerk's Association, as Fremont County's representative to the National Elections Standards Board until January 2014, and as president of the Wyoming Association of County Officials board in 2013; and that she lives in rural Pavillion.

EducationLander Valley High School; Associate of Arts degree, Casper College, 1980. She earlier attended Wyoming Indian Elementary, formerly known as Mill Creek (official county biography).
Prior officesFremont County clerk (the official county biography states she was first elected in 1994 and has been re-elected six times; she was re-elected again in 2026). The same biography states she became elections supervisor in 1982 and deputy county clerk in 1987.
