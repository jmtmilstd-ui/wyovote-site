Background

Jimmy Rush of Wapiti ran in the 2026 Republican primary for Park County clerk. The Powell Tribune reported in June 2026 that he is a military veteran and was one of two Republicans seeking to succeed retiring Clerk Colleen Renner. He lost the August 18, 2026, Republican primary to first deputy clerk Hans Odde.
