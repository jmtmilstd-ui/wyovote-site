Background

Gunnar Malm is a Laramie County commissioner. His official county biography describes him as a sixth-generation lifelong Laramie County resident and a real estate agent and owner with Coldwell Banker The Property Exchange. It states that his family came from Sweden and settled in eastern Laramie County in the 1880s, that his parents, Jody and Julie Malm, grew up in Albin and Pine Bluffs, that he graduated from Central High School, studied political science at the University of Wyoming, and left UW in 2008 to begin work as a residential and commercial realtor in Cheyenne. The biography also states that he has served as public policy chair for the Wyoming Association of Realtors and as president of the Cheyenne Board of Realtors, and has served on boards including Special Friends of Cheyenne, Recover Wyoming, and the Wyoming Housing Opportunities Association, and that he and his wife live in Cheyenne. The county's commissioners page lists him as chairman. He won a new term in the November 3, 2026, general election.
