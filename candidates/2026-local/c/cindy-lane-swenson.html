Background

Cindy Lane, listed on the 2026 ballot as Cindy Lane-Swenson, is the Sweetwater County clerk, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. A 2022 Rocket Miner article on her re-election bid quoted her saying she had lived in Sweetwater County for 35 years and had then served four years as clerk. In an August 2026 Green River Star voter-guide statement she wrote that she had served eight years as clerk, had called the county home for 40 years, graduated from Western Wyoming Community College, attended the University of Wyoming, where she earned two bachelor's degrees and later a master's degree, and has owned and operated a small counseling center in Rock Springs for 20 years. She lost the August 18, 2026, Republican primary to Dale Davis, who takes office in January 2027.
