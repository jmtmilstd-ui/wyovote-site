Background

Rebecca Reid is the Laramie County coroner and was unopposed for another term in 2026. In the coroner's 2022 annual report she wrote that she moved to Cheyenne in 2013, had served as a death investigator with the District 14 Medical Examiner's Office in Panama City, Florida, for seven years, became a Laramie County deputy coroner in December 2013, was promoted to chief deputy coroner in October 2014, and was then in her second term as coroner. The report also states that she has served on the Wyoming Board of Coroner Standards and in the Wyoming Coroner's Association. She was among the incumbents sworn in again on January 3, 2023.

Prior officesLaramie County coroner; previously deputy coroner (from December 2013) and chief deputy coroner (from October 2014), per the office's 2022 annual report
