Background

Becky Kersten is the Hot Springs County clerk and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. The Thermopolis Independent Record reported on August 19, 2021, that commissioners named her clerk during that week's meeting after Rose DeSeyn resigned. She won the November 2022 general election, and the official clerk page lists her in that office.

Prior officesHot Springs County clerk (appointed 2021 after Rose DeSeyn resigned, as reported by the Thermopolis Independent Record; won the November 2022 general election and was re-elected in 2026)
