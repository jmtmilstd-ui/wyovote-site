Background

Ashlee Richins is the Uinta County assessor. The Uinta County Herald reported on June 25, 2025, that during the June 17 county commission meeting, County Attorney Loretta Howieson Kallas swore her in as assessor, replacing outgoing Assessor Lori Perkins. The official assessor page and the official elected officials list name her in that office. She was elected in the November 2026 general election.

Prior officesUinta County assessor (the Uinta County Herald reported she was sworn in on June 17, 2025, replacing Lori Perkins; she was elected in 2026)
