Background

Cozette Winters is the Big Horn County assessor and was elected in the November 2026 general election. The Lovell Chronicle reported on January 29, 2026, that commissioners appointed her the previous Friday (January 23) to replace Gina Anderson, that she started in the assessor's office in 2019 as a deputy assessor, and that she was sworn in by County Clerk Lori Smallwood. The official assessor page lists her in that office. The same newspaper later reported that she ran unopposed in 2026 after the appointment.

Prior officesDeputy assessor, Big Horn County (from 2019, as reported by the Lovell Chronicle); Big Horn County assessor (appointed January 23, 2026, and sworn in by Clerk Lori Smallwood; elected 2026)
