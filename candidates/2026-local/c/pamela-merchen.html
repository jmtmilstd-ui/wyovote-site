Background

Pamela Merchen is the Campbell County clerk of district court and was re-elected in 2026. In a County 17 questionnaire she wrote that she is the candidate who led the office through the transition to the state's universal court management system and electronic filing, which began in November 2022, that she is experienced in case management and administrative operations, and that she has worked with the Wyoming Judicial Branch and the clerks' association for district courts, including on records access, confidentiality, and cybersecurity. The official clerk of district court page lists her as Pam Merchen.
