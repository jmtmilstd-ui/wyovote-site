Background

Lynell Preston is the Park County clerk of district court-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Debra Carroll, who is serving through the current term. The Powell Tribune reported in April 2026 that she began work in the Clerk of District Court office in 2005, served more than 17 years there, including work as second deputy, later worked in circuit court, and at the time of her announcement was a legal assistant at Thompson Law in Cody, and that Carroll was not running again. On her campaign About page she wrote that she is a lifelong Wyoming resident, was raised in Rawlins, moved to Cody in 2000, served more than 17 years as a deputy clerk in the Clerk of District Court office, about a year and a half as a deputy clerk in Circuit Court, and four years as a legal assistant for a local attorney. She was unopposed in the November 3, 2026, general election.
