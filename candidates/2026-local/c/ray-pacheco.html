Background

Ray Pacheco is a Natrona County commissioner-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding James "Jim" Milne, who is serving through the current term. Oil City News identified him as Casper's mayor and reported on May 27, 2026, that he is a lifelong Wyoming resident who has spent more than two decades in public service, mental health work, nonprofit leadership, and higher education, has served on the Casper City Council and as mayor, and works as an assistant lecturer and field coordinator in the Division of Social Work at the University of Wyoming. Official and unofficial 2026 general tallies reported by Oil City News placed him first in the three-seat commission race with 13,256 votes.

Prior officesCasper City Council; mayor of Casper (Oil City News, May 27 and Nov. 4, 2026)
