Background

Anne Lawler is the Park County treasurer-elect and takes office in January 2027, succeeding Barb Poley, who is serving through the current term and was elected a county commissioner in 2026. On her campaign site she wrote that she was born in Newcastle, raised in Powell, and has lived in the Powell, Cody, and Meeteetse areas, and that she has more than a decade of experience in the Treasurer's Office, including service as first deputy overseeing daily operations. The Powell Tribune reported in April 2026 that Treasurer Poley hired her in 2014 after she had been a front-end manager at Albertsons, that she worked her way up through the office, and that she became first deputy when the prior first deputy retired. She was unopposed in the November 3, 2026, general election.
