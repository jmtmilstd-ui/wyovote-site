Background

Brian Kozak is the Laramie County sheriff. The county's official elected-officials page states that he was elected sheriff on November 8, 2022; began his career in 1986 as a Mesa, Arizona, police officer; retired in 2006 to become chief of police in Avon, Colorado; and was appointed Cheyenne's police chief in 2010, serving until 2021 and becoming the city's longest-serving chief. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported in November 2026 that he has served as sheriff since January 2023 and won a second term in the November 3, 2026, general election over Democrat Robin Cozad, after defeating Eric Norris in the Republican primary.
