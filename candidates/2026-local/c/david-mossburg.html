Background

David Mossburg ran for Fremont County commissioner in District 1 in 2026. County 10 reported that he defeated incumbent Clarence V. Thomas in the August 18 Republican primary and later lost the November 3 general election to Independent Nicole A. Wagon. In comments to County 10 after the primary he described a grassroots door-to-door campaign across District 1. No separate public-record account of his private occupation, education, or military service was found in the official pages and other public records reviewed for this Profile.
