Background

Amanda R. Sanchez is the Fremont County clerk of district court and was re-elected in the November 2026 general election. On her official county page she wrote that she was born and raised in Wyoming, has worked in the legal field for a combination of 17 years, spent 13 years at Child Support Services of Wyoming, began clerk work in 2019 with a year in the Clerk of District Court office, then served as a clerk and later chief clerk in Fremont County Circuit Court in Lander, and was appointed clerk of district court in October 2023. She won the November 2024 general election and was unopposed in 2026.

Prior officesFremont County clerk of district court (her official county page states she was appointed in October 2023; she won the November 2024 general election and was re-elected in 2026). The same page states she earlier spent a year as a clerk in that office, then served as a clerk and later chief clerk in Fremont County Circuit Court in Lander.
