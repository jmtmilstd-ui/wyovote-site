Background

Richard "Dick" Stout is the Teton County and prosecuting attorney. The Jackson Hole News&Guide reported on July 24, 2024, that county commissioners appointed him to fill the vacancy left when Gov. Mark Gordon appointed Erin Weisman as Teton County Circuit Court judge, and that Stout had been a practicing criminal defense lawyer in Jackson for 16 years. A May 8, 2026, News&Guide article reported that he was running for the office for the first time after holding it since the 2024 appointment. The same newspaper later reported that he, a Democrat, ran unopposed in the November 2026 general election and was elected.

Prior officesPracticing criminal defense lawyer in Jackson for 16 years (Jackson Hole News&Guide, July 24, 2024); Teton County and prosecuting attorney (appointed by the Board of County Commissioners in 2024 after Erin Weisman was appointed a circuit judge; elected 2026)
