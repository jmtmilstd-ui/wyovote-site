Luke Reiner

Luke Reiner (Republican, Cheyenne): Military officer; U.S. Army National Guard, 1982–2019. Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 8 with 68.0% of the vote.

Background

Luke Reiner served in the U.S. Army National Guard from 1982 to 2019. He earned a high school diploma from the Hillcrest School in Jos, Nigeria. He earned a bachelor's degree from Concordia University in Nebraska in 1986, a graduate degree from the University of Wyoming in 1999, and a graduate degree from the U.S. Army War College in 2005.

I am a Christian, Husband, Father, Grandfather, retired American Soldier, and a lifelong public servant, running as a Conservative, non-caucus Republican. I enlisted in the Army as a teenager and served for 36 years, spending the last eight as the Commanding General of the Wyoming Military Department, where I was responsible for the roughly 3000 men and women of the WY Air and Army Guard. I responded to multiple disasters within Wyoming and deployed to Kuwait in 2004. After retiring from the Guard in 2019, I was privileged to lead another group of men and women as the Director of WYDOT for 4 years, retiring in 2023. …
