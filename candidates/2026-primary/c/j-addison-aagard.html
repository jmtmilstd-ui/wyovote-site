J. Addison Aagard

J. Addison Aagard (Republican, Lovell): Construction worker (self-described: worked in construction since age 12). Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 26 with 24.9% of the vote.

House District 26 · Republican

Primary result: Lost

Research

ResidenceLovell, Big Horn County
OccupationConstruction worker (self-described: worked in construction since age 12)
Background

Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for House District 26 to incumbent Dalton Banks, 644 (24.9%) to 1,938 (75.1%).

Homeschool dropout who has a heart to do right. Worked in construction since I was 12, and helped my dad provide for his family. What makes anyone qualified? An interest in what is good, true and virtuous is what I can call upon for my qualifications.

EducationHomeschooled (self-described "homeschool dropout")
Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

The dissolution of truth, or the deviation from the standard of reality is a great challenge facing our country at large. We have the opportunity to raise the standard of truth to the nation, and innovate independence from the unstable markets that control our economics. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Stop subsidizing it. Artificial or Government interference has a tendency to unbalance the system

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I would support the sale of public land that is of no benefit to the public, to individuals who can use it productively (homesites, businesses, schools, churches etc.) and not corporations or large entities intent on making a profit. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

Spend less. Reevaluate what we actually need. I support homestead exemption from all property taxes. We should not be renting our homes from the government. Counties have means to raise funds without appropriation from the state.

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

Very little. No subsidies, just uphold justice and make sure everyone keeps their promises. Make commerce uniform and consistent.

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I have little to no knowledge regarding this topic, therefore I shall forbear speaking upon it, except in this wise. Don’t cheat, don’t steal, don’t take a bribe. Be moral and virtuous.
