Marcia Neumiller

Marcia Neumiller (Republican, Casper): Paralegal. Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State Senate District 27 with 30.1% of the vote.

Senate District 27 · Republican

Primary result: Lost

Research

ResidenceCasper, Natrona County
OccupationParalegal
Background

Marcia Neumiller graduated from Kelly Walsh High School. She attended Grand Canyon University, earned an associate degree from Casper College in 2012, and a bachelor's degree from Post University in 2023. Her career experience includes working as a paralegal.

I am uniquely qualified to serve Senate District 27 because of my professional experience and a strong record of community service. I work as a paralegal in family law helping families through difficult situations and gaining practical knowledge of the legal system and working with the statutes that are considered and possibly passed on the Senate floor. I am working toward my Master's in Mental Health Counseling to further help our community. I am serving in the Republican party and volunteer on nonprofit boards to engage in different facets of our communities. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Wyoming faces serious challenges in mental and physical healthcare access, the rising cost of living, and the need for a stronger, more diverse economy. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

We need to recruit and retain providers to all of Wyoming. This can be accomplished by reducing unnecessary regulations that make it harder to practice in Wyoming. Provide incentives for providers to care for individuals in rural areas. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

No, I do not support the sell or transfer of public land in Wyoming. Land management decisions should be made by the people of Wyoming. We are the owners of the land. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

Any solution to this must balance the homeowner and property owner with the need for counties, schools, and local services to remain funded. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Wyoming Legislature has a limited but important role in economic development. The legislature should help encourage a stable, business-friendly environment where private enterprise can grow. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

Campaign donations should be legal, transparent, and never allowed to influence the voice of the people I am elected to serve. My campaign will be conducted with honesty, integrity, and full compliance with the law. …
