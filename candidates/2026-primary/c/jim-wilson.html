Jim Wilson

Jim Wilson (Democratic, Rock Springs): Electrical control operator; started career with Pacific Power at the Jim Bridger Plant in 1979. Unopposed in the Aug. 18, 2026 Democratic primary for State Senate District 11.

Background

James A. Wilson has called Southwest Wyoming and Reliance his home for the better part of five decades. He started his career with Pacific Power at the Jim Bridger Plant in 1979. Jim worked as an electrical control operator who believes in keeping the lights on, diversifying our energy economy and keeping good jobs locally with strong union contracts to support our local workers.

Websitewilsonforwyoming.com
Issue positions

Public lands, housing, and cost of living

Public lands access: “keep our public lands in public hands.” Support affordable housing and support infrastructure improvement. Combat rising prices on our local communities. End excessive luxury tax on women’s products and gendered based price disparity.
