Troy Claycomb

Profile · 2026 Primary

Troy Claycomb (Republican, Upton): Retired educator (35 years in Wyoming schools); small business operator. Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 1 with 62.8% of the vote.

Primary result: Won

General result: State House HD01, Republican, 3,688 votes (100%), Unopposed.

Research

ResidenceUpton, Weston County
OccupationRetired educator and educational leader (teacher, coach, principal, and superintendent); small business operator
Background

Troy Claycomb was born and raised in House District 1 and lives in Upton, Weston County. The Sundance Times and Wyoming Pioneer, in matching May 14, 2026 campaign announcement stories, describe him as an Upton High School graduate and University of Wyoming alumnus, and as a husband to Cheri and a father of three. After 35 years in Wyoming schools and operating a small business, he ran for House District 1.

The Associated General Contractors of Wyoming 2026 Primary Election Candidate Guide describes him as a lifelong Wyoming native who grew up in Upton, is a University of Wyoming graduate, and spent his career in education as a teacher, coach, principal, and superintendent.

A May 30, 2012 Gillette News Record report said he had been superintendent of Weston County School District No. 7 in Upton since 2003 and was a former Sage Valley Junior High principal; that article also reported he had been hired as principal at Sunflower Elementary for the following school year.

He won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary and was unopposed in the Nov. 3, 2026 general election. He is the official-elect for House District 1 and takes office in January 2027.
