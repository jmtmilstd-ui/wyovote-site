Background

Noll Roberts lives in Sheridan, Sheridan County. He finished third in the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for House District 29 with 154 votes (7.5%); Myca Sturtevant won with 1,315 (64.2%) and James Temple received 580, according to official primary results already compiled for this site.

He also ran in the 2024 Sheridan County commissioner primary (and lost). His 2026 campaign site, nollroberts.com, is devoted to civic-process pledges for House District 29 precincts and does not state an occupation, education, or prior elected office.

No public occupation, education, or military record was found in the sources reviewed for this fill.
