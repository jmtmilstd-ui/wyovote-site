Dave Hardesty

Dave Hardesty (Independent, Gillette): Teacher and 20-year educator in Wyoming (described as a current teacher in May 2026); U.S. Coast Guard veteran. Lost the Nov. 3, 2026 general election for State Representative H31 with 19.1% of the vote.

House District 31 · Independent

General result: State House HD31, Independent, 358 votes (19.1%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceGillette, Campbell County
OccupationTeacher and 20-year educator in Wyoming (described as a current teacher in May 2026)
Background

Gillette resident (mailing city on the candidate filing) running as an Independent for House District 31 against Republican Douglas Moore.

A 20-year educator and Coast Guard veteran and a University of Wyoming graduate. An avid outdoorsman who has camped in every national forest in the state. Previously ran for House District 31 as an independent in 2018 and lost to then-incumbent Republican Scott Clem, 814 votes to 1,826.

EducationGraduate of the University of Wyoming
MilitaryU.S. Coast Guard veteran
Issue positions

Public schools

Cited the state of public education as a primary reason for running. As a current teacher he said “less government” often means fewer teachers, larger class sizes, and fewer student opportunities, and he advocated that the Legislature financially support public schools as required by the Wyoming Constitution.

Taxes

Questioned calls for tax cuts and said candidates should specify which programs they intend to terminate.

Public lands and wildlife

Voiced concern that abandoning county roads can grant private access to public lands, and said that should be stopped. Proposed codifying corner crossing into law and funding the marking of high-interest crossings. Called for more investment in wildlife crossings on interstates and highways. Said he would work to allow anglers to drop anchor and wade-fish entire rivers where private land lies under navigable waterways.

Party

Said he is running as an independent to evaluate ideas on their own merit rather than follow a national party agenda, with a platform focused on Wyoming’s specific needs.
