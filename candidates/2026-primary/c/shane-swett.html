Shane Swett

Profile · 2026 Primary

Shane Swett (Republican, Laramie): Small business owner. Unopposed in the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 13.

Primary result: Unopposed

General result: State House HD13, Republican, 850 votes (33.6%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceLaramie, Albany County
OccupationSmall business owner; owns A1 Recovery and Towing in Laramie
Background

Shane Swett has spent his life in Laramie and Albany County. His campaign site describes him as a lifelong Albany County neighbor who has raised a family there.

The Albany County Republican Party's candidate page states that he owns A1 Recovery and Towing in Laramie. He is a graduate of Laramie High School and holds a bachelor's degree from the University of Wyoming (already recorded from the 2024 and 2026 research store).
