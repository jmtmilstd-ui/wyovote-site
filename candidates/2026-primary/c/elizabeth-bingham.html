Elizabeth Bingham

Elizabeth Bingham (Republican, Rock Springs): Entrepreneur / business owner; former chairman, Sweetwater County Republican Party. Unopposed in the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 17.

House District 17 · Republican

Primary result: Unopposed

General result: State House HD17, Republican, 1,426 votes (67.3%), Won.

Research

ResidenceRock Springs, Sweetwater County
OccupationEntrepreneur / business owner; former chairman, Sweetwater County Republican Party
Background

Rock Springs has been my home for 22 years. It’s where my husband and I raised our son and built our life. As an entrepreneur, I’ve spent years building businesses, developing programs and serving my community. Politics was often the discussion around my family’s kitchen table, and I volunteered on my first campaign at the age of 16. That interest in public service has stayed with me throughout my life, including serving as Chairman of the Sweetwater County Republican Party. Those experiences taught me about the issues facing our communities and the value of steady, accountable leadership.

Websitebingham4wyoming.com
Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Wyoming’s greatest strength is its people and the industries that built our towns and communities. Our challenge is protecting our way of life while planning wisely for what lies ahead. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Healthcare affordability and access are real challenges in Wyoming, but there is no single legislative solution. I believe the Legislature should work with healthcare providers and communities to identify practical solutions that improve access while keeping costs under control. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

Wyoming’s public lands are central to our economy, outdoor heritage and way of life. I support responsible multiple-use management that balances energy and mineral development, grazing, recreation, wildlife and public access. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

As the discussion has shifted from property tax relief to property tax reform, we must recognize that taxpayer relief and local government funding are not competing goals. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Wyoming Legislature should focus on building a strong foundation for economic growth, not picking winners and losers. If we want the private sector to invest in Wyoming, we must be willing to invest in ourselves by maintaining and building the arterial infrastructure that makes growth possible, including roads, …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I believe Wyoming’s campaign finance laws should promote transparency and public trust while protecting the constitutional rights of citizens to participate in the political process and support the candidates and causes they believe in.
