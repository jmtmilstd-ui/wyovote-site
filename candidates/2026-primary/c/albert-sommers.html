Albert Sommers (Republican, Pinedale): Rancher; Wyoming House of Representatives District 20, 2013–2025. Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 20 with 62.3% of the vote.

Background

Sommers earned his bachelor's degree in electrical engineering from the University of Wyoming in 1981.

I am a life long Sublette County rancher that has served both the rancher community, my local communities, and the State of Wyoming for decades on various boards and commissions. I served for 12 years in the Wyoming House of Representatives, and I was Speaker of the House for the 67th Legislature (2023-2024).

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

At a statewide level, how does Wyoming do a better job of youth retention. This is coupled with the greater challenge of how does Wyoming grow through economic development (providing jobs for Wyoming's youth), while preserving the custom, culture, values and landscapes of Wyoming and its people. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Having served 12 years in the Wyoming Legislature, I know that I cannot be an expert in every arena. I think we need to turn to our very competent Department of Health, Wyoming Medical and Insurance professionals, and health advocacy groups to seek solutions that work for Wyoming. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

I support SJ0009, Keeping public lands protected and decisions local - This resolution passed last session. The core of it is this: That the Wyoming Legislature opposes any state or federal legislation or policy that promotes the broad or indiscriminate sale or exchange of public lands. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

I prioritized property tax relief during my term as Speaker of the House, we passed at least five pieces of legislation designed for tax relief. Subsequently, the Legislature has passed even more relief. We are now at a point where we are impacting local government services and funding for education. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

I support the Wyoming Business Council and the Wyoming Energy Authority. I support both of their efforts to attract business to Wyoming. We need jobs to retain our youth. Every state in the nation does this. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

I accept personal donations to my campaign from individuals. I am NOT currently taking any PAC money for my campaign. I have done it both ways in past elections. I think Wyoming's system is within the constraints of what the courts will allow. Any real reform would take a US Constitutional Amendment. …
