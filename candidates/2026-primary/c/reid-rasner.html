Background

Email [contact removed] to notify us of updates to this biography. Rasner earned a B.A. in English literature with minors in history and theater from the University of Wyoming. Rasner is the founder and CEO of Omnivest financial. His professional experience also includes working as the owner of an automotive, residential, and commercial glass company. Rasner has also been a realtor.

Reid Rasner is a Wyoming businessman, wealth manager, and conservative leader dedicated to strengthening the Cowboy State. His work ethic began at age 13 when he swept floors at Wyoming Glass, saving his earnings to buy his first bike and learning the value of hard work, responsibility, and financial independence. By 16, he was investing in the stock market, laying the foundation for a lifelong commitment to entrepreneurship and economic growth. A graduate of Natrona County High School and the University of Wyoming, Reid has spent his career helping veterans, ranchers, first responders, teachers, and families achieve financial security. …
