Kenneth R. Casner

Kenneth R. Casner (Democratic, Elk Mountain): Small-business owner (Elk Mountain Trading Company and Crossing Cafe, Elk Mountain); Vietnam veteran; Wyoming National Guard, 25 years. Unopposed in the Aug. 18, 2026 Democratic primary for Governor.
