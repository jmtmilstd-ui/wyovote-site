Quin Blair

Quin Blair (Republican, Cody): Hotelier; Chief Marketing Officer of Blair Hotels (family business). Won the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 24 with 66.0% of the vote.

Background

I am a fifth-generation Park County resident, a third-generation hotelier, and a father to two young children — my son, Quin Jude, and my daughter, Brittania Adalyn “Ruthie” Blair. I was born and raised in Cody, and other than my time in Las Vegas attending UNLV’s William F. Harrah College of Hospitality, Wyoming has always been home. At UNLV, I graduated with a 3.96 GPA, completed Honors studies, and earned a Marketing minor. I serve as Chief Marketing Officer of Blair Hotels, a family business operating five Wyoming hotels, including three in Cody. …

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

The greatest challenge facing Wyoming is protecting our way of life while preparing responsibly for the future. That challenge is made harder by outside influences that do not understand Wyoming, our values, or the people who built this state. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

The Legislature should focus on practical solutions that lower costs, keep care close to home, and strengthen the providers we already have. In Wyoming, access is often just as important as affordability. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

No. I do not support selling or transferring public land in Wyoming. Public land belongs to the public, and it should remain available for the benefit of the people; not sold off, locked up, or handed over to special interests. Pride in the land is a core Wyoming value. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

Property tax relief is important, but Wyoming should not pretend every county has the same economy or the same options. The answer is not a blanket approach from Cheyenne. It is giving counties room to use the strengths they already have. In Park County, that strength is tourism. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Wyoming Legislature should play an important role in economic development by creating the conditions for businesses, workers, and communities to thrive. Government should not pick winners and losers, hand out unfair advantages, or subsidize business models that cannot stand on their own. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

Wyoming campaigns should be driven by Wyoming people, Wyoming communities, and Wyoming interests. I am concerned when outside money flows into local races, we risk outside agendas that do not reflect our values or our way of life. …
