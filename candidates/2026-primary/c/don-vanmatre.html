Don VanMatre

Don VanMatre (Constitution, Sinclair). Lost the Nov. 3, 2026 general election for State Senator S11 with 6.0% of the vote.

Senate District 11 · Constitution

General result: State Senate SD11, Constitution, 297 votes (6%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceSinclair, Carbon County
Background

Sinclair resident (mailing city on the candidate filing) running as the Constitution Party nominee for Senate District 11.

Listed as the Constitution Party speaker for Senate District 11 at a Carbon County Higher Education Center general-election forum scheduled for Oct. 28, 2026. He was not among the Senate District 11 speakers at the Rock Springs Chamber of Commerce political forum on Aug. 12, 2026, which featured Bob Davis and Jim Wilson. A Rocket Miner election preview described him only as a local community member and Constitution Party candidate; it did not include a personal biography. No occupation, education, or candidate questionnaire was found. (A 2019 obituary for Don Van Matre Jr., a former Green River mayor and Sweetwater County commissioner, is a different person.)
