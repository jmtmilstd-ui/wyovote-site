Rebecca Bextel

Rebecca Bextel (Constitution, Jackson): Small-business owner; has operated small businesses in Wyoming with her husband, Jonathan. Lost the Nov. 3, 2026 general election for Governor with 16.8% of the vote.

Governor · Constitution

General result: Governor, Constitution, 31,444 votes (16.8%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceJackson, Teton County
OccupationSmall-business owner; has operated small businesses in Wyoming with her husband, Jonathan
Background

Has lived in Wyoming more than 20 years. She and her husband, Jonathan, raised four daughters and operated small businesses. Campaign biography says she entered politics after watching outside consultants and insiders take advantage of Wyoming, filed public-records requests, and later worked with Eyes on Wyoming on property rights and taxpayer issues (as described by the Constitution Party of Wyoming). Mailing city on the candidate filing: Jackson.

Prior officesRepublican precinct committeewoman, state committeewoman, and alternate delegate to the Republican National Convention (2020 and 2024) (self-reported)
Websitehttps://REBECCAFORWYOMING.COM (leaves WyoVote)
Issue positions

Governing principles

Running to defend limited government, responsible spending, energy independence, the Second Amendment, property rights, the sanctity of life, parental rights, and Wyoming’s Western way of life; would oppose federal overreach and unnecessary interference with families and small businesses.

Party and accountability

Presents herself as a constitutional conservative alternative for voters who believe a Republican label alone is no longer enough; campaign site says having an “R” by a name does not mean a candidate stands for Republican values.
