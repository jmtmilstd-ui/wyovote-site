Susan Bigelow

Susan Bigelow (Republican, Sheridan): Business consultant. Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 51 with 48.0% of the vote.

Background

Susan Bigelow graduated from Spearfish High School. She earned a bachelor's degree from California Polytechnic State University in 1986, a graduate degree from the University of South Dakota in 1993, and a Ph.D. from Colorado State University in 2011. Her career experience includes working as a business consultant.

Following a career as an defense industry engineer and manager, Susan returned to her hometown of Spearfish, SD, where she started working in economic development. In 1996, she moved to Gillette to work for five northeast Wyoming counties. In 2006, following a successful grant application to expand Gillette College's technical training programs, she was hired by Sheridan College where she served as the Vice President for External Relations and Economic Development. Susan represented the Sheridan Economic and Education Development Authority (SEEDA) for the Weatherby relocation to Sheridan. …
