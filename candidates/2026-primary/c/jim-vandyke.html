Jim VanDyke

Jim VanDyke (Democratic, Cheyenne): Technology, computing facilities and construction management. Unopposed in the Aug. 18, 2026 Democratic primary for State House District 42.

House District 42 · Democratic

Primary result: Unopposed

General result: State House HD42, Democratic, 695 votes (22.6%), Lost.

ResidenceCheyenne, Laramie County
OccupationTechnology, computing facilities and construction management
Background

My career has been in technology, computing facilities, construction and management. I started at 12 when I designed and built my first computer. I received my degree in computer science and began in a computing center. My "big picture" skills were recognized, and I proposed, designed and constructed larger and larger projects. I chaired a computing steering committee at a 1500+ employee company with multiple divisions and subsidiaries. I led teams to propose, design, construct and manage million-dollar technology projects with a focus in networking, computing and data centers. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Like other states, Wyoming can initiate programs which help its citizens with healthcare without major increases in taxes. These programs require the different government segments to work together to develop a functioning and sustaining program. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

No. Public lands are a promise to the people by the government to maintain this proud country. Wyoming has some of the most beautiful areas and wildlife in the country which is why many citizens have moved or stay in this state. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

This is one of my top issues. it is irresponsible of the government and people to cut property taxes without understanding the impacts to public services we enjoy. Wyoming's property taxes are well below many states in comparison which already strains the local service systems. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The government is the stewards of the public and the public pays for the government. It's the government's responsibility to ensure the state stays viable by bringing in new businesses and diversifying the economy. …
