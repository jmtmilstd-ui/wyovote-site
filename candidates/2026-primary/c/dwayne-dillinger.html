Dwayne Dillinger

Dwayne Dillinger (Republican, Gillette): Campbell County Parks and Recreation (career). Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State Senate District 23 with 44.7% of the vote.

Senate District 23 · Republican

Primary result: Lost

Research

ResidenceGillette, Campbell County
OccupationCampbell County Parks and Recreation (career)
Background

Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for Senate District 23 (Gillette area) to Abby Angelos, 1,657 (44.7%) to 2,054 (55.3%). County 17 published a candidate Q&A (Aug. 5, 2026), not read in full here.

Fourth-generation Campbell County resident; says he led a water-conservation project at Bell Nob Golf Course that saved taxpayers over $1 million.

Websitedillingerwyoming.com
Issue positions

Affordability and government

Affordability is the top issue; make tax dollars go further; streamline permitting and reduce regulations rather than government building housing or creating jobs.

Industry

Protect agriculture, coal, oil and gas; support industrial siting legislation for mines.

Schools

Fix the school funding model so local boards have flexibility; "the best government is local."
