Background

Andrew Byron was born in Jackson, Wyoming, and lives in Teton County. Byron graduated from Jackson Hole High School. He earned a bachelor's degree from the University of Wyoming in 2008. Byron's career experience includes working as a real estate agent. Byron also volunteered as a lieutenant for Jackson Hole Fire/EMS.
