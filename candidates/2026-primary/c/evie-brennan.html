Background

Evie Brennan was born in Cheyenne, Wyoming. Brennan graduated from East High School in 2000. She earned a B.S. in nursing from the University of Wyoming in 2004. Her career experience includes working as a registered nurse.

Senator Evie Brennan is a consistent conservative who is working to preserve Wyoming for her children and their generation like her grandparents preserved Wyoming for her. Evie is a third generation Wyoming native whose great-grandfather homesteaded in Wyoming. What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Some of the biggest challenges facing Wyoming today are jobs and our economy. Because of the state’s boom‑and‑bust cycle, we must get serious about diversifying our economy. We have outstanding natural resources and should continue to develop them responsibly while protecting our landscapes and communities. …

Healthcare is a complex system that can be difficult for patients and families to understand and navigate. Much of this challenge stems from the intricate relationships among providers, insurers, pharmaceutical companies, and government programs. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

Wyoming's public lands are not for sale. They belong to the people of Wyoming and are a vital part of our heritage, economy, and way of life. These lands support outdoor recreation, tourism, agriculture, energy development, and local communities across the state. …

Over the last several years, the Wyoming Legislature has worked thoughtfully to address rapidly rising property taxes while recognizing the need for counties and local governments to maintain essential infrastructure and public safety services. …
