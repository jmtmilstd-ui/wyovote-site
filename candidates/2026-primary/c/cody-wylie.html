Background

Cody Wylie was born in Rock Springs, Wyoming. He earned a bachelor's degree from the University of Wyoming in 2011. His career experience includes working as a construction manager. As of 2022, Wylie served on the Rock Springs planning and zoning board.

Issue positions

What are the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today? As a legislator, how would you address them?

Wyoming’s biggest challenge is creating opportunities for future generations while protecting the industries and values that define us. Energy, mining, agriculture, and construction remain vital, and we must support them while responsibly diversifying our economy. We can grow without losing what makes Wyoming special. …

We asked WyoFile readers to rank issues that are important to them, and healthcare costs and access topped the list. What can the Legislature do to make healthcare more affordable and accessible to Wyomingites?

Healthcare affordability and access are challenges for families across Wyoming, especially in rural communities. We must ensure our rural hospitals, clinics and providers remain viable so Wyomingites can access care close to home. …

Do you support efforts to sell or transfer public land in Wyoming? How do you plan to ensure that state and federal land is managed in the best interest of the people of Wyoming?

For the most part, I believe in keeping public lands in public hands. Growing up in southwest Wyoming has taught me there can be exceptions, but they should be limited, practical, and locally driven. When communities need room to grow, there should be a reasonable process for acquiring small parcels of public land. …

No two Wyoming counties are the same when it comes to how they generate revenue. As a legislator, how would you approach the public's desire for property tax relief with local revenue needs?

Wyoming's counties are not all the same, and a one size fits all approach to property tax relief can create unintended consequences. …

What role, if any, should the Wyoming Legislature play in economic development?

The Wyoming Legislature has an important role in economic development, but government should be a partner, not the driving force. Our job is to maintain a tax and regulatory environment that encourages investment, job creation, and long-term growth. …

Explain your values when it comes to campaign donations. Does Wyoming's system for monitoring campaign donations and expenditures need reform?

Transparency in campaign donations is essential. Voters should know where candidates’ funding comes from to make informed decisions. Wyoming’s system offers some transparency, but it can be improved. The Legislature should continue reviewing it thoughtfully. …
