Profile · 2026 Primary

Deb Mutter Shamley (Republican, Cheyenne): Retired military flight nurse; former mine labor-relations manager (Green River); board member, Wyoming Public Charter Schools Association; Wyoming Air and Army National Guard, nearly 36 years (retired 2018). Lost the Aug. 18, 2026 Republican primary for State House District 7 with 34.4% of the vote.
