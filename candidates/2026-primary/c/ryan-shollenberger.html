Ryan Shollenberger

Ryan Shollenberger (Libertarian, Cheyenne): K-12 substitute teacher in Laramie County School District 1; wildland firefighter with Albany County Fire; former TV meteorologist on Wyoming News Now; U.S. Air Force veteran (2009–2021), with deployments to Afghanistan and Colombia; current field artillery officer in the Wyoming Army National Guard (self-described on campaign site). Lost the Nov. 3, 2026 general election for State Representative H11 with 6.7% of the vote.

House District 11 · Libertarian

General result: State House HD11, Libertarian, 152 votes (6.7%), Lost.
