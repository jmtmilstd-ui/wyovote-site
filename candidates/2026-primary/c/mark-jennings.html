Background

Former legislator. Clifford "Mark" Jennings (R, Sheridan County) served in the Wyoming House for House District 30 from 2015 through 2024 (wyoleg.gov legislator profile; born in Larned, Kansas). He won the seat by defeating incumbent Kathy Coleman in the 2014 Republican primary and was re-elected in 2016, 2018 (96.3%), 2020 (95.4%, 3,985 votes) and 2022 (95.4%, 2,912 votes). In 2024 he ran for Senate District 22 and lost the Republican primary; he left the House on Jan. 6, 2025, succeeded by Thomas Kelly. In 2026 he sought to return to House District 30 and lost the Aug. 18 Republican primary to Ryan Koltiska, 972 to 1,632.

My name is Mark Jennings, and I am running again as your State Representative. For ten years I fought in the House of Representatives to rein in government spending. I fought for and got passed parental and grandparents rights bills. I have also worked to pass pro-life and election integrity bills. I believe that Wyoming is the greatest state and we are so blessed to be here. But keeping Wyoming free takes diligence and hard work. I am excited to be running again for this seat and will continue to be a voice for the people of my district.
