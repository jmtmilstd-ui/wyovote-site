Background

Gwynn G. Bartlett is a Carbon County commissioner. She won the November 2024 general election, and the official commissioners page lists her as a member. The Rawlins Times reported on December 18, 2024, that she submitted her resignation as county clerk at the December 17 commission meeting, that she would leave the clerk's office on January 5, 2025, and take the commissioner seat on January 7, and that she had served four and a half terms as clerk and worked in the clerk's office for 27 years. The same report said the commission appointed chief deputy Lisa Smith as interim clerk effective January 6, 2025.

Prior officesCarbon County clerk (four and a half terms, and 27 years in the clerk's office, as reported by the Rawlins Times; resigned effective January 5, 2025); Carbon County commissioner (elected 2024)
