Background

Scott Mangold is a Park County commissioner. The official commissioners page lists him on the board. The Powell Tribune reported in March 2024 that he lives in Powell, is a former mayor of Powell, and is a co-owner, host, and announcer of the KPOW radio station in Powell, and that he was running for a second term after Commissioner Lee Livingston chose not to run again. He won election in the November 2024 general election.

Prior officesPark County commissioner (the Powell Tribune reported in March 2024 that he was seeking a second and, he said, final term). Former mayor of Powell.
