Background

Ty Zwonitzer is a Laramie County commissioner. His official county biography describes him as a fifth-generation Wyomingite raised on the east side of Cheyenne who grew up helping a family business, Western Auction and Z's Home Furnishings, attended school at Baggs, Carey, and Cheyenne East High, and earned a degree in business management from the University of Wyoming. The biography states that he learned auctioneering from his father, Dave, and grandfather, Mel, graduated from the Western College of Auctioneering, became a realtor in 2008, and after the sale of the family companies in 2012 worked as a land management specialist with the Wyoming Department of Transportation. It also states that he later joined Cheyenne Fire Rescue and has served as a lieutenant of suppression and as a cadre instructor for new trainees, and that he and his wife, Shawna, a Laramie County School District 1 elementary teacher, live in the area. He won election as commissioner in the November 5, 2024, general election.
