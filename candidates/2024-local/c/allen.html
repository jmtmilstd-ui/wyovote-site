Background

Larry Allen is chairman of the Fremont County Board of Commissioners, representing District 2. The official commissioners page and elected officials directory list him as chairman. He won the November 2024 general election for the District 2 seat. The clerk's office-holders list, drawn from the 2024 election, gives his term as ending in January 2029.

Prior officesFremont County commissioner, District 2 (won the November 2024 general election; the clerk's office-holders list gives a term ending in January 2029)
