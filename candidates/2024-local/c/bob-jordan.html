Background

Bob Jordan is a Campbell County commissioner. County 17 reported that he was selected in January 2024 to fill a vacated seat and was elected to a four-year term in November 2024. In a County 17 primary questionnaire he wrote that he had spent 30 years in corporate work for an electric utility, worked in operations and maintenance management and industrial and home-building construction, was a small-business owner in agriculture and oilfield work, retired early to start a nonprofit agriculture consulting company, and served in the U.S. Army in an engineering unit in the early 1980s, and that he had previously served on the Conservation District Board and the Campbell County Planning Board.
