Background

Kelly Simone is a Park County commissioner, elected in the November 2024 general election. The official commissioners page lists her on the board. In a September 21, 2022, Cody Enterprise article about her campaign for the West Park Hospital District board, the paper reported that she had served as a physician assistant at Cody Regional Health for 18 years, had recently been hired at Billings Clinic, had 21 years of experience as a physician assistant including work in Driggs, Idaho, and Greybull, and that she had previously served on a school board.

Prior officesPark County commissioner (elected November 2024). The 2022 Cody Enterprise article reported prior service on a school board.
