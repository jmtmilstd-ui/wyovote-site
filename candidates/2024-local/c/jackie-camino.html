Background

Jackie Camino is the Johnson County clerk, serving through the end of the current term in January 2027. Sheridan Media reported on December 6, 2022, that commissioners appointed her over Darcy Goni and Jody Telkamp after Kate Harriet withdrew, that she received two of three commission votes, and that she would be sworn in in January and serve a two-year term. She won the 2024 election for the office. Incoming clerk Becky Rodriguez takes office in January 2027.

Prior officesJohnson County clerk (appointed Dec. 6, 2022, after Kate Harriet withdrew; sworn in January 2023 for a two-year term, as reported by Sheridan Media; won the 2024 election)
