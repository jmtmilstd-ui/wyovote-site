Background

Don Hollingshead is a Laramie County commissioner. His official county biography describes him as a third-generation Laramie County resident and states that he served 27 years at the Laramie County Sheriff's Office, including rural patrol, and retired as a captain. The biography also states that he has been a Kiwanis member for nine years, served as a board member of Safe Harbor Children's Advocacy Center for nine years, much of that time as board chair, and devoted 25 years to Special Olympics of Wyoming. The Wyoming Tribune Eagle reported in April 2023 that he was one of the Laramie County Republican Party's nominees for the county assessor vacancy later filled by appointment of Todd Ernst. He won election as commissioner in the November 5, 2024, general election.
