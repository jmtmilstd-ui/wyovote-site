Background

Marty Ertman is a Weston County commissioner. The official commissioners page photograph lists her on the board. The News Letter Journal reported on January 13, 2021, that on January 5 she became the first female chairman of the Weston County Board of Commissioners and was then in her 11th year on the board, and that County Clerk Becky Hadlock said she was only the second female commissioner in county history. In an October 31, 2018, News Letter Journal questionnaire she wrote that she had owned and operated a small retail store in Newcastle for 30 years and had been a local sports referee for 15 years. She won the November 2024 general election. This Profile fills only her 2024 local page.

Prior officesWeston County commissioner (the News Letter Journal reported she was first elected about eight years before a 2018 candidate questionnaire and became the board's first female chairman on January 5, 2021; she won the November 2024 general election)
