Dan Dockstader

Dan Dockstader (Republican, Afton): Radio host and publisher; Wyoming House District 16, 2007–2008; Wyoming Senate District 16, 2009–Present. Unopposed in the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State Senate District 16. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · Afton, Lincoln, Teton Counties County

Primary result: Unopposed, 3,168 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: Unopposed, 8,830 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

Background

Dockstader earned his B.A. from Brigham Young University. Dockstader has worked as both a radio host and a publisher.

Nearly four decades of working with and reporting on all levels of government. Attended thousands of meetings related to government.

EducationBachelor's: Brigham Young University
Prior officesWyoming House District 16, 2007–2008; Wyoming Senate District 16, 2009–Present
Positions

How, within the framework of a lawmaker's powers and duties, would you address the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today?

Maintaining a strong, energy based economy to sustain Wyoming individuals and families.

As a state lawmaker, what will guide your approach to local control? How do you determine when a decision should be left to local leaders or made by the Wyoming Legislature?

Speaking directly with the local leaders, as I have done for decades in public office and my occupation.

Wyoming has wrestled for years with issues of housing, health care, education and economic development. What do you believe the state's role should be in supporting the well-being of Wyoming citizens and their families?

Establishing jobs that create self-reliant individuals and families.

How willing are you to compromise with legislators and other officials with different perspectives?

The needs of my home district will come first.

How do you balance the often competing demands of wildlife and open spaces versus energy, industrial and real estate development?

As a state we have been finding that balance for years and will continue to do so.
