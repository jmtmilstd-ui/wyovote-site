Terry Lee Weickum

Profile · 2024 Primary

Terry Lee Weickum (Republican, Rawlins): Mayor of Rawlins and former Carbon County commissioner (self-described on his 2024 iVoterGuide biography: four years as Rawlins mayor and eight years as Carbon County commissioner); Mayor of Rawlins, Wyoming; Carbon County, Wyoming, Commissioner; Wyoming Army National Guard, E-5, 7½ years (self-reported on his 2024 iVoterGuide biography). Lost the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 15 with 19.1% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost, 223 votes (19.1%), 2nd of 3.

Positions

How, within the framework of a lawmaker's powers and duties, would you address the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today?

Our changing energy sources will require a lot of work to fine-tune the taxation of them. With the introduction of illegal drugs into our nation, social issues will be a challenge Infrastructure in most cities and towns are in terrible condition and those issues must be addressed as soon as possible

As a state lawmaker, what will guide your approach to local control? How do you determine when a decision should be left to local leaders or made by the Wyoming Legislature?

As much as possible, it should be left to local leadership as there are several areas in wyoming that differ from other areas.

Wyoming has wrestled for years with issues of housing, health care, education and economic development. What do you believe the state's role should be in supporting the well-being of Wyoming citizens and their families?

Concentrate on health, safety and welfare issues.

How willing are you to compromise with legislators and other officials with different perspectives?

Absolutely, but I will not compromise my core values.

How do you balance the often competing demands of wildlife and open spaces versus energy, industrial and real estate development?

I have always taken a fair and balanced approach.
