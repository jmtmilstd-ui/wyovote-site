Tina Clifford

Profile · 2024 Primary

Tina Clifford (Republican, Lander): Former bookkeeper (described as such in an August 2024 Cowboy State Daily column by Bill Sniffin, who wrote that she had been his bookkeeper and later left the job to further her education because she wanted to become a high school business teacher). Lost the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 54 with 45.7% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost, 1,208 votes (45.7%), 2nd of 2.

Background

Tina Clifford of Lander ran in the 2024 Republican primary for House District 54 and lost. She did not submit a biographical answer to the 2024 WyoFile Election Guide questionnaire.

In an Aug. 10, 2024 column, Lander publisher Bill Sniffin described her as his former bookkeeper and a “good friend,” writing that he admired her when she quit so she could increase her education because she wanted to become a high school business teacher. He also wrote that she was sponsored by the Freedom Caucus in her 2024 challenge to Rep. Lloyd Larsen. That column does not independently confirm that she later became a classroom teacher, so that title is not used here as an occupation.
