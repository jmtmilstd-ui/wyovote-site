Richard Brubaker

Profile · 2024 Primary

Richard Brubaker (Libertarian, Riverton): Truck driver. Lost the Nov. 5, 2024 general election for U.S. House with 9,223 votes (3.5%).

General result: U.S. House, Libertarian, 9,223 votes (3.5%), Lost.

Research

ResidenceRiverton, Fremont County (2024 SOS general candidate list)
OccupationTruck driver; formerly a dairy farmer (Wyoming Tribune-Eagle, 2012; Thermopolis Independent Record, 2024)
Background

Richard Brubaker of Riverton was the Libertarian nominee for Wyoming’s at-large U.S. House seat in 2024 and lost the Nov. 5 general election with 9,223 votes (3.5%). The official 2024 general candidate list gives a Riverton mailing address.

The Thermopolis Independent Record, in an Oct. 31, 2024 federal-candidate summary, said he lives in Riverton, attended Central Wyoming College and the University of Wyoming, is a truck driver, and had also been a U.S. House candidate in 2014, 2018, 2020, and 2022. Ballotpedia likewise describes him as a truck driver and records earlier Libertarian U.S. House runs, including 2012.

A Sept. 21, 2012 Wyoming Tribune-Eagle profile said the then-59-year-old Riverton resident had been a dairy farmer before becoming a truck driver in 1998, had graduated from Wind River High School and had some college at Central Wyoming College, and had previously run unsuccessfully for House District 34 three times.
