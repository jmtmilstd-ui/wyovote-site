Laura Mckee

Profile · 2024 Primary

Laura Mckee (Republican, Rock Springs). Lost the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 39 with 43.7% of the vote.

Primary result: Lost, 457 votes (43.7%), 2nd of 3.

OccupationWork in office management, computer science, and mortgage origination, as she described it in her 2024 WyoFile questionnaire
Background

In her 2024 WyoFile questionnaire, Laura McKee wrote that she lived in Rock Springs, had lived there 39 years, was born in West Valley City, Utah, and would be 58 on Election Day 2024. She wrote that her background was jobs in office management, computer science, and mortgage origination, and that she has a degree in accounting and computer science. She did not name the institution that awarded the degree.

EducationA degree in accounting and computer science (institution not named in the 2024 WyoFile questionnaire)

Issue positions

How, within the framework of a lawmaker's powers and duties, would you address the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today?

I would listen to the people and work with members of the legislature to find the best solutions to challenges we face in Wyoming. Like keeping coal and gas production going. I would work with the Legislatures to help grow and diversify Wyomings economy and find ways to create jobs. …

As a state lawmaker, what will guide your approach to local control? How do you determine when a decision should be left to local leaders or made by the Wyoming Legislature?

We do need local control but it needs to be in the best interest of the people and what the majority of people in our area want not the minority. It should not be coming from boards in other parts of Wyoming.

Wyoming has wrestled for years with issues of housing, health care, education and economic development. What do you believe the state's role should be in supporting the well-being of Wyoming citizens and their families?

Housing is a hard issues because of lack of land and costs. The Blm manages alot of the public land and interest rates are high as well. We need to figure out how to build homes that are affordable. Even our lot rent for a trailer is over $500/month. …

How willing are you to compromise with legislators and other officials with different perspectives?

I will definately work with all Legislators: however, I will be the voice of the majority of people in Sweetwater County because they are the ones who will have elected me. I will only have the power they have granted me to be their Representative.

How do you balance the often competing demands of wildlife and open spaces versus energy, industrial and real estate development?

Our wildlife is extremely important to us in Wyoming along with recreation. We need to preserve our way of life in Wyoming. We need our energy sources of coal, oil and natural gas to be prolonged and protected. All situation needs to be studied and weighed for real estate development.
