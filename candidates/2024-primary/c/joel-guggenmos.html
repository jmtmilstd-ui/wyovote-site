Joel Guggenmos

Joel Guggenmos (Republican, Riverton): Residential construction; Wyoming House District 55, 2025–Present. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 55 with 55.6% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.
