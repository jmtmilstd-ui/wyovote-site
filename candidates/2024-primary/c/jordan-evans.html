Jordan Evans

Jordan Evans (Democratic, Cheyenne): Government. Unopposed in the Aug. 20, 2024 Democratic primary for State House District 7. Lost the Nov. 5, 2024 general election with 34.3% of the vote.

Democratic · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Unopposed, 304 votes (100%), 1st of 1.

General result: State House HD07, Democratic, 1,456 votes (34.3%), Lost.

Background

Jordan Evans was born in Cheyenne, Wyoming. He earned a bachelor's degree from the University of Wyoming in 2011. His career experience includes working as a government mapmaker. He has been affiliated with Laramie County Democratic Party and Miles of Smiles Wyoming.

I have a profound affection for Wyoming and its residents, and I am eager to play a significant role in shaping our state's future and tackling the issues we confront. Throughout my life in Wyoming, I have often felt that our state legislators have not fully represented my views or the views of many of my fellow residents. I've observed a worrying shift among lawmakers who increasingly prioritize national political debates over addressing the real and immediate problems our state faces. Additionally, it's disheartening to see many of my peers leave Wyoming, taking their skills and potential contributions with them, because they don't see opportunities for a fulfilling future here. …
