Kathy Russell

Kathy Russell (Republican, Cheyenne): Executive director of the Wyoming Republican Party (since 2018); previously a project manager and senior regulatory analyst in energy production and transmission, focused on oil and gas in Wyoming, for 18 years; Douglas, Wyoming, City Council. Lost the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 7 with 39.0% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 682 votes (39%), 2nd of 2.

Background

Paid executive director of the Wyoming Republican Party since 2018, involved in the state GOP since around 2008. Previously served nearly two years on the Douglas City Council around 2008 and worked 18 years as a project manager and senior regulatory analyst in energy production and transmission, focused on oil and gas in Wyoming. She said she had no plans to step down from the party job if elected.

Prior officesDouglas, Wyoming, City Council
Positions

Family, taxes and party platform

In her campaign announcement she said she would “fight for conservatives” and cited “protecting children, taxpayers and jobs.” She said she aligns with the Republican Party platform and wanted more focus on family and parental rights in law. “When you have a family that’s functioning and happy and healthy, we need to be protecting them,” she said.

Legislative testimony and process

Cowboy State Daily reported that in 2023 she testified there should be no vaccine mandates of any kind, and that in 2019 she spoke against a bill that would have barred employment discrimination based on sexual orientation or gender identity. She said she would bring a more structured, predictable approach to lawmaking and would “look for answers, do their homework.”
