Dan Zwonitzer

Dan Zwonitzer (Republican, Cheyenne): Wyoming House District 43, 2005–2024; Wyoming House of Representatives District 43, 2005–2025. Lost the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 43 with 41.1% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 560 votes (41.1%), 2nd of 2.

Background

Email [contact removed] to notify us of updates to this biography. Zwonitzer earned his B.A. in government and classical history from Georgetown University in 2002. Zwonitzer was a policy analyst for the Wyoming Governor's Office in 2003. He also worked as an auctioneer for the Western Auction Company from 1997 to 2004. His career experience also includes working as a program coordinator for the Wyoming Department of Agriculture.
