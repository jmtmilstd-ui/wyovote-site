Lee Filer (Republican, Cheyenne): Railroad Conductor; Wyoming House District 12, 2013–2014; Wyoming House District 44, 2025–Present; U.S. Air National Guard. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 44 with 54.6% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Won, 314 votes (54.6%), 1st of 3.

General result: State House HD44, Republican, 2,265 votes (100%), Unopposed.

Background

Lee Filer was born in Cheyenne, Wyoming. He served in the U.S. Air National Guard. His career experience includes working as a railroad conductor and business owner.

Positions

How, within the framework of a lawmaker's powers and duties, would you address the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today?

First and foremost, I would actually work with all legislators and my voters to insure we write great legislation instead of allowing a certain group to tell me how to vote.

As a state lawmaker, what will guide your approach to local control? How do you determine when a decision should be left to local leaders or made by the Wyoming Legislature?

Local control is important, we should make as many decisions as possible at the local level. Also local level all the way up should be working together for the people.

Wyoming has wrestled for years with issues of housing, health care, education and economic development. What do you believe the state's role should be in supporting the well-being of Wyoming citizens and their families?

Again working with all levels of government to insure we allow families to make the best choice for themselves. Many times government stands in their way.

How willing are you to compromise with legislators and other officials with different perspectives?

Always willing to work for the betterment of the people.

How do you balance the often competing demands of wildlife and open spaces versus energy, industrial and real estate development?

This is a subject that truly needs all levels working together to make sure we have growth and prosperity but also maintaining our wild culture.
