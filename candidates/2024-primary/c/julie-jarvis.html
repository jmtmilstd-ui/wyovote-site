Julie Jarvis

Julie Jarvis (Republican, Casper): Director; Wyoming House District 57, 2025–Present. Won the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 57 with 56.2% of the vote. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · Casper, Natrona County

Primary result: Won, 623 votes (56.2%), 1st of 2.

General result: State House HD57, Republican, 2,405 votes (100%), Unopposed.

Background

Julie Jarvis earned a bachelor's degree from the University of Bridgeport in 2000, a graduate degree from the University of Bridgeport in 2001, and a Ph.D. from the University of Connecticut in 2018. Her career experience includes working as a director. Jarvis has been affiliated with The Reading League Wyoming and the American Legion Auxiliary.

As a fourth-generation Basque Wyomingite, my roots are deeply tied to the Wyoming way of life. I grew up on a small farm outside of Buffalo with conservative values, emphasizing faith, family, and a strong work ethic. My upbringing revolved around sports, hunting, and hard work. I graduated from Buffalo High School and then received an athletic scholarship to a DII school in Connecticut. The summer after completing my masters, I was married and took my first teaching job out there. Then, expecting my first child a year and a half later, I quickly realized there was no place better than Wyoming to raise children, so I immediately returned home. …

Positions

How, within the framework of a lawmaker's powers and duties, would you address the biggest challenges and opportunities facing Wyoming today?

By listening to my local community and working collaboratively with state leaders, we can collectively address challenges such as job creation, environmental stewardship, and healthcare access.

As a state lawmaker, what will guide your approach to local control? How do you determine when a decision should be left to local leaders or made by the Wyoming Legislature?

My approach to local control is not just a belief but a conviction. Decisions that affect local communities must be in the hands of the people who live there. My political platform reflects this conviction. …

Wyoming has wrestled for years with issues of housing, health care, education and economic development. What do you believe the state's role should be in supporting the well-being of Wyoming citizens and their families?

I believe we can improve the well-being of Wyoming residents by cutting bureaucratic barriers and empowering local communities.

How willing are you to compromise with legislators and other officials with different perspectives?

I firmly believe in conservative principles and the value of considering different perspectives to develop innovative solutions for our present challenges. However, my main objective is to consistently serve the best interests of the constituents in House District 57.

How do you balance the often competing demands of wildlife and open spaces versus energy, industrial and real estate development?

I am committed to working closely with local stakeholders to ensure that development projects benefit our communities and preserve the open spaces and wildlife that make Wyoming unique.
