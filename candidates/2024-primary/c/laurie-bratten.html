Laurie Bratten

Laurie Bratten (Republican, Sheridan): Agricultural business owner; Wyoming House District 51, 2025–Present. Unopposed in the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 51. Unopposed in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

Republican · Sheridan, Sheridan County

Background

Bratten worked in Colorado politics from 2008 to 2016. Before working in politics, Bratten was a high school science teacher and a pharmaceutical salesperson. In addition to her work in legislative offices, she was "active with various Republican and Liberty Groups throughout the state, speaking and presenting on topics such as the legislative process, effective advocacy at the state capitol, the use of social media in politics, and legislative analysis." From 2010 to 2014, Bratten worked as a legislative aide in the Colorado State Legislature, first working for former state Sen. Ted Harvey (R) and then with state Sen. Kent Lambert (R). …
