Carmen Whitehead

Profile · 2024 Primary

Carmen Whitehead (Democratic, Green River). Reached the 2024 general-election ballot after a Democratic primary write-in campaign. Lost the Nov. 5, 2024 general election for State House HD60 with 951 votes (27.7%).

General result: State House HD60, Democratic, 951 votes (27.7%), Lost.

Background

Carmen Whitehead is a Democrat from Green River who ran for House District 60 in 2024. The Rocket Miner reported on Nov. 2, 2024, that she reached the general-election ballot through a write-in campaign. Ballotpedia’s election results also list her as a write-in winner of the Aug. 20, 2024 Democratic primary. She lost the Nov. 5, 2024 general election.

The same Rocket Miner profile, drawing on a press release from Whitehead, said she is the daughter of an iron-ore miner and a stay-at-home mother, and that her father helped organize a union at the CF&I mine in Sunrise, Wyoming. Locally, the article said, she has worked in the Third Judicial District Court and later transferred to the Sweetwater County Engineering, Planning and Zoning department. It also reported that she was a delegate twice at State Democratic conventions and chaired the Supervisory Committee for the Green River Basin Federal Credit Union for numerous years.
