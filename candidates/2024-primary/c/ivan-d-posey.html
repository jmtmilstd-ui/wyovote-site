Background

Ivan Posey served in the United States Army. His career experience includes working for the USDA Forest Service in the Shoshone National Forest and as culture director for Arapahoe School in the Fremont County School District #38. Posey was appointed by Gov. Dave Freudenthal (D) to serve as the first Tribal Liaison for the Shoshone and Arapaho Tribes of the Wind River Indian Reservation from 2003 to 2004. He is an enrolled member of the Eastern Shoshone tribe and is also Northern Cheyenne and Northern Arapaho. Posey has been affiliated with Americans for Indian Opportunity, Advancement for Indigenous Opportunity International, the Wind River Development Fund, and Community Entry Services. …
