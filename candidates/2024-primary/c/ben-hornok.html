Ben Hornok

Ben Hornok (Republican, Cheyenne): General contractor; Wyoming House of Representatives District 42, 2023–2025; Wyoming House District 42, 2023–2024. Lost the Aug. 20, 2024 Republican primary for State House District 42 with 49.1% of the vote.

Republican · Cheyenne, Laramie County

Primary result: Lost, 844 votes (49.1%), 2nd of 2.

Research

OccupationGeneral contractor
Background

Ben Hornok earned a B.A. in Biblical studies from the Frontier School of the Bible in 1997 and an associate degree in construction management from Salt Lake Community College in 2003. His career experience includes working as a general contractor.
